## The Culture Is Real

The culture is real. Nobody's questioning that. Families look out
for each other. You don't air dirty laundry. You don't bring
outsiders into family business. You handle it at the kitchen table.

That's how DaSean was raised. That's how Deanna raised him. You
show up. You provide. You don't ask for credit. You don't run
from hard conversations. When Kevin needed to tell DaSean
something hard, he said it to his face at the kitchen table —
not through a lawyer, not through a filing, not through someone
else's phone.

The culture is also this: **you don't use your own people.** You
don't eat off someone else's plate and claim you cooked. You don't
take someone's name and put it on paperwork they never saw. You
don't use your children as a revenue stream. That's not culture.
That's exploitation wearing culture's clothes.

## Kendrick and Drake — Why It Fits

This isn't about the beef. It's about what the beef revealed.

Kendrick came up from Compton. Section 8. Food stamps. Real
poverty — not the story of poverty, the experience of it. He
made it out by being undeniably good at what he does. Every bar

neighborhoods in Toronto. Private school. Acting career before
rap. He had every advantage. And he still chose to put other

it was easier.

**That's the distinction.** It was never about who's Blacker or
who's more "culture." It's about: when you have options, what
do you choose? Kendrick chose to earn it. Drake chose to take it.
