anymore. Make sure Mom understands this is not a scheduling

trying to take the girls from Shayla. He's trying to keep both
parents in their lives.

### "She said 'see you in court' and then didn't file anything."

we're filing the default motion today.

This is the hard one. Mom wants to help. Mom thinks she's being
a bridge. She needs to hear: "Shayla is using you so she doesn't
have to deal with me or Kevin directly. She knows you love me
and she knows you'll pass messages. That's not helping — it's

- **Be a grandmother.** Love the girls. Be present. That matters
  more than anything.

  to DaSean or Kevin about that. I love you but I'm not the
  go-between."

  argue. Don't relay it. "That's between you and DaSean."
- **If Shayla asks about Kevin:** "Kevin is family. Talk to him
  directly."

Mom can't be neutral here. Shayla filed to remove DaSean as a
parent. Neutral means accepting that. DaSean needs Mom to
understand that supporting him IS supporting the girls.
