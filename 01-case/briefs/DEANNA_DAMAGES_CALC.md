Deanna's childcare. Their home (Dresden) was the economic center.
Deanna's free labor reduced the Smith household's childcare
expenses to zero, freeing Leon's federal salary and Marquita's

## SETTLEMENT STRATEGY

Deanna's case is designed to settle. Here's why:

1. **She needs money now.** Fixed income, Section 8, no savings.
2. **The Smiths need this gone.** Every day this case is open,
   discovery generates evidence for Kevin's case, the children's
   case, the custody case, and the federal/state investigations.
3. **The numbers are defensible.** DOL data, Care.com rates,
   published market research. A jury will see a grandmother in
   Section 8 housing who fed and cared for children for years
   while a banking professional with 9 credit cards used her
   food stamps.

### Settlement targets:

| Scenario | Amount | What it buys the Smiths |
|----------|--------|------------------------|
| **Floor** | $25,000 | Deanna's case goes away; no discovery |
| **Target** | $50,000 | Deanna's case + cooperation on testimony |
| **Trial value** | $137,568+ | Jury verdict, conservative |

### What settlement does NOT buy:
- Kevin's case (separate plaintiff, won't settle)
- The children's case (separate plaintiff)
- The custody case (separate proceeding)
- The OIG investigation (federal, can't be withdrawn)
- The MDHHS investigation (state, can't be called off)

**Settling Deanna's case buys them NOTHING except removing one
front. But it costs them cash they don't want to spend. And the
settlement amount itself becomes evidence of liability in the
other cases — "they paid Deanna $X because they knew they owed
her."**
