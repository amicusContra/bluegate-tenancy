PROPOSED CONSENT ORDER RE: SOLE CUSTODY,
PARENTING TIME, AND ALL RELATED MATTERS

    The parties, having reached agreement, stipulate and consent
to the entry of the following Order:

CUSTODY

1. Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff DaSean Mitchell shall have SOLE
   LEGAL CUSTODY and SOLE PHYSICAL CUSTODY of the minor children:
   - Sahana Saige Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld])
   - Allanna Star Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld])

2. DaSean Mitchell shall have exclusive decision-making
   authority on all matters regarding the children's health,
   education, welfare, residence, and upbringing. No consultation
   with Plaintiff is required.

3. The children's sole residence shall be with Defendant at such
   address as Defendant determines, currently [REDACTED_ADDR_2],
   [REDACTED_CITY].

PARENTING TIME

4. Plaintiff shall have supervised parenting time only, at
   such times, locations, and frequency as Defendant determines
   in his sole discretion.

5. All parenting time shall be supervised by Defendant or a
   supervisor designated by Defendant. Plaintiff shall not be
   alone with the children at any time without Defendant's
   prior written authorization.

6. Plaintiff shall have no unsupervised parenting time, no
   overnight parenting time, and no minimum guaranteed
   parenting time under this Order.

7. Defendant may modify, reduce, suspend, or expand Plaintiff's
   supervised parenting time at any time upon written notice.

8. Plaintiff shall have the right to reasonable telephone
   contact with the children at times designated by Defendant.

EDUCATION

8. All education decisions — including school enrollment,
   withdrawal, transfer, tutoring, and extracurricular
   activities — shall be made by Defendant in his sole
   discretion without consultation.

COMMUNICATION

9. All communication regarding the children shall be directed
   to Defendant or his designated representative(s). Defendant
   determines the communication structure.

10. Plaintiff shall not block Defendant's phone number or
    restrict his ability to contact her.

11. Plaintiff shall not use family members, the children, or
    third parties as communication intermediaries.

MEDICAL AND INSURANCE

12. All medical, dental, mental health, and related decisions
    shall be made by Defendant in his sole discretion.

13. Plaintiff shall maintain the children on her employer-
    provided health insurance and provide Defendant with current
    insurance cards within fourteen (14) days of entry of this
    Order.

VITAL DOCUMENTS

14. Within seven (7) days of entry of this Order, Plaintiff
    shall provide Defendant with originals of: birth
    certificates, Social Security cards, immunization records,
    passports (if any), and health insurance cards for both
    children.

TRAVEL

15. Plaintiff shall not take or attempt to take the children
    outside of Defendant's designated county of residence
    without Defendant's prior written consent.

16. Plaintiff shall not apply for, obtain, possess, or renew
    a passport for either child. Any existing passports for
    the children shall be surrendered to Defendant within
    seven (7) days of entry of this Order.

17. Defendant may take the children outside the State of
    Michigan upon fourteen (14) days' written notice to
    Plaintiff. Plaintiff's consent is not required.

CHILD SUPPORT

18. Child support shall be calculated by the Friend of the Court
    pursuant to the Michigan Child Support Formula, based on both
    parties' verified income.

19. Plaintiff shall file an Amended Verified Statement (FOC 23)
    with complete and accurate income information within seven
    (7) days of entry of this Order.

ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RELIANCE (ESTOPPEL)

20. Plaintiff acknowledges that during the parties' relationship,
    Defendant relied upon Plaintiff's representations of
    commitment (including two commitment ceremonies) and
    Plaintiff's role as primary earner in forgoing independent
    employment and career development in order to serve as
    primary caregiver to the children.

21. Plaintiff acknowledges this reliance was reasonable and
    resulted in economic disadvantage to Defendant.

DEVICE MONITORING AND SOCIAL MEDIA

22. Neither parent shall monitor, record, or intercept the other
    parent's communications through the children's electronic
    devices or any other means.

23. Neither parent shall post, share, or publish on any social
    media platform any information about the other parent, the
    children, or this case.

24. Neither parent shall permit family members or associates to
    make such posts on their behalf.

OTHER PROVISIONS

25. Neither parent shall disparage the other in the presence of
    the children or permit others to do so.

26. Plaintiff shall not file any motion to modify this Order for
    a period of twelve (12) months from the date of entry,
    absent a showing of emergency or danger to the children.

DISCOVERY AND PENDING MOTIONS

27. Upon entry of this Order, all pending discovery shall be
    deemed withdrawn. All pending motions shall be deemed moot.

28. The preservation obligations established by Defendant's
    August 16, 2026 Preservation Requests shall remain in effect
    for twelve (12) months following entry of this Order.

DISMISSAL

29. Upon entry of this Order, Plaintiff's original Complaint
    shall be dismissed with prejudice. Defendant's Counterclaim
    shall be deemed satisfied by this Order.


IT IS SO ORDERED.
