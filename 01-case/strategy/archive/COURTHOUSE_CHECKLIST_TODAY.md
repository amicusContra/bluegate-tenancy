## AT THE CLERK'S WINDOW

1. Tell the clerk: "I'm filing a civil complaint, pro se"
2. Hand them the original complaint + exhibits
3. Pay the filing fee
4. They assign a case number and stamp all copies
5. Get your stamped copy back — this is your proof of filing
6. Ask about service requirements — you'll need to serve
   each defendant
