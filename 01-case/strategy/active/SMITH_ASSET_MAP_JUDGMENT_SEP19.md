### THE LEGAL FRAMEWORK

Under **MRPC 3.3 (Candor Toward the Tribunal)**, a lawyer SHALL NOT:
- Knowingly make a false statement of fact to a tribunal
- Fail to correct a false statement of material fact previously made
- Offer evidence the lawyer knows to be false

Under **MRPC 1.6(c)(3)**, a lawyer MAY reveal confidential information to:
- Rectify a fraud in which the lawyer's services have been used

Under **MRPC 1.16(a)**, a lawyer SHALL withdraw if:
- The representation will result in violation of the Rules of Professional Conduct
