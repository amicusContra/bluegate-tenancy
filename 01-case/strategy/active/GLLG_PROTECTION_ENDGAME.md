## LEVEL 3: FEDERAL — If RICO Complaint Moves to Federal Court

### Current Posture

The RICO complaint (2026-4349-CZ) is in Ingham County. If it moves to federal court (E.D. Michigan) via removal or refiling:

### Shelton Test — Deposing Opposing Counsel

The Shelton test (8th Cir. 1986, adopted E.D. Mich.) limits depositions of opposing counsel to cases where:
1. No other means exist to obtain the information
2. The information is relevant and nonprivileged
3. The information is crucial to case preparation

**Kevin's position:** He does NOT need to depose Crump-Gibson about litigation strategy. He needs her LARA filings and entity formation documents — which are public records he already has. The Shelton test is irrelevant because Kevin isn't seeking attorney work product.

**But MRPC 3.7 still applies in federal court.** Federal courts apply state ethics rules for attorney conduct. The fact witness issue (Purpose Foundation formation, §5.2 representation) survives removal.

### Federal Protective Orders

If GLLG withdraws before any federal proceedings:
- Kevin does NOT seek to pierce attorney-client privilege
- Kevin does NOT seek GLLG's work product
- Kevin's evidence is entirely from public records (LARA, ICHAT, FOIA, court filings)
- GLLG's confidential communications with Banks stay protected

Kevin has zero need for anything in GLLG's files. The evidence base is public.
