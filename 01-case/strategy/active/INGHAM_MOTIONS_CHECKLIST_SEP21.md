### 7. 📝 CONSIDER: Motion for Entry of Default (Clerk's Entry)
**What:** Before the full motion for default judgment, you can request the clerk enter a "default" under MCR 2.603(A)(1). This is a ministerial act — the clerk checks whether an answer was filed within the time allowed. If not, default is entered.
**Then:** After default is entered, file Motion for Default Judgment (damages hearing).
**Advantage:** Clerk's entry doesn't require a motion hearing. It's administrative.

## STRATEGIC NOTES

**Why this sequence matters:**

1. **Alt service order → default entry → default judgment.** This is the cleanest path. If the court grants alternative service and deems Sep 18 service effective, the answer deadline has already passed. Clerk enters default → you file for default judgment → damages hearing.

2. **The PPO hearing on Sep 29 is the forcing function.** If Banks doesn't appear, the PPO is entered. If he appears, he has to explain why he hasn't identified counsel, hasn't answered the civil complaint, and hasn't responded to the C&D. Either outcome helps.

3. **"Identify counsel or be deemed pro se"** is the knife. Banks can't claim he has a lawyer without naming one. If he names Perkins, Perkins has documented conflicts (brother is judge, AGC complaint). If he doesn't name anyone, he's pro se — and his "J.D." claim is on the record.

4. **Leon's text message ("see you in court") is your best exhibit for actual notice on the Smiths.** A defendant who says "see you in court" has admitted he knows about the litigation. He can't claim he wasn't served.
