## Michigan Case Law — Authorities for Each Pattern

### Established Custodial Environment

| Case | Holding | Our Use |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Berger v. Berger**, 277 Mich App 700, 706 (2008) | Custodial environment is established when "the child naturally looks to the custodian for guidance, discipline, necessities, and parental comfort" | DaSean is the ECE — 9 years daily care |
| **Pierron v. Pierron**, 282 Mich App 222, 234 (2009) | An ECE is demonstrated by the stability and interdependence of the relationship, not by a court order | No order exists; ECE is factual, not legal |
| **Baker v. Baker**, 411 Mich 567, 579-80 (1981) | Court must consider "over an appreciable time" — length of custody is critical | 9 years is an "appreciable time" by any standard |

### Unilateral Decisions / Factor (j)

| Case | Holding | Our Use |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Lombardo v. Lombardo**, 202 Mich App 151 (1993) | Relocation without other parent's consent or court order weighs against relocating parent on Factor (d) | She moved children from MMSA to PCA without consent (Plaintiff's Doc 4, pp.1-3) |
| **Rains v. Rains**, 301 Mich App 313, 328 (2013) | Factor (j) considers whether parent "has willingly aided in thwarting the other parent's efforts" | Father's Day ambush (Plaintiff's M-12); blocked phone; no school notification |
| **Brown v. Loveman**, 260 Mich App 576, 595 (2004) | Factor (j) weighs against parent who "placed barriers between the child and the other parent" | Phone block, one-way communications, scheduling over his time (Plaintiff's M-28/29/30) |

### Income Concealment / Credibility / Factor (f)

| Case | Holding | Our Use |
|------|---------|---------|
| **Fletcher v. Fletcher**, 229 Mich App 19, 28 (1998) | Factor (f) includes "willingness to... facilitate the close and continuing parent-child relationship" — includes honesty with the court | Blank FOC 23 while admitting "breadwinner" (Doc 1, Para 10) |
| **Foskett v. Foskett**, 247 Mich App 1, 13 (2001) | Failure to disclose income reflects on moral fitness under Factor (f) | MDHHS notices in hand (Plaintiff's Doc 6, pp.30-36), wrote "N/A" anyway |
| **Stallworth v. Stallworth**, 275 Mich App 282 (2007) | Court may impute income to a parent who refuses to disclose | Supports Motion to Compel Amended FOC 23 + Interim Support |

### Device Monitoring / Privacy

| Case | Holding | Our Use |
|------|---------|---------|
| **MCL 750.539d** | It is a felony to "install, place, or use in any private place... any device for observing, recording, transmitting, photographing, or eavesdropping" | She admits monitoring children's devices to overhear private conversations (Doc 1, Para 13) |
| **Dombrowski v. Dombrowski**, unpub. Mich App (2017) | Electronic surveillance of other parent through children weighs against surveilling parent | Spying on DaSean through kids' devices |

### Established Custodial Environment — Burden of Proof

| Case | Holding | Our Use |
|------|---------|---------|

| **Vodvarka v. Grasmeyer**, 259 Mich App 499 (2003) | Proper cause / change of circumstances threshold for modifying custody (applicable to initial determinations of ECE) | Her filing is effectively seeking to change an established arrangement |
