## COMPARISON METHODOLOGY

### For Court / Police Report:

1. **Side-by-side exhibit**: Green card "signature" next to authentic signature
2. **Point out inconsistencies**: letter formation, slant, pressure, spacing
3. **Note impossibilities**: e.g., if Marquita was at a documented location when
   the green card was supposedly signed at the door
4. **Note pattern**: all signatures appear similar → same hand wrote all of them

### Professional Analysis (if needed later):
- Forensic document examiner (FDE) can provide expert opinion
- MI SOS Forensic Science Division can assist law enforcement
- FBI Laboratory Services (Questioned Documents Unit) — for federal prosecution

### Key Questions for Each Green Card:
1. Who ACTUALLY signed the green card?
2. Does the signature match the addressee's authentic signature?
3. Do ALL the "different" signatures share handwriting characteristics?
4. Does the handwriting match LEON's authentic signature instead?
5. Was the "Addressee" or "Agent" box checked?
