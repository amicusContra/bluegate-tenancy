## LEGAL SIGNIFICANCE

### 1. Post-Service UPL (Unauthorized Practice of Law)

Banks claimed to be an attorney AFTER being served with a
complaint that specifically alleges UPL. This demonstrates:
- **Continuing violation** — not a one-time slip
- **Consciousness of guilt** — knew about the complaint, did it anyway
- **Pattern of conduct** — supplements the August 29 in-person claim
- Strengthens the State Bar complaint (filed Aug 31)

### 2. Witness Intimidation / Tampering

Banks contacted DaSean — a party and potential witness — AFTER
being served with the lawsuit. His statements:
- Characterized lawful litigation as "stalking"
- Told DaSean that Kevin is "a problem"
- Invoked "judges and law enforcement"

This pattern constitutes **intimidation of a witness/party**:
- MCL 750.122 — Intimidating a witness
- MCR 2.114(D) — Improper post-service conduct
- Federal: 18 USC 1512 (if connected to federal proceedings)

### 3. Consciousness of Guilt

The immediate phone call after service shows Banks:
- Perceived the lawsuit as a threat
- Attempted to undermine the plaintiff through a third party
- Tried to maintain control over DaSean's perception
- Did not contact his own attorney — contacted the father directly

### 4. "Judges and Law Enforcement in His Interactions"

This statement, if proven, corroborates:
- Miller's board membership at PCA
- Yancey's financial ties to Banks' consulting firm
- Potential improper ex parte contacts with the judiciary

If Banks is telling the truth about having judges in his network,
he just confirmed the JTC complaint allegations. If he's lying,

**Either way, it's bad for Banks.**
