## VENUE TRANSFER ROUTES

### Route 1: PRE-JUDGMENT Transfer — MCR 2.222(B)
**This is the route. Available NOW.**

MCR 2.222(A): "The court may order a change of venue of a
civil action... for the convenience of parties and witnesses
or when an impartial trial cannot be had where the action
is pending."

MCR 2.222(B): "If the venue of the action is proper, the
court may not change the venue on its own initiative, but
may do so **only on motion of a party**."

**Key 2025 SCAO memo (FOCB Memorandum 2025-01) confirms:**
> "A prejudgment transfer is governed by MCR 2.222(B)
> (change of venue). MCR 2.222(A) allows a court to change
> venue for the convenience of parties or witnesses."

**DaSean can file a Motion to Change Venue arguing:**

   (Deanna, Kevin), his work — all in Ingham.

**Who can file this motion?**
Under MCR 2.222, either party can move for venue change.
DaSean is the defendant/counter-plaintiff. He can file.

Under Dawley v. Hall (2018 MSC), MCR 2.223 (improper venue)
can only be raised by a defendant or the court sua sponte.
But MCR 2.222 (proper venue, convenience) is broader —
any party can file.

### Route 2: POST-JUDGMENT Transfer — MCR 3.212
**Available AFTER initial custody order entered.**

Requirements (when party files):
- ✅ Neither party has resided in Wayne for 6+ months
  → DaSean hasn't. Shayla's Wayne residence is questionable.
- ✅ At least one party has resided in Ingham for 6+ months
  → DaSean has.
- ✅ Ingham is NOT contiguous to Wayne
  → Correct. Livingston County sits between them.

**Advantage of Route 2:** If FOC or the court initiates
the transfer, the residency requirements DON'T APPLY.

**The play:** If Ingham FOC becomes aware of the custody
case through the PPO proceedings (same parties, same family),
the Ingham FOC could initiate a transfer. The 2025 SCAO memo
specifically says:

> "If a friend of the court becomes aware of a more recent
> [order] involving the same parties issued in a different
> [county], [the FOC may] initiate a transfer..."

If Koenig enters PPO orders on Sep 29, those are Ingham
orders involving the same family. That gives Ingham FOC
a hook.

   the court or FOC initiates (not a party), the residency
