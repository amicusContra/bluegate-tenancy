Banks represented himself to Detroit Police officers as "an attorney."
He is NOT a licensed attorney. He has no bar number. He is a convicted
felon (MCL 750.174a — embezzlement, Case No. 14-006194-FH, convicted 2016).

unless admitted to the State Bar of Michigan.

Banks exercised legal authority he does not possess to:
- Control police officers' interaction with a parent
- Direct the outcome of a parental access dispute

of Banks' continued unauthorized practice of law — in front of witnesses

The custody case (26-108221-DC) is PENDING — no determination has been made.

from a parent or guardian who has custody or parenting time rights

Banks is NOT a parent. Banks is NOT a guardian. Banks is NOT an attorney.
Banks used a false claim of legal authority to prevent a father from
accessing his children at a school. The police assisted based on
