### Step 2: Threshold Hearing (Initial)
For **custody motions**, Wayne County uses a **two-stage process**:

**STAGE 1 — THRESHOLD HEARING (Referee)**
- Held before the assigned FOC referee at Penobscot
- Referee determines: has the moving party met the **threshold of pleading**?
- This means: has DaSean shown enough facts to justify a full investigation?
- If YES → referred to **F.A.M.E.** (Family Assessment, Mediation & Evaluation)
- If NO → motion may be dismissed at threshold stage
- This is a FORMAL evidentiary hearing (MCR 3.215(D)):
  - Sworn testimony
  - Michigan Rules of Evidence apply
  - Record is kept
  - Witnesses can be called
  - Exhibits can be submitted

**STAGE 2 — F.A.M.E. INVESTIGATION (if threshold met)**
- Family Assessment, Mediation and Evaluation
- Full custody investigation:
  - Parent interviews (both DaSean and Shayla, separately)
  - Home visits (Dresden, wherever DaSean lives)
  - School records review
  - Financial/employment review
  - Children's welfare assessment
  - Possibly talk to children (depending on age/maturity)
- F.A.M.E. produces a **written report and recommendation**
- Return hearing scheduled before the **assigned Judge (Triplett)**

### Step 3: Return Hearing Before Judge
- Triplett receives F.A.M.E. report + referee's recommendation
- Full hearing on custody — both sides present
- Judge rules based on:
  - F.A.M.E. report (heavily weighted)
  - Referee's threshold findings
  - Both parties' testimony
  - Best interest factors (MCL 722.23)

## THE VENUE PLAY — FIGHT AT FOC, NOT AT JUDGE

### The Problem With a Direct Venue Motion
Filing a Motion to Transfer Venue goes to Judge Triplett. She rules on it.
Even if the law supports transfer, judges resist losing jurisdiction.
A pro se filing arguing venue is easy to deny on procedural grounds.

### The FOC Route (Much Smarter)
Instead of arguing venue before the judge, **let FOC discover it themselves**.

**How it works:**

1. DaSean presents his custody case to the FOC referee at threshold hearing
2. As part of his testimony, he presents evidence of where children actually live:
   - Children at PCA (Detroit) but previously at MMSA (Macomb)
   - Shayla's COA from Bradford (Ingham) to Dresden (Wayne) — but lives in Eastpointe (Macomb)
   - Family network is Macomb (Stricker, Normandy, Merrimac, Virginia — ALL Eastpointe/Clinton Twp)
   - DaSean lives in Ingham County (Lansing)
3. FOC investigates — **home visits reveal the truth**:
   - Dresden may be a mailing address, not where children sleep
   - Shayla's actual residence is a Smith family home in Macomb
   - DaSean is in Ingham County
4. FOC findings create the FACTUAL BASIS for transfer
5. **Two paths from here:**

### Path A: FOC-Initiated Transfer (Best Case)
Under SCAO FOCB Memorandum 2025-01 and MCR 3.212:
- **FOC can initiate a case transfer itself**
- FOC CTCs (Case Transfer Coordinators) communicate between counties
- SCAO recommends transferring to "the county where the support payer resides
  or to where the child primarily resides"
- If FOC initiates, the $150 transfer fee is WAIVED
- Both CTCs must agree, then FOC seeks transfer order from judge
- Judge signs the order based on FOC recommendation

### Path B: FOC Advises DaSean to File Motion
Under MCR 3.212 and MCL 722.1207:
- FOC can "advise parties that they can file a motion or consent to transfer venue"
- DaSean files Motion to Transfer Venue backed by FOC's own investigation findings
- Triplett rules, but she has FOC's facts supporting the motion
- Much harder to deny when FOC itself found the children don't live in Wayne County

### Path C: UCCJEA Inconvenient Forum (MCL 722.1207)
Can be raised by party motion, court's own motion, OR request of another court.
Factors that favor transfer (all apply here):
- (a) Domestic violence has occurred — Sep 2 assault at Dresden
- (b) Length of time child resided outside Wayne County — Macomb schools, Macomb family homes
- (c) Distance between courts — Ingham (Lansing) to Wayne (Detroit) is 90 miles
- (d) Relative financial circumstances — DaSean in Ingham, Smith network in Macomb
- (f) Nature and location of evidence — children, witnesses, school records all Macomb/Ingham
- (g) Ability to decide expeditiously — Ingham has no political entanglements

### Why This Is Better Than a Direct Motion
| Direct Venue Motion | FOC-Routed Venue Transfer |
|---|---|
| DaSean argues it himself | FOC investigator discovers it |
| Judge can deny on discretion | FOC recommends based on findings |
| Looks like forum shopping | Looks like child's best interest |
| Pro se = easy to dismiss | Institutional recommendation = weight |
| Shayla opposes = adversarial | FOC is neutral fact-finder |
