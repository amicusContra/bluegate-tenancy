### Mandatory Reporting Obligation

Under MCL 722.623, a licensed social worker who "has reasonable cause
to suspect child abuse or neglect" **shall** make an immediate report
to MDHHS Children's Protective Services.

Failure to report is a misdemeanor under MCL 722.633.

Parental alienation, financial exploitation of children's identities
(using their SSNs for benefits fraud), and exposure to household
instability (8 people, serial bankruptcies, international travel
without the other parent's knowledge) may constitute neglect or
emotional abuse under Michigan's Child Protection Law.
