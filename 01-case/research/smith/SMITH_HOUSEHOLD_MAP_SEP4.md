### Why This Matters

1. **Separate BK filings hide household income.** If Marquita, Simone,
   and Leon all live together (or share resources), their combined income
   would change the means test calculation for ALL of their bankruptcies.

2. **Benefits optimization.** Three separate "households" = three separate
   claims for SNAP, Medicaid, housing assistance, childcare subsidies,
   and utility assistance. If they're actually operating as one economic
   unit, this is benefits fraud.

3. **Asset concealment.** Leon owns Dresden. Marquita is on the deed but
   doesn't list it. Simone parks her car there but claims Eastpointe.
   The $68K mortgage isn't on any BK filing. The house itself isn't on
   any BK filing. The family has effectively made Dresden "invisible"
   to the federal courts.

4. **Children as leverage.** DaSean's daughters are at the one address
   (Dresden) that NONE of the bankruptcy filers claim to live at. The
   children are parked at the "clean" address — the one not in any BK
   filing — while the adults file from addresses they may not actually
   occupy.
