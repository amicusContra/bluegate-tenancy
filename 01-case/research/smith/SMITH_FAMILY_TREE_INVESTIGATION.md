## RESEARCH PATHS (Priority Order)

### PATH 1: MACOMB ROD — PARCEL HISTORY (Fastest)
**Tool:** macombcountymi-web.tylerhost.net
**Method:** Search by PARCEL NUMBER (not name)
**Cost:** Free to search; $6/document download

| Parcel | Property | What to Search |
|--------|----------|---------------|
| **1215326015** | XXXXX N River Rd | All documents recorded against this parcel |
| **1217427030** | XXXXX Old N River Rd | All documents recorded against this parcel |

**What you'll find in parcel history:**
- **Death Affidavit** or **Affidavit of Heirship** — filed to transfer title from deceased to heirs (names ALL heirs)
- **Probate Court Order** — if estate went through probate, the order will name personal representative and heirs
- **Original deeds** — who sold/transferred to Leonard T Smith Sr
- **Prior mortgages** — paid off, discharged, or inherited
- **Tax liens or judgments** — any claims against the property
- **The FULL chain of title** — every owner back to platting

The parcel search is better than a name search because it catches
ALL documents regardless of what name was used.

### PATH 2: MACOMB COUNTY PROBATE COURT
**Tool:** courts.macombgov.org (Probate Division)
**Also:** Odyssey case search — courtexplorer.com or micourtaccess.com
**Cost:** Free search; nominal for copies

**Search for:**
- "Smith Leonard" — any probate/estate case
- "Smith" at Harrison Twp addresses
- Case type: Decedent Estates (DE), Trust proceedings

**What probate gives you:**
- **Petition for probate** — names the decedent, lists ALL heirs
- **Inventory** — lists ALL property the decedent owned at death
- **Letters of Authority** — names the Personal Representative (who managed the estate)
- **Closing documents** — how property was distributed
- **Will** (if one exists) — may name specific bequests
- **Affidavit of heirs** — if intestate, lists all legal heirs with relationships

**This is the GOLD MINE.** If Leonard T Sr died and the estate went through
Macomb County Probate, the entire family tree is in the file.
