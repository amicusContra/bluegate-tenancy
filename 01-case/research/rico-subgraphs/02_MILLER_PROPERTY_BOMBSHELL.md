## ANALYSIS

### 1. The Forfeiture-Redemption Dependency

Between 2014 and 2018, Miller's 48214 property went through
**six forfeiture cycles** — each one processed by the Wayne
County Treasurer's office under Eric Sabree.

In Michigan, the county treasurer has discretion over:
- When to initiate forfeiture proceedings
- How aggressively to pursue auction
- Whether to extend redemption windows
- How to process redemption payments

**Six times, Sabree's office could have pushed Miller's
property to auction. Six times, she was allowed to redeem.**

Total redemption payments: **$11,439.98** — spread over 4 years.
This is approximately $2,860/year in back taxes on a property
that would eventually sell for $370,000.

A judge who owed back taxes to the Treasurer's office for
