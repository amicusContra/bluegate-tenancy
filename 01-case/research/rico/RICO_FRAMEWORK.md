## II. PATTERN OF RACKETEERING ACTIVITY

Federal RICO (18 U.S.C. § 1961(1)) defines "racketeering activity" to include federal crimes (mail/wire fraud, Hobbs Act, money laundering, etc.) and state felonies. Need **≥2 predicate acts** that are **related** (*H.J. Inc. v. Northwestern Bell*, 492 U.S. 229 (1989)) and show **continuity** (threat of continued activity or closed-period spanning substantial time).

> **Note on judicial immunity:** Civil RICO against judges for **judicial acts** (rulings, sentencing) is barred by absolute immunity (*Mireles v. Waco*, 502 U.S. 9 (1991)). But non-judicial conduct — co-founding schools, board memberships, campaign payments, endorsements — is NOT immune (*Clark v. Conahan*, 737 F. Supp. 2d 239 (M.D. Pa. 2010) — Kids for Cash template).
