Think of it like CLIA certification for a clinical lab. You can
know everything about running a PCR assay. You can have a PhD in

you were wrong. Because you weren't authorized.

## THE MICHIGAN STATUTE

### MCL 450.681 — Penalty for Unauthorized Practice of Law

> Any person who shall practice law, or who shall hold himself
> out to the public as being entitled to practice law, or use
> any title or description which shall imply that he is
> authorized to practice law in this state who has not been
> duly licensed and authorized to do so shall be guilty of a
> **felony**.

**Classification:** Felony
**Penalty:** Up to **5 years imprisonment** and/or fine
**Additional:** Contempt of court

This is not a misdemeanor. Not a regulatory slap. A felony. The
Michigan legislature decided that pretending to be a lawyer is
serious enough for prison time. Because when someone relies on
legal advice from a fake lawyer, the consequences can be
devastating — lost custody, lost property, lost freedom.

states where it's a **felony**. Here's why it matters:

similar severity because the harm is comparable.
