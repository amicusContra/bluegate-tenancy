## LEGAL FOUNDATION FOR REFILING ON NEW EVIDENCE

Dismissal with prejudice operates under **res judicata** — it bars the same claims between the same parties on the same facts. It does NOT bar:

1. **New claims** arising from newly discovered evidence
2. **New acts** committed after the dismissal date
3. **Fraud on the court** — if the cooperator lied in their sworn declaration, the original dismissal can be vacated (MCR 2.612(C)(1)(c))
4. **Different causes of action** — cooperation on civil fraud does not bar a subsequent filing for, e.g., conspiracy, conversion, or RICO predicate acts not covered by the original complaint

The cooperator's plea covers what they disclosed. What they hid is a new case.
