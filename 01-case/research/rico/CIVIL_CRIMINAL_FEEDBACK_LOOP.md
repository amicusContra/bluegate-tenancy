## HOW CRIMINAL FEEDS CIVIL

Criminal investigations (OIG, MDHHS) produce evidence that
strengthens the civil case:

### What criminal investigations generate:

| Investigation | What it produces | Civil value |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------|
| **USPS OIG** | Investigator's report, Harper Woods PO camera footage, postal clerk interview, Marquita's statement | Proves mail interception — eavesdropping/invasion of privacy damages in civil suit |
| **USPS OIG** | Leon's employment records, any administrative action | Proves USPS employee connection; quantifies damages (career loss = financial harm to household the girls live in) |
| **MDHHS OIG** | Fraud investigation findings, address verification, income verification | Proves address fraud pattern — supports unjust enrichment claim in civil suit |
| **MDHHS OIG** | EBT transaction records | Proves Deanna's card was used by Shayla — supports exploitation claim |
| **Criminal charges (if filed)** | Arraignment, plea, conviction | Collateral estoppel — criminal conviction CONCLUSIVELY PROVES the underlying facts in civil court. Shayla can't relitigate in civil what she was convicted of in criminal. |

### Collateral estoppel (issue preclusion):

If Marquita is convicted of mail theft (18 USC 1702/1708),
that conviction ESTABLISHES the following facts in civil court,
which cannot be disputed:

1. Marquita intercepted restricted delivery mail
2. The mail was addressed to someone other than Marquita
3. Marquita was not authorized to receive it
4. The interception was knowing and intentional

Kevin's civil suit for invasion of privacy, eavesdropping, and
interference with communications is then a DAMAGES-ONLY trial.
Liability is already established by the criminal conviction.

one proceeding is available to the others. Every lie told in
one proceeding is perjury provable in another. Every refusal
to produce documents in civil discovery is sanctionable AND
suggests consciousness of guilt in criminal proceedings.

## WHY THE SMITHS CANNOT MAKE THIS STOP

To stop the civil suit: settle. Kevin sets the terms. The terms
include the custody consent order. Shayla won't agree.

To stop the OIG: they can't. Federal investigation. No private
party can withdraw a federal complaint once the OIG decides to
investigate. And they will investigate — a USPS employee's
household committing mail fraud at a USPS facility is exactly
what the OIG exists for.

To stop the MDHHS: they can't. State investigation. Benefits
fraud with children's healthcare involved = mandatory review.

To stop the custody case: consent order. Shayla signs. DaSean
gets sole custody. This is the ONLY proceeding the Smiths can
resolve by agreement. And it's the one Shayla refuses to resolve.

**The Smiths cannot settle their way out of all three proceedings
simultaneously.** The criminal investigations proceed regardless.
The civil suit settles only on Kevin's terms. The custody case
resolves only if Shayla concedes. They would need to cooperate
on ALL fronts — and cooperation requires the kind of good faith
they've never shown.
