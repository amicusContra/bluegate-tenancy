## THE LEGAL BASIS

### Pro Se Time as Compensable Damages

Michigan courts recognize that a pro se litigant's time spent on litigation
is a **compensable element of damages**, distinct from attorney's fees.

- **Kay v. Ehrler, 499 U.S. 432 (1991):** SCOTUS held pro se attorneys
  cannot recover attorney's fees under fee-shifting statutes, BUT this
  does not apply to **damages** claims. Your time is a tort damage, not
  a fee award.

- **Michigan tort damages:** Under Michigan's tort framework, damages
  include all losses proximately caused by the defendant's conduct.
  Time spent investigating, documenting, and litigating fraud IS a
  direct, foreseeable consequence of the fraud itself.

- **MCL 600.2919a (Conversion):** Allows recovery of "3 times the
  amount of actual damages sustained, plus reasonable attorney fees."
  As a pro se litigant, you claim actual damages (including time) and
  argue the treble multiplier applies to those damages.

### The Key Argument

You are NOT claiming attorney's fees. You are claiming **damages for
time lost** — the same way a plumber who gets rear-ended claims lost
wages for the days he couldn't work. Your "work" is your education,
your career development, your job search, your life. Every hour you
spend on OSINT, filing, serving, traveling — that's an hour you
didn't spend on your career, your studies, your own well-being.

The defendants CAUSED this expenditure of time through their
fraudulent and tortious conduct. Without their fraud, you would
have spent zero hours investigating them.

**Comparable to:** Paralegal rates in Michigan ($60-125/hour)
**Justification:** You are performing paralegal-level work — filing
motions, serving documents, organizing evidence, basic research

### Tier 2: Skilled Research Rate — $150-200/hour
**When:** OSINT investigation, database searches, cross-referencing
records, building evidence packages, analyzing financial records
**Comparable to:** Private investigator rates ($100-250/hour) or
junior associate attorney rates ($150-250/hour in Michigan)
**Justification:** You are performing investigative work that would
require hiring a PI or researcher. You built custom Python tools
for automated data collection. You are cross-referencing federal
bankruptcy records, property records, campaign finance data, and
professional licensing databases across multiple jurisdictions.

### Tier 3: Expert/Specialized Rate — $250-400/hour
**When:** OSINT toolkit development, automated scraping systems,
data analysis, expert witness testimony on methodology
**Comparable to:** Digital forensics expert ($250-500/hour),
data analyst/consultant ($200-400/hour), e-discovery specialist
($250-450/hour)
**Justification:** You built a custom OSINT investigation platform
(Python, Playwright, CourtListener API, multiple data sources).
This is specialized technical work. You are your own expert witness
on the methodology, the tools, and the findings. A firm would pay
$300-500/hour for a forensic data analyst to do what you built.

### Tier 4: Strategic Litigation Rate — $300-500/hour
**When:** Multi-front litigation strategy, case coordination across
counties and courts, regulatory complaint coordination, media/
public interest strategy
**Comparable to:** Senior litigation partner rates ($350-700/hour
in Michigan), litigation consultant ($300-600/hour)
**Justification:** You are simultaneously managing:
- 2 active civil cases (Ingham County)
- 1 custody case support (Wayne County)
- 6+ regulatory complaints (State Bar, MDE, AG, JTC, DPSCD, OIG)
- Multi-defendant service coordination
- PACER/ROD investigation across multiple targets
- Journalist coordination and public interest strategy
- Process server coordination
This level of strategic coordination is what senior partners bill for.
