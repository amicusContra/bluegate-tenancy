personal injury attorney with Mike Morse Injury Law Firm."**

including the title, description, and introduction — and approved
it for publication. The title "From G.E.D to Law Degree" was a
deliberate editorial choice by Michigan's largest injury firm.

   professional

   means Banks is NOT an attorney

   convictions) would have changed the entire narrative
4. **Asked "When are you giving it another shot?" [21:36]** —
   treating the C&F denial as a temporary setback rather than

   to practice law
5. **Did not report** — MRPC 8.3 requires attorneys to report

   using "J.D." publicly without licensure. Morse saw this
   and did not report.

### The "Content Checked" Liability
