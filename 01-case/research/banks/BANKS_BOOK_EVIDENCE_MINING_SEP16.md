## WHY EVERYTHING IN THIS BOOK IS ADMISSIBLE

### Michigan Rule of Evidence 801(d)(2) — Party Admissions

> **"A statement is not hearsay if the statement is offered
> against a party and is (A) the party's own statement."**

Banks is a **named party** in:
- *Mok v. Banks et al.*, Case No. 2026-4349-CZ
- *Mok v. Smith et al.*, Case No. 2026-4301-CZ (amended in)

**Every word in "It Had 2 Happen" is admissible against Banks
in both civil cases.** It's his own published statement, offered
against him as a party-opponent. It is NOT hearsay under MRE
801(d)(2)(A). No foundation required beyond authentication
(which is trivial — it's a published book with his name on it).

### What This Means Practically:

1. **Any factual claim** Banks makes in the book can be presented
   to the court as HIS OWN STATEMENT
2. **Any admission** (of criminal conduct, addresses, employment,
   relationships) is binding
3. **Any inconsistency** between the book and his deposition
   testimony is devastating impeachment
4. **Any omission** of material facts (like the bar denial, the
   8 felonies as "seventeen counts") proves knowing concealment
5. **The credential branding** ("Brian Banks J.D.") on the
   cover/Amazon listing is itself evidence of UPL

### Authentication (MRE 901):

The book self-authenticates as a published work. Just buy a
copy, hand it to the court, and quote from it. The ISBN, Amazon
listing, and publisher information establish it's genuinely
Banks' work. No witness needed.
