## NO API — BUT BULK DATA AVAILABLE

MI School Data (mischooldata.org) has **no public API**. Data access is through:
1. **Report Builder** → custom datasets, export as .xlsx (150K row limit)
2. **K-12 Data Files** → bulk CSV/Excel downloads by year
3. **EEM Public Data Sets** → entity master (CSV/Excel/XML) at cepi.state.mi.us/eem/
4. **Parent Dashboard / School Index** → rendered via Power BI (no scraping)
5. **Financial Transparency Dashboard** → district-level financials

For deep analysis: Use Report Builder → select MacDowell (82747) → export all years.

Detroit's charter school sector was supposed to provide alternatives
to failing public schools. Instead, many charters perform WORSE:

| Metric | MacDowell (Charter) | DPSCD Average | MI State Average |
|--------|-------------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Math proficiency | 3% | ~12% | 35% |
| ELA proficiency | 10-14% | ~16% | 45% |
| Per-pupil spending | $15,217 | ~$16,500 | ~$14,000 |

MacDowell spends MORE per pupil than the state average while
producing results in the BOTTOM 3%. The money goes in. The
education doesn't come out. Where does it go?

### The "Spiritual Leadership" Pattern

Your observation is sharp. In Detroit's charter sector:

1. **Judges serve as board chairs** (Miller = PCA, Yancey = MacDowell)
   — community authority figures lending credibility

   Law & Public Service" is aspirational marketing
3. **Community trust is exploited** — families trust judges and
   community leaders; they send their children
4. **The data is hidden** — parents don't check MI School Data;
