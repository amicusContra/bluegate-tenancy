## THE PLAYERS AND THEIR BREAKING POINTS

### TIER 1: DPSCD BOARD — THE AUTHORIZERS

#### Bishop Corletta Vaughn (Board VP / Former President)
**Power:** Led the board that approved PCA's charter (July 2025).
Still on the board as VP. Controls 1 of 7 votes on any oversight
or revocation action. Connects Banks to 92 faith-based MOUs and
70+ churches.

**Her incentives to protect Banks:**
- Personal: She publicly endorsed him on LinkedIn ("Proud of you
  and proud to SUPPORT YOUR WORK"). Walking that back is
  embarrassing.
- Institutional: She championed faith-based partnerships for 8
  years. Banks' schools are part of that infrastructure. If Banks
  falls, the faith-based model she built takes a hit.

  personality. Admitting she was conned by a felon is not her
  brand.

**Her breaking point:**
- **Federal investigation.** If DOE OIG opens a case, Vaughn's
  LinkedIn endorsement becomes a liability, not a boast. She'll
  distance herself fast to protect her own position.
- **Media exposure.** Vaughn is public-facing — TV, podcasts,
  press. If Elrick or Jones names her as the board president who
  approved a convicted felon's charter while publicly endorsing
  him, her brand takes damage she can't repair with a sermon.
- **Other board members.** If enough board members decide Banks
  is a liability, Vaughn can't shield him alone. She needs a
  majority.
- **Her own nonprofit exposure.** Go Tell It Ministry is a
  501(c)(3) with international operations. If someone looks at
  its finances with the same rigor applied to Banks, she may have
  her own vulnerabilities. She won't risk that scrutiny to
  protect someone else.

**Your move:**
- DOE OIG complaint (FILED — names DPSCD authorization as the
  mechanism enabling fraud)
- FOIA the July 2025 board vote (FILED — requesting minutes
  and recusal records)
- DO NOT attack Vaughn directly. Let the federal investigation
  and media create the pressure. She'll protect herself before
  she protects Banks.

## HOW THE SHIELD COLLAPSES

The protection network has a structural weakness: **every
person in it is protecting themselves, not Banks.** The moment
the personal cost of protecting Banks exceeds the cost of
abandoning him, they will abandon him. This is not loyalty —
it's calculation.

### The Collapse Sequence:

**1. Ellis/Yecart flips first** (weeks 2-4)
She's a vendor with a CPA license. When the FOIA responses come
back and discovery opens, she'll cooperate to protect her license
and her other clients. She'll provide the financial records that
prove Banks' self-dealing.

**2. Perlmuter stays neutral** (weeks 1-4)
He formed an entity. He'll answer subpoenas. He won't fight.

**3. Stallworth lawyers up** (weeks 2-6)
She'll get a personal attorney who will tell her she has fiduciary
liability. She'll either settle, resign from the board, or
negotiate cooperation in exchange for reduced exposure.

**4. Dagnogo distances** (weeks 4-8)
When media coverage begins, Dagnogo will quietly stop advocating
for Banks at DPSCD. She won't vote to revoke (too politically
costly), but she'll stop blocking oversight.

**5. Vaughn repositions** (weeks 6-12)
When the DOE OIG contacts DPSCD, Vaughn will frame herself as
the board member who "didn't have all the information." She'll
support an audit to show she takes oversight seriously. This is
her saving herself, not helping you.

**6. Church network goes silent** (weeks 8-16)
No pastor will publicly defend Banks after Elrick publishes.
They won't attack him either. They'll just stop talking about
him. The redemption narrative dies quietly.

**7. Miller loses the election** (November 3)
If the WXYZ and Free Press coverage hits before November, Miller's
re-election becomes a referendum on three ethics scandals and a
charter school chaired for a convicted felon. If she loses, PCA's
board chair is gone, and the school has to reorganize.

## MLCOA (Most Likely Course of Action)

The power network slow-walks everything. Vaughn and Dagnogo don't
actively protect Banks but don't actively investigate either.
Stallworth circles the wagons at MacDowell. Ellis continues
providing CFO services while documenting everything for her own
protection. The church network stays quiet.

**You break this by:**
- Federal pressure (DOE OIG) forcing DPSCD to respond
- Media (Elrick, Jones) making silence politically impossible
- Discovery (lawsuit) forcing testimony under oath
- Time: Miller's November election creates a natural deadline
  that forces the story into public view

## MDCOA (Most Dangerous Course of Action)

The entire network coordinates: Vaughn leads DPSCD to block
FOIAs and delay audits. Dagnogo activates political allies.
Stallworth and Perlmuter restructure the entities. Ellis
destroys or alters financial records. The church network rallies
parents against you.

**You survive this by:**
- Spoliation notice makes destruction a crime, not just bad
  optics
- FOIAs to DPSCD AND Wayne RESA create parallel record requests
  — both would have to be blocked
- DOE OIG bypasses DPSCD entirely — federal investigators don't
  need DPSCD's permission
- The record is already fossilized in two OSINT briefs distributed
  to 10 people and 2 journalists
- Coordinated cover-ups require trust. This network is built on
  money, not loyalty. The first person who gets personally
  threatened (Ellis, Stallworth, Holland) will break.
