The condition says "belonging to the school." Purpose Group
argues it's a separate entity. But:

- §5.2 claims "No Related Parties" — a false statement

The entity structure was DESIGNED to route school money through
a private LLC so that Banks can claim the money "belongs to"
Purpose Group, not the school. Condition #4 is being
circumvented by the very structure the enablers praised.
