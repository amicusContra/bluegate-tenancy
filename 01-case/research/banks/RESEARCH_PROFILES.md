### The Contamination

1. **Voted to approve the charter of a man with 8 felony convictions.**

   "Support your work and vision" is not a neutral statement from the

   The Board that Vaughn led has a financial interest in PCA's success.

   a disinterested regulator.

   through GEI COGIC. The DPSCD Faith-Based Council, which Vaughn
   participates in, connects the board to the church networks that
   Banks uses for legitimacy and fundraising.

5. **Pattern: DPSCD rejected Banks in 2019, then approved him in 2025.**
   What changed? Banks got a PhD (Walden, 2022) and an admin cert
   (MDE, Aug 2023). But his 8 felonies didn't change. His FBI wiretap
   target status didn't change. His UPL pattern didn't change.
   What changed was the Board leadership.
