| **# NOT certified** | **~14 of 19** | **74% of teachers lack valid certification** |
| Guidance counselors | **0** | N/A listed |
| Student-teacher ratio | 19:1 | State avg: 16:1 |

**74% of MacDowell's full-time teachers are NOT CERTIFIED.**

Under Michigan law (MCL 380.1233b):
- Charter schools MUST employ certified, endorsed teachers
- Non-certified teachers require Annual Career Authorization (ACA)
- ACA requires: enrollment in teacher prep program + mentor + MDE approval
- ACA is supposed to be TEMPORARY — not 74% of your staff

Their own 2021 job posting (Chalkbeat) said:
> "Willingness to seek valid state certification **if needed**."

That's the tell. They're hiring people who aren't certified and
calling certification optional.

### What This Means for Students

When 74% of your teachers aren't certified to teach the subjects
they're teaching:
- They don't have the content knowledge
- They don't have the pedagogical training
- They haven't passed the Michigan Test for Teacher Certification
- They haven't completed student teaching
- **The kids get 3% math proficiency**

This isn't correlation. This is causation. You can't teach math
if you don't know math.
