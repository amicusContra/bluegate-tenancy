**Credentialing abuse.** Banks lists "J.D., Ph.D." after his name
on every platform — LinkedIn, school websites, the Pahara
Institute fellowship directory, Purpose Charter Academy's founder
page. He holds a Juris Doctor from Michigan State University
College of Law but has never been admitted to any bar. He has 8
felony convictions. The credentials are real. The authority they
imply is not. Each institutional platform that publishes "Dr. Brian
Banks, J.D., Ph.D." launders the last platform's endorsement.

**Social engineering.** Banks cultivates relationships with people
who have institutional power — judges, bishops, board members,
elected officials. Each relationship creates mutual legitimacy.
The judge sees the bishop's endorsement. The bishop sees the
judge's board membership. The authorizer sees both and assumes
due diligence was done. No one checks the primary source because
everyone trusts the network.

**Regulatory capture.** Every institution that should be checking
Banks' power is instead amplifying it. The charter authorizer
approved his school and collects fees from it. The school board
includes a sitting judge who is Banks' political successor and
self-described friend. The development team for his new school
includes a criminal division judge. The Michigan Department of
Education issued his administrator certificate with conditions
and has not visibly enforced them. The State Bar has not
prosecuted his unauthorized practice of law despite 14 years of
public reporting by M.L. Elrick.
