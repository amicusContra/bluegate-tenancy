## PART 1: THE REMAND — What It Was and Why It Matters

### The Setup

Someone close to Rita (likely a family member, based on context) was sentenced by **Barry County Circuit Court Judge Michael Schipper**. Schipper imposed **upward sentencing departures** — meaning he sentenced above what Michigan's sentencing guidelines called for. Under Michigan law, a judge can only depart upward from guidelines if they articulate **"substantial and compelling reasons"** grounded in the record and consistent with constitutional due process.

Rita's position: Schipper's departures were **constitutionally infirm** — inadequately supported, procedurally defective, and exceeding his lawful discretion.

### What "Remand as Leave Granted" Means

In plain English: The Michigan Supreme Court told the Court of Appeals, **"You were wrong to deny this. Look at it again, and this time treat it as if you granted the appeal."**

This is NOT a reversal (the MSC didn't decide the case itself). It's a structural intervention — the highest court in the state directed renewed review after the intermediate court had already said no.

### Why This Matters for Kevin

1. **Rita beat the odds as a non-attorney.** The MSC accepts a tiny fraction of applications. She built the application after appointed counsel gave up, and the Supreme Court ordered the state to respond.

2. **She identified what professional attorneys missed.** The MAACS lawyer left gaps. Rita found them and reframed the arguments herself.

3. **She knows how appellate courts actually move.** Her own words: "Institutions move when arguments are clear, records are clean, and persistence outlasts resistance." That's the same operational principle Kevin uses.

4. **Schipper got hit twice.** Two Supreme Court remands in 3 months. That doesn't happen unless the judge was systematically exceeding his authority — which is exactly what Rita documented.

5. **She also caught record tampering during the process.** Proof-of-service discrepancies, metadata contradictions — she turned an appeal into an institutional forensics investigation.
