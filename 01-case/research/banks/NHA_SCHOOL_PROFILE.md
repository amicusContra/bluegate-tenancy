## ACADEMIC PERFORMANCE

### The Good

- **Stanford CREDO study:** NHA students gain 3.5 months additional
  learning per year vs traditional public school peers
  - 69 additional days in math
  - 46 additional days in reading
  - Earned "gap busters" designation for closing achievement gaps
    for minority and poverty students
- **Basis Policy Research (Aug 2026):** NHA 8th graders perform like
  a top-10 state nationally (#4 reading, #10 math) while Michigan
  overall ranks 45th
- **US News 2026:** Top 8 elementary schools in Detroit are ALL
  charter schools — multiple NHA schools in the top 10
- **Pembroke Academy:** #1 elementary in Detroit, #186 in all of
  Michigan (out of 3,600+), 2024 National Blue Ribbon
- **Detroit Enterprise Academy:** Grade A on Michigan Context and
  Performance Report Card, Schools of Excellence Eagle Award 2024-25
- **7 NHA schools** have been named National Blue Ribbon Schools

### The Concerns

- **For-profit model:** NHA uses "sweeps contracts" where virtually
  all public revenue is transferred to NHA's corporate account
- **Building ownership:** NHA owns school buildings AND contents
  (desks, computers, books) even if purchased with taxpayer money
- **Above-market rent:** Schools pay rent to NHA subsidiaries that
  own the buildings; rent doesn't decrease after NHA recoups costs
- **Board independence:** Critics argue school boards have limited
  real oversight because NHA controls all operations
- **Financial transparency:** Brooklyn Excelsior audit (2012) —
  NY auditors couldn't determine how $10M was being used because
  NHA refused to provide full financials
- **BBB:** Not BBB accredited; complaints about customer service
  and responsiveness
- **Teacher reviews:** Trustpilot/BBB reviews from staff cite high
  turnover, inadequate special education support, and retaliation
- **Political connections:** Founder Huizenga is major GOP donor
  (Bush, Romney); close ties to Betsy DeVos

## GOVERNANCE & AUTHORIZATION

NHA schools are authorized by universities (not local school boards):
- Central Michigan University
- Grand Valley State University (largest NHA authorizer, 21 schools)
- Lake Superior State University
- Saginaw Valley State University

University authorization means:
- Independent oversight, not controlled by local politics
- Annual performance reviews against peer benchmarks
- Can revoke charter for non-performance
- Required financial audits

## HONEST ASSESSMENT

**NHA is not perfect.** It's a for-profit operation with legitimate
criticisms about financial transparency, the sweeps contract model,
board independence, and building ownership. Teacher reviews are mixed.
The political connections to DeVos and GOP donors are real.

**But it is a functioning, accredited, university-overseen school
system that educates 68,000 students without felons running it.**

The comparison to PCA is not close:
- NHA's worst scandal is a financial transparency audit dispute
- PCA's superintendent has 8 felony convictions and claims to be a lawyer
- NHA has 7 National Blue Ribbon schools
- PCA has a board chaired by a judge being sued for civil rights violations

For DaSean's children, moving from PCA to any NHA Detroit school is
an upgrade in every measurable dimension: academic performance,
governance, oversight, safety, and legitimacy.
