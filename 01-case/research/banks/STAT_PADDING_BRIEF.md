## SUMMARY OF DOCUMENTED FACTS

MacDowell Preparatory Academy, operated by Brian Roderick Banks
through The Purpose Group Michigan LLC, ranks **1,443rd out of
1,488** public elementary schools in Michigan (3rd percentile).
Its math proficiency rate is **3%**. Its reading proficiency rate
is **10-14%**. Chronic absenteeism reached **68.5%** in 2021-22.
Per-pupil spending is **$15,217** — higher than most nearby schools.

Despite these outcomes, Banks obtained DPSCD authorization for a
second charter school (Purpose Charter Academy) based on
representations of improvement at MacDowell, and subsequently
enrolled children — including the minor daughters of a father
who did not consent to the enrollment — at the new school.

This brief documents the statistical model that makes both
schools financially viable despite academic failure.

### Documented: Stallworth's Public Statement

### Documented: Actual Proficiency

| Subject | Proficiency Rate |
|---------|-----------------|
| Math | 3% |
| Reading | 10-14% |
| Science | 6-9% |

### Arithmetic

For a "30% proficiency gain" to yield 3% math proficiency, the
prior proficiency would have been approximately 2.3%.

```
Prior: 2.3%
Gain:  2.3% × 1.30 = 2.99% ≈ 3%
```

A "30% gain" from 2.3% to 3% represents a **0.7 percentage point**
improvement — meaning approximately **2-3 additional students**
demonstrated proficiency.

If those 2-3 students transferred in from other schools with
existing academic competence, the "gain" reflects enrollment
management, not instructional improvement.
