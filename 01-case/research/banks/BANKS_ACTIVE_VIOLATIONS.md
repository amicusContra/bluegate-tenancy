## 4. FBI WIRETAP — GASPER FIORE BRIBERY INVESTIGATION

**Source:** Elrick (July 3, 2022); federal court records

Banks was named as a **"target subject"** in an FBI wiretap
application targeting towing company owner Gasper Fiore in 2016.

FBI Special Agent Bob Beeckman's affidavit includes:
- Transcripts of conversations between Banks and Fiore
- Banks discussing helping Fiore secure an **MDOT contract**
- Banks discussing advocating for Fiore with a **"high-ranking
  police official"**
- Fiore offering Banks a mobile billboard (Banks declined)
- Banks asking if Fiore's daughter would "stop pushing
  amendments" if Fiore got the contract
- Fiore asking to meet Banks to "show him paperwork" — the
  FBI agent wrote: **"I believe that 'paperwork' in this
  context means a cash bribe payment."**

Fiore was later convicted of bribing Clinton Township Trustee
Dean Reynolds. Banks was not charged — likely because he resigned
from the Legislature before the investigation concluded.

**Legal status:** No charges filed. But the wiretap transcripts
exist in federal court records and can be subpoenaed. This
material is devastating for character and fitness arguments.

### Who to Notify

Not an active violation — but the transcripts should be
referenced in:
- State Bar UPL complaint (character and fitness)
- MI Dept of Education complaint (certificate fitness)
- AG referral (pattern of corruption)
- Elrick outreach (he already has the transcripts)
