### LEGAL BASIS (for Kevin's reference, not for DaSean to recite):

|-----------|-------------|
| **MCL 600.1651** | Change of venue — "for the convenience of parties and witnesses and in the interest of justice" |
| **MCR 3.205(d)** | Communication between courts in custody matters — judges can coordinate to avoid conflicting orders |
| **MCL 722.26** | Jurisdiction in custody — court where child has "home state" or "significant connection" |

| **UCCJEA (MCL 722.1101 et seq.)** | Home state jurisdiction — child must have lived in state 6 months. Michigan IS home state. The question is WHICH Michigan county |
