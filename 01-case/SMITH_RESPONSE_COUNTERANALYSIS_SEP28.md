## PART 3: THEIR DENIALS ARE NON-SPECIFIC AND PROVABLY FALSE

### MCR 2.312(B) — How RFA Denials Work
Under Michigan rules, a denial must "fairly meet the substance of the request."
A blanket "DENIED" without explanation, when the person has knowledge, is
improper. The court can deem improperly denied RFAs as ADMITTED and award
costs (MCR 2.312(D)).

### Simone: 26 out of 28 = "DENIED" with no explanation
She didn't fairly meet the substance. She just wrote "DENIED" to everything.
This is exactly what MCR 2.312(D) sanctions are for.

### Leon: 26 out of 30 = "OBJECTION" on relevance
He didn't deny anything — he refused to answer on relevance grounds.
RFA objections are evaluated strictly. If the requests ARE relevant to
the case, his objections fail and the matters are deemed admitted.
