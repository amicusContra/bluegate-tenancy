REQUEST FOR INVESTIGATION — ATTACHED NARRATIVE
Part III: Facts Alleged to Constitute Misconduct

Judicial Officer: Hon. Cylenthia LaToye Miller
Court: Third Judicial Circuit Court, Criminal Division,
      Detroit, Michigan

---

Judge Cylenthia LaToye Miller served on the nine-member
development team that founded Purpose Charter Academy (PCA), a
K-8 public charter school at 10101 E. Canfield Street, Detroit,
MI 48214.

This was reported by Chalkbeat on September 8, 2025: "The nine-
member development team behind Purpose Charter Academy includes
Michigan Third Circuit Court Judge Cylenthia LaToye Miller,
attorney Lamar Moreland, as well as educators, mental health
practitioners, and civic leaders, said Board member Sherry
Gay-Dagnogo."

PCA was founded by Brian Roderick Banks, who has 8 felony
convictions (plea deal, 2017), has never been admitted to the
Michigan Bar despite claiming to be an attorney, is forbidden
by his administrator certificate to handle cash, was named as a
"target subject" in an FBI wiretap application (2016), and owes
$14,500 in campaign finance fines.

Judge Miller serves in the Criminal Division of the Third
Circuit Court. She is a criminal division judge who helped a
man with 8 felony convictions for financial fraud found a
charter school whose stated mission is to prepare students for
careers in "law and public service."

Judge Miller previously served on the 36th District Court from
April 2006 through August 2019 — the same court where Judge
Tenisha Yancey now sits. Judge Yancey separately serves as
Board Chair of MacDowell Preparatory Academy, Banks' other
school.

Judge Miller's campaign endorsers (keepjudgemiller.com) include
Wayne County Executive Warren Evans and Wayne County Treasurer
Eric Sabree — the same officials who endorsed Banks' 2022
Wayne County Commission bid (per Elrick, Detroit Free Press,
July 3, 2022).

On August 29, 2026, Banks told me "I'm a lawyer" at PCA — the
school Judge Miller helped found — while denying a father's
authorized representative access to the school. This is a
felony under MCL 450.681.

This conduct raises concerns under:

- Canon 2: "A judge should avoid impropriety and the appearance
  of impropriety in all activities." A criminal division judge
  lending her name and judicial credibility to a charter school
  development team led by a man with 8 felony convictions
  creates the appearance of impropriety.

- Canon 4: A judge's extrajudicial activities shall not cast
  reasonable doubt on the judge's capacity to act impartially.
  Judge Miller's affiliation with Banks' school raises
  questions about her impartiality in matters involving Banks,
  charter schools, or connected parties.

Judge Miller is currently running for re-election to the Third
Circuit Court in the November 3, 2026 general election.
