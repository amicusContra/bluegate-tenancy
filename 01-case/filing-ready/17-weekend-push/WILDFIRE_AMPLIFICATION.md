# WILDFIRE — Human Amplification Layer

**Not government. Not press. PEOPLE.**

The storm tags actors and agencies. The wildfire puts the data
in front of communities who are already pissed, already organized,
already loud. They don't need instructions. They need fuel.

**Principle:** Diffraction of power through public scrutiny.
Concentrated power (Dykema, Banks, Barrett, Ellison) gets broken
up when multiple independent communities see it from their own
angle simultaneously. The harmed get cover because no single
retaliatory vector works when 15 groups are watching from 15
directions.

**Format:** Each group gets ONE link. The page does the work.
No asks. No coordination. Just: "here's the data, you'll know
what to do."

# THE DIFFRACTION PRINCIPLE

Each group sees the data from their own angle:

```
482Forward sees ──── charter extraction ──── 3% math
MMOP sees ───────── dark money laundering ── name changes
IMI7 sees ───────── Barrett's donor ──────── Dykema connection
MiCOG sees ──────── FOIA weaponized ──────── honeypot threat
Common Defense sees ─ veteran hypocrisy ──── pusillanimous
Detroit Action sees ── institutional capture ── board composition
RITDH sees ──────── their candidate smeared ── who paid for it
Canfield Consortium ── their neighborhood ──── who runs the school
```

No group needs to coordinate with any other. Each pulls the
thread from their own direction. The concentrated power of the
network can't retaliate against all of them simultaneously
because they're independent, they have different funding sources,
different memberships, and different institutional protections.

The harmed get cover through diffraction. When 15 groups are
looking at the same network from 15 angles, no single actor can
suppress all 15 without drawing more attention.

The data is the fuel. The communities are the fire.
We just open the window.
