## DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Each email follows these rules:

1. **Open with their node.** First line identifies THEM.
   They need to know this is about THEM, not a mass blast.

2. **Reveal one connection they thought was private.** The SID
   number. The name-change timeline. The Dykema employment.
   The "your own employee." Something that says: we mapped you.

3. **Show adjacent nodes.** Not the full graph — just enough
   to see that OTHERS are mapped too. "Your fellow board
   member." "The journalist it defeated." "Your colleague."

4. **End with a link.** The published page shows more than
   the email. The email is the hook. The page is the reveal.
   Every click deepens the "oh shit" moment.

5. **No demands. No threats. No legal posturing.** Just data.
   The data IS the threat. The organization IS the demand.
   The publication IS the consequence.

6. **Last line: eco.primal@pm.me.** No name. No signature.
   No credentials. Just data and a return address.
   They can't discredit the messenger because there is
   no messenger. There's just a mirror.
