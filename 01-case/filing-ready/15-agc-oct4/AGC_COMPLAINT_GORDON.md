## MRPC VIOLATIONS ALLEGED

### MRPC 8.4(b) — Criminal Act Reflecting on Fitness

"It is professional misconduct for a lawyer to commit a criminal act
that reflects adversely on the lawyer's honesty, trustworthiness, or
fitness as a lawyer in other respects."

If the FBI/IRS/DOL investigation results in charges or findings against
Mr. Gordon, his conduct in the UAW bribery matter would constitute a
criminal act reflecting on his fitness. Even absent charges, the
documented investigation — involving over $3 million in payments to
vendors including his company — raises questions the AGC should examine.

### MRPC 8.4(c) — Dishonesty, Fraud, Deceit, or Misrepresentation

The dissolve-and-reform pattern (Custom Promotions Inc → Custom
Promotions LLC, same name, 3-month gap) may constitute conduct
involving deceit if done to evade liabilities or investigation.

### MRPC 8.4(d) — Conduct Prejudicial to the Administration of Justice

Operating a law firm from the same address as a federally investigated
political promotions company prejudices the administration of justice
by commingling professional legal services with an enterprise under
criminal investigation.
