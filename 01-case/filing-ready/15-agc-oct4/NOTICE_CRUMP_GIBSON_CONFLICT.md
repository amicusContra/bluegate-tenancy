## THE CONFLICT MS. CRUMP-GIBSON NOW FACES

1. **Attorney-in-fact vs. defense counsel:** As attorney-in-fact for Purpose Foundation, she had knowledge of and responsibility for the entity's formation and filings. As defense counsel, she is now defending the same entity structure she helped create. She cannot independently evaluate the §5.2 false representation if her own authority contributed to the entity's legal framework.

2. **Witness potential:** If the Purpose Foundation's formation, LARA filings, or §5.2 representation become subjects of discovery or trial, Ms. Crump-Gibson may be a fact witness regarding the entity she helped maintain. MRPC 3.7 generally prohibits a lawyer from acting as advocate at trial where the lawyer is likely to be a necessary witness.

3. **Scope limitation as evidence of awareness:** On October 1, 2026, at 2:28 PM, Ms. Crump-Gibson sent a written communication stating she is "NOT retained for law enforcement matters, police reports, or PPO proceedings." This scope limitation suggests she recognized exposure in certain areas — but the structural conflict runs deeper than the areas she carved out.
