## SUMMARY OF COMPLAINT

Todd Russell Perkins served as attorney for Brian Roderick Banks (SID
2029469K, nine-time convicted felon, habitual offender fourth offense)
and simultaneously represented targets of Operation Northern Hook —
an ongoing FBI investigation into corruption within Detroit city
government and the Detroit Police Department.

Mr. Perkins then ran for Mayor of Detroit in 2025, accepting campaign
contributions from convicted bribery defendant Gasper Fiore ($500) and
former DPSCD board member Sherry Gay-Dagnogo ($750) — both members of
the Banks political network. He finished fourth in the primary.

Mr. Perkins currently serves as City Attorney for Inkster, Michigan,
while maintaining the relationships described below.

## MRPC VIOLATIONS ALLEGED

### MRPC 8.4(d) — Conduct Prejudicial to the Administration of Justice

Accepting campaign contributions from a convicted bribery defendant
(Fiore) while serving as a public law officer (Inkster City Attorney)
and maintaining professional relationships with subjects of an ongoing
FBI investigation (Operation Northern Hook) prejudices the
administration of justice.

### MRPC 1.7 — Conflict of Interest (Concurrent Clients)

Representing both Brian Banks AND Carol Banks (siblings) AND Operation
Northern Hook targets creates concurrent conflicts where the interests
of one client may be materially adverse to another. Brian Banks was
recorded on the FBI wiretap of Gasper Fiore — the same Fiore who
donated to Perkins's campaign.
