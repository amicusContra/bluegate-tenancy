## SUMMARY OF COMPLAINT

Verkeydia Hall is a licensed Michigan attorney serving on the Board of Directors of MacDowell Preparatory Academy — a charter school managed by Brian Roderick Banks, a nine-time convicted felon classified as a habitual offender, fourth offense.

As a licensed attorney serving in a board governance role, Ms. Hall has heightened professional obligations. The MacDowell board accepted and maintained a Management Agreement containing a materially false representation (§5.2 "No Related Parties") with Banks's single-member LLC — Purpose Group LLC. As a licensed attorney, Ms. Hall's acceptance of this false representation raises questions about her professional conduct.

This complaint alleges violations of MRPC 8.4(c) (Conduct Involving Dishonesty) and MRPC 8.4(d) (Conduct Prejudicial to the Administration of Justice).

## FACTUAL BASIS

### 1. MacDowell Board Membership

Ms. Hall serves on the Board of Directors of MacDowell Preparatory Academy, Entity #82747, a charter school authorized by DPSCD. The school is managed by Purpose Group LLC — Banks's single-member company — under a Management Agreement.

### 2. The §5.2 False Representation

Section 5.2 of the Management Agreement between Purpose Group LLC and MacDowell Preparatory Academy contains a "No Related Parties" representation. This representation is false:

- Banks is the sole member of Purpose Group LLC (the management company)
- Banks is a co-officer (with Holland) of Purpose Foundation (the 501(c)(3))
- Holland appears in all four officer positions of Purpose Foundation (President, Vice President, Secretary, Treasurer)
- Holland has six felony convictions and five personal bankruptcies
- Purpose Group LLC receives 72.67% of MacDowell's revenue ($4,285,201 in FY2025)

The related-party relationship between Purpose Group LLC and Purpose Foundation is documented in LARA filings, MDOC records, and FY2025 audit data. It is not hidden — it is publicly verifiable.

### 3. Board Oversight Failure

As a board member, Ms. Hall has a fiduciary duty to the school entity. As a licensed attorney, she has a heightened ability to identify false representations in contracts the board oversees. The §5.2 representation is not ambiguous — it is false on its face given the overlapping officers between the management company and the school entity.

The MacDowell board accepted a contract where:
- 72.67% of revenue flows to one man's private LLC
- The "No Related Parties" clause is false
- The school produces 3% math proficiency ($15,217/student)
- $0 staff salaries are reported in NCC filings

### 4. Responsibility

A board member who is not a lawyer may claim ignorance of contract terms. A board member who is a licensed attorney cannot. Ms. Hall had the professional training to identify the false §5.2 representation, and a fiduciary duty to act on it.

## MRPC VIOLATIONS ALLEGED

### MRPC 8.4(c) — Dishonesty, Fraud, Deceit, or Misrepresentation
"It is professional misconduct for a lawyer to engage in conduct involving dishonesty, fraud, deceit, or misrepresentation."

Ms. Hall's continued participation in board governance over a contract containing a materially false representation — without objection or corrective action — constitutes participation in a misrepresentation.

### MRPC 8.4(d) — Conduct Prejudicial to the Administration of Justice
"It is professional misconduct for a lawyer to engage in conduct that is prejudicial to the administration of justice."

The false §5.2 representation enables the self-dealing that diverts public education funds. Ms. Hall's failure to exercise the professional judgment expected of a licensed attorney serving on a board contributes to the perpetuation of this scheme.
