conduct under the MRPC, which apply to Judge Miller as a bar member

## MRPC VIOLATIONS ALLEGED

### MRPC 8.4(d) — Conduct Prejudicial to the Administration of Justice

"It is professional misconduct for a lawyer to engage in conduct that
is prejudicial to the administration of justice."

A bar member who serves as Board President of a school operated by a
nine-time convicted felon — whose network funds her re-election
campaign through interconnected endorsement slates — engages in conduct
that prejudices the administration of justice. The public cannot have
confidence in the legal system when a judge/attorney lends institutional
credibility to an enterprise operated by a habitual offender.

Judge Miller's bar license is part of what makes her board presidency
valuable to Banks — it is a professional credential being deployed
in service of an enterprise that the AG's office itself opposed.

### MRPC 8.4(c) — Dishonesty, Fraud, Deceit, or Misrepresentation

"It is professional misconduct for a lawyer to engage in conduct
involving dishonesty, fraud, deceit, or misrepresentation."

By serving as Board President while accepting endorsements from the
Banks network's endorsement infrastructure, Judge Miller participates
in a system where her judicial/professional credentials create an
appearance of independence and legitimacy that does not exist. The
public is misled into believing that PCA has independent board
governance when the Board President, the Secretary, and a Director
are all connected through the same political network.
