### Legal Basis

Under Michigan law, parties who destroy or conceal evidence after
receiving notice of its relevance to pending litigation may be
subject to:

- **Adverse inference instructions** — the Court may instruct the
  jury that destroyed evidence was unfavorable to the spoliating
  party. *Brenner v Kolk*, 226 Mich App 149, 161 (1997).

- **Sanctions under MCR 2.313(B)** — including dismissal of claims
  or defenses, preclusion of evidence, striking of pleadings, or
  default judgment.

- **Independent tort of spoliation** — intentional destruction of
  evidence to disrupt pending litigation.
