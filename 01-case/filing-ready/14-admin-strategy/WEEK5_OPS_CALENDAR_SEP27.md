## THE LOGIC

```
SPOLIATION NOTICES (this week)     "Preserve everything."
         │
         ▼
DEMAND LETTERS (this week)         "Pay and cooperate, or face judgment."
         │                          (Also serves as MCR 2.603(A)(3) notice)
         ▼
RFA DEEMED ADMITTED (Oct 10)       Facts established automatically.
         │
         ▼
DEFAULT MOTION (Oct 6-10)          "They didn't answer. Facts are admitted.
         │                          Enter judgment."
         ▼
SUBPOENA BLITZ (Oct 13+)          "Now produce the records."
                                    (Spoliation notice = they can't destroy
                                     what was already demanded preserved)
```

**The spoliation wave and the subpoena blitz are two jaws of the same trap.**
- Spoliation says "preserve" → creates the duty
- Subpoena says "produce" → enforces the duty
- Anyone who destroyed records between notice and subpoena → adverse inference + contempt + federal obstruction (18 USC § 1519)
