TRACK 1 — DaSean v. Wayne County et al. (§ 1983 / Parental Rights)
================================================================

THEORY: Wayne County and DPSCD, through their agents and
officers, deprived DaSean Mitchell of his constitutionally
protected parental rights by (a) allowing Shayla Smith to
exercise sole custody for 9 years without a court order,
(b) authorizing a charter school operated by a convicted
felon to enroll DaSean's children without parental consent,
and (c) failing to act on complaints about the school.

PARTIES:
  Plaintiff: DaSean Mitchell
  Representative: Kevin Mok (authorized)

  Defendants:
  1. Wayne County (via FOC / Family Court Division)
     - Allowed Shayla to maintain de facto sole custody for
       9 years without ever establishing legal custody
     - FOC has mechanisms to investigate — never triggered
     - DaSean was invisible to the system because no one
       asked where the father was

  2. Detroit Public Schools Community District (DPSCD)
     - Authorized PCA's charter (Jul 2025, 3-year contract)
     - Failed to conduct adequate background check on Banks
       (9 felony convictions discoverable through ICHAT)
     - Board members with conflicts of interest (Miller as
       PCA Board Chair) voted on charter authorization
     - Failed to act on complaints filed Aug 31, 2026

  3. Michigan Department of Education (MDE)
     - Investigated Banks in Feb 2022 for lacking credentials
     - Gave school a loophole ("get a permit") instead of
       requiring immediate removal
     - Banks never got a valid permit — app #590606 on HOLD
     - MDE issued admin certificate Sep 2, 2022 (3 days
       AFTER school year started, 7 months after investigation)
     - School "self-managed" 4 months after investigation
     - MDE had the information and chose not to act

  4. Brian Roderick Banks (individual capacity)
     - Enrolled DaSean's daughters without father's consent
     - Denied DaSean access to school
     - Claimed to be an attorney (UPL)
     - Filed false police report against Kevin (Sep 9)

  5. Shayla M. Smith (individual capacity)
     - Exercised sole custody without court order for 9 years
     - Enrolled daughters at PCA without DaSean's consent
     - Coordinated retaliatory PPO + emergency custody filing
       to block DaSean and Kevin from accessing children

CLAIMS:
  A. 42 U.S.C. § 1983 — Deprivation of rights under color of law
     - 14th Amendment substantive due process: parental rights
       are a fundamental liberty interest (Troxel v. Granville,
       530 U.S. 57 (2000); Stanley v. Illinois, 405 U.S. 645
       (1972))
     - State actors (Wayne County, DPSCD, MDE) facilitated the
       deprivation by inaction and by affirmative authorization
       of a school run by a convicted felon

  B. 42 U.S.C. § 1985(3) — Conspiracy to deprive rights
     - Banks + Shayla + institutional actors conspired to deny
       DaSean access to his children and their education

  C. Monell v. Department of Social Services (1978)
     - Municipal liability: Wayne County's customs and policies
       (or lack thereof) caused the deprivation
     - DPSCD's failure to screen charter operators = policy
     - MDE's failure to enforce credentialing = policy

  D. State claims:
     - Negligent supervision (DPSCD over PCA charter)
     - Failure to protect children (MDE over credentialing)
     - Parental interference (Shayla, Banks)

VENUE: U.S. District Court, Eastern District of Michigan
  (federal § 1983 claims) OR
  Michigan Court of Claims (state institutional claims)

  Strategy: File in federal court for § 1983 against the
  institutions. State claims against individuals stay in
  Ingham County circuit court (already filed).

EVIDENCE ALREADY IN HAND:
