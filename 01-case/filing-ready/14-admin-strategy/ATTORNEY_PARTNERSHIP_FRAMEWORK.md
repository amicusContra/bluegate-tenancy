### Role 3: Expert Witness (Kevin's cases, potentially others)

**Potential expert designations:**

Kevin may qualify as an expert witness in specific areas
based on his training, experience, and demonstrated

1. **Public Records Research / OSINT Methodology**
   - Demonstrated proficiency across 22+ government databases
   - Systematic approach to evidence collection
   - Documented methodology (sporeprint.primals.eco)
   - Reproducible results — anyone can follow the same steps

2. **Data Analysis and Network Mapping**
   - MSU advanced degree in research methodology
   - Network graph (43 nodes, 57 edges) built from

   - Statistical and analytical training

3. **Digital Forensics and Evidence Architecture**
   - Git-provenance evidence archiving
   - BLAKE3 content hashing
   - Machine-readable evidence endpoints
   - Chain of custody documentation

**MRE 702 standard:** Expert testimony is admissible if:
(a) scientific, technical, or specialized knowledge will
help the trier of fact, (b) the witness is qualified by
knowledge, skill, experience, training, or education, (c)
the testimony is based on sufficient facts or data, (d) the
testimony is the product of reliable principles and methods,
and (e) the witness has reliably applied the principles and
methods to the facts of the case.

**Kevin meets all five prongs** for public records research
and data analysis. He doesn't need a law degree — he needs
demonstrated expertise in the methodology he used. His
portfolio at primals.eco IS the demonstration.

**How expert testimony works with the attorney:**

- Attorney files notice of expert testimony (MCR 2.302(A))
- Attorney qualifies Kevin as expert on the stand
- Kevin testifies to methodology, conclusions, and opinions
- Expert witnesses can give OPINIONS — fact witnesses cannot
- Kevin can say: "Based on my analysis of 9 years of
  financial records, the entity structure appears designed
  to extract public funds through a single management
  company" — a fact witness can't say that, an expert can

## THE FLOW — ONE HEARING, START TO FINISH

### Before (Kevin leads)
1. Kevin identifies the issue from the evidence archive
2. Kevin drafts the substance: facts, exhibits, witnesses
3. Kevin and attorney discuss strategy
4. Attorney drafts the legal framework
5. Attorney files the motion/notice
6. Kevin prepares exhibits (organized, labeled, copied x4)
7. Kevin briefs the attorney on likely opposing arguments
   (he already knows what they'll say because he's mapped
   the network's playbook)

### During (Kevin + Attorney together)
1. Attorney argues to the court
2. Kevin sits at counsel table, passes notes
3. When Kevin is called as a witness:
   - Attorney examines Kevin (direct)
   - Kevin presents his evidence methodically
   - Opposing counsel cross-examines
   - Kevin answers from personal knowledge and (if
     qualified as expert) from analysis
4. Kevin returns to counsel table after testimony
5. Kevin continues feeding notes to attorney for remaining
   witnesses and argument

### After (Kevin leads)
1. Kevin updates the evidence archive with hearing results
2. Kevin identifies new leads from testimony
3. Kevin drafts follow-up FOIA requests
4. Kevin and attorney discuss next moves
5. Attorney files any post-hearing motions
6. Kevin resumes the walk (STRIDe — pursuit predation pace)
