I respectfully submit this Request for Investigation against three members

I. THE SUBJECT OF THEIR ENABLING: BRIAN RODERICK BANKS

Brian Roderick Banks is the founder and operator of Purpose Charter Academy
(PCA), a Detroit Public Schools Community District (DPSCD)-authorized charter
school located at 10101 E. Canfield Street, Detroit, Michigan 48214. The
school is managed by "The Purpose Group," Mr. Banks' entity. Public materials
identify him as "Brian Banks, J.D., Ph.D."

Legal Credentials and Criminal History:

    - J.D., Michigan State University College of Law (2010)
    - Never admitted to the State Bar of Michigan; no P-number
    - Felony conviction for embezzlement — Case No. 14-006194-FH, Wayne
      County Circuit Court (convicted 2016)
    - Publicly uses "J.D., Ph.D." and attorney-adjacent titles on school
      and business materials

A search of the Michigan State Bar member directory confirms that Brian Banks
is not licensed to practice law in Michigan.

Prior Notice and Repeated Conduct:

On or about August 31, 2026, a complaint regarding Mr. Banks' unauthorized
practice of law was filed with the State Bar of Michigan. Approximately one
week before September 9, 2026, Mr. Banks was served with an Ingham County
civil suit that included the State Bar complaint receipt as an exhibit. He
was therefore on notice that his unauthorized practice of law was the subject
of a formal complaint. Despite that notice, Mr. Banks again represented
himself as an attorney on September 9, 2026.

September 9, 2026 — Detroit Police Department Incident:

On September 9, 2026, at Purpose Charter Academy, Mr. Banks told Detroit
Police Department officers that he was "an attorney" during a custodial
dispute involving DaSean Mitchell, the biological father of children enrolled
at the school. Mr. Banks used that false claim to prevent Mr. Mitchell from
accessing his children on school property. Mr. Banks did not confirm that
the children were at the school. Police escorted Mr. Mitchell off the property
based in part on Mr. Banks' false representation of attorney status.

II. RESPONDENT I: HON. CYLENTHIA LATOYE MILLER

Identification:

    Name:         Hon. Cylenthia LaToye Miller
    Position:     Judge, 3rd Circuit Court of Michigan
    Bar Status:   Member, State Bar of Michigan
    PCA Role:     Board Chair, Purpose Charter Academy

Conduct at Issue:

Judge Miller serves as Board Chair of Purpose Charter Academy — the governing
leader of the school founded and managed by Brian Banks. This is not a passive
or social association. As Board Chair, she presides over board governance,
oversees school operations, and lends her judicial title and institutional
authority to an enterprise operated by a person who is not licensed to practice
law.

Judge Miller cannot plausibly claim ignorance:

    - Mr. Banks was convicted of embezzlement in Wayne County — the same
      county in which Judge Miller sits as a circuit judge.
    - Judge Miller is a licensed attorney and judge. Bar admission status
      can be verified through ordinary professional means.
    - Mr. Banks publicly uses "J.D." and attorney-adjacent titles on school
      materials.
    - On September 9, 2026, Mr. Banks committed unauthorized practice of law
      on PCA's campus — the institution Judge Miller chairs.

Despite these facts, Judge Miller has not reported Mr. Banks' unauthorized
practice of law to the State Bar. Instead, she has continued to serve as
Board Chair of the school where that misconduct occurred.

Rules Implicated:

MRPC 8.3(a) — Reporting Professional Misconduct. A lawyer who knows that
another lawyer has committed a violation of the Rules of Professional Conduct
that raises a substantial question as to that lawyer's honesty, trustworthiness,
or fitness as a lawyer in other respects, shall inform the appropriate
professional authority. Judge Miller knew or should have known that Mr. Banks
was not admitted to the bar and was holding himself out as an attorney. She
failed to report.

MRPC 8.4(a) — Violation of Rules Through Another. It is professional
misconduct for a lawyer to violate or attempt to violate the Rules of
Professional Conduct, knowingly assist or induce another to do so, or do so
through the acts of another. By chairing the board of a school operated by
an unlicensed person committing unauthorized practice of law on campus,
Judge Miller lent her credentials and governance authority to Mr. Banks'
misconduct.


III. RESPONDENT II: MR. LAMAR MORELAND

Identification:

    Name:         Mr. Lamar Moreland
    Position:     Assistant Attorney General, State of Michigan
    Bar Status:   Member, State Bar of Michigan
    PCA Role:     Board Vice Chair, Purpose Charter Academy

Conduct at Issue:

Mr. Moreland is an Assistant Attorney General — a state prosecutor whose
professional duty is to enforce the law and uphold public integrity. He
simultaneously serves as Board Vice Chair of Purpose Charter Academy, a school
founded and operated by a convicted felon who is not licensed to practice law
and who commits unauthorized practice of law on the school's campus.

Mr. Moreland is not a private citizen volunteering for a community board. He
is a state law enforcement attorney lending the title and implied authority
of the Michigan Attorney General's office to the governance of an institution
run by a person committing ongoing unauthorized practice of law.

On September 9, 2026, Mr. Banks told Detroit Police officers he was "an
attorney" at PCA — the same institution where Mr. Moreland serves as Vice
Chair. Mr. Moreland either knew of Mr. Banks' unauthorized practice and
failed to act, or failed to exercise the diligence expected of a prosecutor
and bar member governing such an institution.

Rules Implicated:

MRPC 8.3(a) — Reporting Professional Misconduct. Mr. Moreland, as a licensed
attorney and state prosecutor, had a duty to report Mr. Banks' unauthorized
practice of law once he knew or reasonably should have known of it. He did not.

MRPC 8.4(a) — Violation of Rules Through Another. By serving as Vice Chair of
PCA's board while Mr. Banks operated the school and committed unauthorized
practice of law on campus, Mr. Moreland assisted and lent institutional
credibility to that misconduct.
