Wayne County Register of Deeds records establish that on June 24,

UNDISCLOSED SECURED CLAIM

On the same date (June 24, 2025), Leon A. Smith and Marquita L.
Smith jointly executed a mortgage on the Dresden property in favor
of Lake Trust Credit Union in the amount of $68,000.00. This
mortgage was recorded with the Wayne County Register of Deeds.

Ally Financial (for a vehicle). Lake Trust Credit Union does not

If Ms. Smith is a co-borrower on a $68,000 secured mortgage, this
obligation should be disclosed as a secured claim on Schedule D,

matrix to receive notice of the bankruptcy filing.
