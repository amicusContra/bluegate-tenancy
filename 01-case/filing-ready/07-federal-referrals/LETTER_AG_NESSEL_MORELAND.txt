THE FACTS

Brian Roderick Banks is the founder and operator of Purpose Charter Academy.
He holds a J.D. from Michigan State University College of Law (2010) but has
never been admitted to the Michigan State Bar. He was convicted of felony
embezzlement in Wayne County (Case No. 14-006194-FH, convicted 2016). He
publicly uses "J.D., Ph.D." after his name.

On August 31, 2026, a complaint regarding Mr. Banks' unauthorized practice of
law was filed with the State Bar of Michigan. Approximately one week before
September 9, 2026, Mr. Banks was served with an Ingham County civil suit that
included the State Bar complaint receipt as an exhibit. He was on notice of
the complaint.

On September 9, 2026, at Purpose Charter Academy, Mr. Banks told Detroit Police
Department officers that he was "an attorney" during a custodial dispute
involving Mr. Mitchell, the biological father of children enrolled at the
school. Mr. Banks used that false claim to prevent Mr. Mitchell from accessing
his children on school property. Mr. Banks did not confirm that the children
were at the school. Police escorted Mr. Mitchell off the property based in
part on Mr. Banks' false representation.

    Police Report:      DPD Report #26-0909-0176
    Reporting Officer:   Officer M. Maciejewski #2726

Public sources, including https://www.purposecharteracademy.com/boardofdirectors,
identify Mr. Moreland as Board Vice Chair of Purpose Charter Academy. Hon.
Cylenthia LaToye Miller, a sitting 3rd Circuit Court judge, serves as Board
Chair. Mr. Moreland therefore holds a senior governance role at the school
where this incident occurred.


WHY I AM WRITING

This is the equivalent of a hospital board chaired by a licensed physician and
a state health official, allowing an unlicensed person with a medical degree
but no license — and a prior conviction for misconduct — to perform
examinations on patients while wearing a lab coat, under the implied authority
of the board's credentials. No medical board would tolerate this.

An Assistant Attorney General of the State of Michigan serves as Vice Chair of
the board governing an institution whose operator — a convicted felon not
licensed to practice law — falsely claimed attorney status to law enforcement
on school property. Mr. Moreland either knew or should have known that Mr.
Banks was not admitted to the bar. The September 9 incident occurred at the
school Mr. Moreland helps govern.

I have filed a separate Request for Investigation with the Attorney Grievance
Commission regarding Mr. Moreland's obligations under the Michigan Rules of
Professional Conduct, including MRPC 8.3(a) and 8.4(a). I am not writing to
duplicate that process or to request any specific action from your office. I
believe the Attorney General should be independently aware that one of your
Assistant Attorneys General occupies this governance role at PCA.

If your office determines that internal review is appropriate, I respectfully
suggest that the Professional Responsibility, Education, and Policy Board
(PREP Board) may be the appropriate body to consider whether Mr. Moreland's
board service raises institutional or ethical concerns warranting attention.


I am prepared to provide additional documentation upon request, including a
