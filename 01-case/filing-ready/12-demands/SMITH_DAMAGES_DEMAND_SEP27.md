## ⚠️ JOINT AND SEVERAL LIABILITY — ALL OR NONE

**THIS OFFER REQUIRES ALL FOUR DEFENDANTS TO ACCEPT.**

Under MCL 600.2910 (civil conspiracy) and the joint and several
liability doctrine, each Smith defendant is liable for the **full
amount** of all damages caused by the conspiracy — not just their
individual share. This means:

- **Any one defendant** can be held liable for the entire $385,000
  in compensatory damages (plus statutory claims and punitive damages)

  defendants remain jointly and severally liable** for the conspiracy
  claims alongside the holdout
- **There are no individual deals.** You settle as a family or you
  go to court as a family
- A partial settlement where some defendants cooperate and others
  don't **does not release anyone** from joint liability on the
  conspiracy claims

**Why it works this way:** You acted as a unit — multi-household
address fraud, coordinated service evasion, the September 2 assault
involved multiple family members, the school enrollment was a family
decision. The conspiracy was a family conspiracy. The resolution is
a family resolution.
