## II. LEGAL BASIS

Under **MCR 2.302(B)(1)**, parties may obtain discovery regarding any
matter not privileged that is relevant to the subject matter of the
pending action. All documents requested above are directly relevant
to the custody, parenting time, and child support issues before this
Court.

Under **MCL 722.23** (Best Interest Factors), the Court must evaluate
both parents' capacity and disposition to provide for the children.
Defendant cannot meaningfully participate in this evaluation without
access to the children's records.

As the legal father — established by Affidavit of Parentage under
**MCL 722.1003** and confirmed by Plaintiff's own sworn filings —
Defendant has the legal right to access all educational, medical,
and vital records concerning his children.
