BACKGROUND

On September 2, 2026, Defendant Brian Roderick Banks was
served with the original Complaint in Case No. 2026-4349-CZ
at his residence, 1968 Severn Rd, Grosse Pointe Woods, MI.

Within hours of being served, Banks telephoned the father
of the minor children enrolled at Purpose Charter Academy.

BANKS' STATEMENTS (as relayed by the children's father)

1. Banks stated that Kevin Mok was "out of line" and was
   "stalking him and the school."

2. Banks stated that "the mother is right" and that Kevin
   Mok was "a problem."

3. Banks again claimed to be an attorney. This is the second
   documented instance:
       - First:  August 29, 2026, in person at PCA
       - Second: September 2, 2026, by phone after service

4. Banks stated he had "judges and law enforcement in his
   interactions."

SIGNIFICANCE

A. Banks repeated his claim of attorney status AFTER being
   served with a complaint specifically alleging Unauthorized
   Practice of Law (Count I).

B. Banks contacted a parent to discourage participation in
   litigation, characterizing lawful process service and
   public records research as "stalking."

C. Banks invoked "judges and law enforcement" connections
   in conversation with a parent — a statement reasonably
   interpreted as intimidation.

D. Banks has documented relationships with sitting judges:
   Judge Cylenthia Miller (PCA development team member)
   and Judge Tenisha R. Yancey (MacDowell Board Chair, who
   paid Banks' PAC 44.9% of her 2022 campaign expenditures).

THE FATHER'S RESPONSE

The father informed Kevin Mok of the call and stated he had
applied to enroll his children at a different school. The
father independently decided to remove his children from PCA.
