### Why a microbiologist is good at this

COIST training is OSINT training. You take publicly available
information — maps, imagery, reports, intercepted communications —
and you build a picture of the opposition's behavior, resources,
and network. You brief it. You get corrected. You refine it.

Microbiology is contamination identification. You take a sample,
culture it, identify what's growing, trace the contamination
source, and determine how it spread. The methodology is the same
whether the sample is a bioreactor or a school board.

Data science is pattern recognition at scale. You take a dataset,
clean it, model it, validate against known baselines, and
document discrepancies. Banks' public record is the dataset. His
claims are the model. The facts are the baseline. The
discrepancies are the findings.

I am not a prompt engineer using AI to generate text. I am a
scientist using AI to accelerate the same analytical work I would
do manually — reading financial statements, cross-referencing
public records, mapping network connections, and verifying claims
against primary sources. The AI processes data faster. The
analysis is mine.
