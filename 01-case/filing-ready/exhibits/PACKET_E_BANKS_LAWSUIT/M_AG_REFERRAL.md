BANKS_OSINT_BRIEF for the complete pattern with citations).

- "Advise, counsel, and develop strategic plans... including

This constitutes ongoing UPL under MCL 450.681 — both through
direct false claims and through public advertisement of legal
services by an unlicensed individual.

MCL 450.681 classifies UPL as a felony punishable by up to 5 years
imprisonment. With Banks' 8 prior felony convictions, the habitual
offender statute (MCL 769.12) permits enhanced sentencing.

I have filed a concurrent complaint with the State Bar of Michigan
UPL Department on August 31, 2026.
