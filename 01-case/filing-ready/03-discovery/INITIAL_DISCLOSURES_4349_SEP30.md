### (a) Factual Basis for Claims

Plaintiff's claims arise from a coordinated enterprise
operated by Defendants Banks and Holland through a network of
entities (Purpose Group LLC, Purpose Charter Academy, Purpose
Foundation) used to: deny parental access to a legal father's
children; file false police reports against Plaintiff; claim
false legal authority to obstruct Plaintiff's authorized role;
commit FERPA violations; defame Plaintiff and Plaintiff's
family; and conceal assets and financial misconduct through
overlapping entity structures, a revocable living trust, and
joint property — all of which Banks and Holland control.

The factual basis is set forth in the First Amended Complaint
and proposed Second Amended Complaint.

### (b) Legal Theories

Plaintiff anticipates the following claims against Defendants
in this action (claims to be refined in the operative
pleading):

- False police report / abuse of process (Banks — DPD
  Report #26-0909-0176)
- Unauthorized practice of law / fraud (Banks — "I am a
  lawyer" to police, legal advice to Smith family)
- FERPA violations (Banks, PCA — denial of parental access
  to enrollment records for a legal father's children)

  Dr. Mok, in written communications; false statements
  about Plaintiff's conduct)
- Tortious interference with parental relationship (Banks,
  PCA — blocking authorized representative's access)
- Civil conspiracy (Banks + Holland + entities acting as
  unified enterprise)
- Fraud / constructive fraud (Banks, Holland — entity
  structures used to shield assets; Management Agreement
  Section 5.2 false "no related parties" representation)
- Piercing the entity veil / alter ego (Purpose Group LLC
  is Banks's alter ego — sole member, commingled operations)
- Aiding and abetting concealment of assets (Holland —
  successor trustee, BK concealment pattern)
