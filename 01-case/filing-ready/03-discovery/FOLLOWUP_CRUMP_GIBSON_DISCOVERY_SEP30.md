## INTERNAL NOTES

### What this does

Banks has been ignoring Kevin's demands for a month. Now those
demands go to an actual attorney who:

1. **Cannot ignore discovery.** MRPC 3.4(d) requires reasonably
   diligent effort to comply with discovery requests. An attorney
   who ignores a proper demand faces sanctions.

2. **Cannot help the client hide evidence.** MRPC 3.4(a) prohibits
   unlawfully obstructing access to evidence. If Banks told the
   court he has video and affidavits, his lawyer has to produce
   them or explain why not.

3. **Cannot make false statements to the court.** MRPC 3.3(a)(1).
   If the video and affidavits don't exist, Crump-Gibson can't
   let Banks's prior claim stand uncorrected.

4. **Has preservation obligations.** The lawyer now personally
   knows about the preservation duty. If evidence disappears
   AFTER the lawyer was notified, that's sanctionable for the
   attorney personally.
