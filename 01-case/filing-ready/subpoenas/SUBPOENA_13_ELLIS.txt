                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

You are listed as CFO of Purpose Foundation, a 501(c)(3)
entity registered at Defendant Banks' personal residence.
You are also a named defendant in this action.

LARA records show no CPA license in Michigan under your
name. Plaintiff seeks to understand your qualifications
to serve as CFO of a tax-exempt foundation and whether

proper stewardship of charitable assets.

Purpose Foundation and Purpose Group Michigan LLC are
both registered at 1968 Severn Rd -- Banks' personal
address. The commingling of a personal residence with
multiple corporate entities raises questions about
proper separation of personal and organizational
finances.
