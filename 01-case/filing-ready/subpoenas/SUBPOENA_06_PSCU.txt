                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

This Subpoena seeks records relevant to a pattern of
coordinated financial transactions connecting Defendant
Brian Roderick Banks to a network of judicial officers
and family members.

Defendant Banks has obtained ALL of his residential
mortgages, refinances, and lines of credit exclusively
through PSCU -- an unusual pattern for a consumer with
two federal bankruptcies and four state tax liens.

Judge Tenisha R. Yancey, who paid Defendant Banks
$15,750 from her campaign fund (44.9% of total
expenditures in the 2022 cycle) and chairs the board
of Defendant's charter school (MacDowell Preparatory
Academy), obtained her condominium mortgage from the
same credit union -- PSCU.

Plaintiff seeks to determine whether:
  (a) An employee of PSCU facilitated these transactions
      through an insider relationship;
  (b) Shayla Smith -- who is known to have been employed
      at Lake Trust Credit Union, another Michigan credit
      union, where she facilitated an insider mortgage
      for her own parents -- is or has been employed at
      PSCU;
  (c) The referral path from Banks to PSCU involved any
      judicial officers or their staff;
  (d) NCUA Regulation O disclosures were properly filed.

This Subpoena is issued pursuant to MCR 2.305 by a party
to a pending civil action.
