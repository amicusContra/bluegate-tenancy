                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

The Guidance Center is a major behavioral health provider
in Wayne County, offering court-ordered family services,
custody evaluations, and child welfare programs. Tunezha
Crowder, LCSW, is or was employed there in a role
involving children and family services.

This Subpoena seeks institutional records, not individual
