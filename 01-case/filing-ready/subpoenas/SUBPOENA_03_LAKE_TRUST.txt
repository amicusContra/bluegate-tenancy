                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

This Subpoena seeks records relevant to a pattern of
financial fraud connected to the defendants in this action.

Shayla Smith is an employee of Lake Trust Credit Union. Her
parents, Leon A. Smith and Marquita L. Smith, obtained a
mortgage from Lake Trust on the Dresden property. This
mortgage was taken on a property that had been free and
clear of encumbrances for 11 years, approximately six (6)
days before Marquita L. Smith filed for bankruptcy.

Federal regulations (NCUA Regulation O, 12 CFR 701.21)
require credit unions to identify and disclose insider
transactions -- loans to employees or their family members.
If Shayla Smith was involved in originating, processing,
or approving her parents' mortgage, or if the insider
relationship was not properly disclosed, this constitutes
a regulatory violation.

This financial fraud pattern is directly relevant to
Plaintiff's claims because it demonstrates the coordinated
misconduct of the network surrounding Defendant Banks,
who is connected to the Smith family through Purpose
Charter Academy.
