                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

DPSCD is the authorizer of Purpose Charter Academy's
charter. Defendant Brian Roderick Banks is the
superintendent and controlling operator of PCA.

Plaintiff has discovered that Banks:
  - Has multiple prior felony convictions (People v. Banks,
    Wayne Co. Circuit Court, 2003 and 2004)
  - Has two federal bankruptcies (E.D. Michigan, 2011 and
    2019)
  - Has four Michigan state tax liens (2017-2024)
  - Claims to hold a Juris Doctor but is NOT licensed to
    practice law in Michigan
  - Has falsely represented himself as an attorney to
    parents of PCA students
  - Is named in a prior sexual harassment complaint
    (Cotton v. Banks)

DPSCD has a statutory obligation to ensure that charter
school operators meet fitness and character requirements.
If Banks failed to disclose his criminal history, financial
distress, or misrepresented his qualifications, DPSCD
may have a basis to revoke or decline to renew PCA's
charter.
