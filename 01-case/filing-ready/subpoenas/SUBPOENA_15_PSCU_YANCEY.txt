                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

Wayne County ROD records establish that both Defendant
Banks and Judge Yancey obtained residential mortgages
from the same small credit union (PSCU) within a short
timeframe. Banks' HELOC was recorded October 2025;
Yancey's mortgage was recorded October 2024.

This is notable because:
  - PSCU is a relatively small, niche credit union
    (not a national bank);
  - Both borrowers have documented financial distress
    (Banks: 2 bankruptcies, 4 tax liens; Yancey:
    2 government mortgage bailouts, tax forfeiture);
  - Both borrowers have a pre-existing financial
    relationship (Yancey paid Banks $15,750 from
    campaign funds);
  - A shared lender pathway suggests either a referral
    relationship or an insider connection at PSCU.
