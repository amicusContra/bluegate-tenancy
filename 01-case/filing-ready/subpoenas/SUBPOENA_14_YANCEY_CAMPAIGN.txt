                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

Public campaign finance records show that Judge Tenisha
R. Yancey paid Defendant Banks (through Banks Strategy
& Consultants) approximately $15,750 from her campaign
fund during the 2022 election cycle -- representing
44.9% of her total campaign expenditures.

Judge Yancey also chairs the board of MacDowell
Preparatory Academy, a charter school operated by
Defendant Banks. This dual relationship (financial
dependence through campaign services AND governance
appointment) raises serious questions about judicial
independence and potential conflicts of interest.

Additionally, Judge Yancey and Defendant Banks both
obtained mortgages from the same credit union (Public
Service Credit Union) -- a pattern that is the subject
of a separate subpoena (#06).

Plaintiff seeks complete campaign finance records to
determine:
  - The full scope of financial payments to Banks;
  - Whether any payments were quid pro quo for
    favorable judicial treatment;
  - Whether campaign funds were properly accounted for
    under Michigan Campaign Finance Act (MCL 169.201
    et seq.).

This Subpoena is issued pursuant to MCR 2.305 by a party
to a pending civil action.
