                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

MacDowell Preparatory Academy is a charter school where
Defendant Banks serves in an administrative capacity and
Judge Tenisha R. Yancey serves as board chair.

A published check register for MacDowell (obtained by
journalist Rita Williams) shows that on April 24, 2025,
MacDowell issued a $25,174.74 payment to Bank of America
for credit card charges. Internal line items labeled
"Banks Credit Card" account for approximately $25,665
of the charges being settled. The nature of these credit
card charges is not broken out in the register.

Plaintiff seeks the underlying documentation for these
charges to determine whether school funds were used for
personal expenses -- a potential misuse of public charter
school funding.

The governance overlap (Banks as operator, Yancey as
board chair, Yancey paying Banks from campaign funds)
suggests a self-dealing arrangement that warrants
full financial transparency.

This Subpoena is issued pursuant to MCR 2.305 by a party
