                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

Banks holds MDE Administrator Certificate #AD0000048782,
issued November 1, 2022, with an Elementary and Secondary
(K-12) endorsement in Central Office Administration,
expiring June 30, 2027. The certificate is listed as
"Conditional Approval."

Plaintiff seeks to understand:
  - Why the certificate was conditionally approved;
  - Whether Banks disclosed his felony convictions on
    his application;
  - Whether Banks' doctoral degree from Walden University
    was properly verified (Walden has been the subject
    of FTC enforcement actions regarding deceptive
    practices);
  - Who approved the certificate despite Banks' criminal
    history;
  - Whether the approval process followed MDE's own
    policies for applicants with criminal records.

Banks has used his MDE certification and claimed
credentials (including falsely claiming to be an attorney)
to gain the trust of parents and control enrollment at
Purpose Charter Academy. Understanding the approval
chain is essential to Plaintiff's fraud claims.
