                    PURPOSE AND RELEVANCE

Plaintiff has documented financial and governance
relationships between Defendant Banks and two Wayne
County judges: Judge Cylenthia Miller (who has an active
federal civil rights lawsuit against her, Bryant v. Miller,
2:26-cv-10917) and Judge Tenisha Yancey (who paid Banks
$15,750 from campaign funds and chairs his school board).

The assignment of custody matters involving children
enrolled in Banks' school to judges with financial ties
to Banks raises questions about whether the case
assignment process is truly random or has been influenced.

Plaintiff does not allege that the Wayne County Clerk
is involved in any misconduct. This Subpoena seeks
purely administrative records to verify the integrity
of the assignment process.
