## WHY THE PPO FAILED

The PPO hearing was the WRONG venue. Here's why:

| PPO Hearing | What It Means |
|-------------|---------------|
| DaSean bears the entire burden of proof | He has to prove everything; Shayla proves nothing |
| No discovery | Can't compel Shayla to produce the AOP or any documents |
| No compulsory process (practical) | Can't subpoena witnesses easily |

| Shayla doesn't even have to respond | She can sit on Zoom and stay silent (which she did) |

**The PPO was never the right tool for a custody fight.** It's a
protection order, not a fact-finding proceeding. DaSean was trying
to use a shield as a sword.

### 2. F.A.M.E. Investigation (After Oct 7 referral)

**What it is:** Wayne County's custody investigation program —
Family Assessment, Mediation and Evaluation. NOT a hearing. It's
an investigation.

**Witnesses:** Not in the hearing sense — but the investigator
**interviews people:**
- Both parents (mandatory if requested — MCL 552.505)
- Children (age-appropriate)
- **Other witnesses** — the investigator CAN interview anyone
  relevant: Kevin, Deanna, neighbors, teachers, doctors

**What happens:**
- Home visits to BOTH residences
- Assessment of children's needs and wellbeing
- Review of filings and documents from both sides
- Written report + recommendation to the court based on 12
  best-interest factors (MCL 722.23)

**Strategy:** When the investigator contacts DaSean, he should
tell them: "I'd like you to speak with Kevin Mok — he's been a
witness to my parenting for the past year and has contemporaneous
documentation." The investigator decides who to interview, but
DaSean can suggest names.

### 3. Custody Evidentiary Hearing (MCR 3.210(C))

**THIS IS THE BIG ONE.**

**What it is:** A full trial-like proceeding before the JUDGE
(not the referee). Required by court rule when custody is
contested and would change the established custodial environment.

**MCR 3.210(C)(1):** "When the custody, parenting time, change
of domicile, or another motion regarding a minor is contested,
**the court may not enter an order** resolving the contested
matter that changes a child's established custodial environment
**without first holding an evidentiary hearing** to determine
whether clear and convincing evidence exists to support the
order."

**MCR 3.210(C)(1) timing:** "a hearing on the matter **must be
held within 56 days**" of filing.

**Witnesses:** ✅ FULL WITNESS TESTIMONY
- DaSean can call witnesses
- DaSean can **subpoena witnesses** under MCR 2.506
- Subpoena signed by attorney OR court clerk (pro se = clerk)
- Must be served at least **2 days before** the hearing
- Must tender witness fees at time of service

**Cross-examination:** ✅ Full cross-examination of all witnesses

**Evidence:** ✅ Full exhibits, documents, records

**Rules of Evidence:** ✅ Michigan Rules of Evidence apply

**Judge must find:** Specific findings on each of the 12
best-interest factors (MCL 722.23), separately for each child.

### 4. De Novo Hearing Before Judge (If referee recommendation is bad)

**What it is:** If DaSean objects to the referee's Oct 7
recommendation, he gets a NEW hearing before the actual judge.
Filed within 21 days (MCR 3.215(F); MCL 552.507(4)).

**Witnesses:** ✅ YES
- MCR 3.215(F)(2): "the court **must allow the parties to present
  live evidence** at the judicial hearing"
- CAN present new witnesses — but must show they weren't
  available at the referee hearing, or that their testimony is
  necessary to address the objection
- CAN supplement the referee record with new evidence

**Evidence:** ✅ Full record from referee PLUS new evidence

**Three types of de novo hearing (MCL 552.507(6)):**
- (a) New decision based entirely on referee record
- (b) New decision based only on new evidence at de novo
- (c) New decision based on referee record + supplemental evidence

**Strategy:** If the referee recommendation is bad — object within
21 days. Request de novo type (c) — referee record supplemented
by live witness testimony. This is where Kevin, Deanna, and
others testify in front of the judge.

## SUBPOENA POWER — HOW DASEAN COMPELS WITNESSES

**MCR 2.506:** DaSean can subpoena anyone.

**Process:**
1. Go to the Wayne County Clerk (CAYMC, Room 201)
2. Ask the clerk to sign blank subpoenas (pro se parties get
   subpoenas signed by the clerk, not an attorney)
3. Fill in the witness name, hearing date, and location
4. Serve the subpoena on the witness at least **2 days before**
   the hearing (14 days if requesting documents)
5. Tender **witness fees** at time of service (currently $12/day
   + $0.10/mile mileage)

**Who DaSean can subpoena:**
- Kevin Mok (willing — but subpoena formalizes it)
- Deanna Mitchell (willing — subpoena protects her from work)
- Heather Hannon (landlord — may need subpoena to appear)
- Shayla Smith (can be compelled to testify as adverse party)
- Leon Smith (can be compelled)
- School officials at PCA (enrollment records, who authorized)
- Banks (if relevant to the school change decision)

**Why subpoena even willing witnesses:** A subpoena protects a
witness who needs to miss work. The employer must honor it. It
also shows the court that DaSean is using proper process.
