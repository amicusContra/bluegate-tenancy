### Why F.A.M.E. first, not GAL

- F.A.M.E. is free or minimal cost — GAL is $2-10K
- F.A.M.E. is the standard FOC process — referee expects it
- F.A.M.E. visits BOTH homes — the investigator sees House 2
 AND Dresden AND PCA
- F.A.M.E. findings become the professional foundation for
  everything that follows — GAL, evidentiary hearing, custody
  order
- DaSean requesting F.A.M.E. shows the referee he WANTS
  investigation, not avoidance
- The investigator is a MANDATORY REPORTER (MCL 722.623) —
  if they find harm at PCA or Dresden, they must report
- GAL stays in pocket as escalation based on F.A.M.E. findings
