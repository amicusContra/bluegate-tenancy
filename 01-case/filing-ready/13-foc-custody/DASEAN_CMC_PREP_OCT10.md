## WHAT IS A CMC?

A Case Management Conference is where the judge decides:
- What issues are in the case
- What needs to happen next (investigations, discovery, hearings)
- A timeline/schedule for the case
- Whether any temporary orders are needed

**You are not on trial.** Nobody is deciding custody today.
The judge is building a roadmap.

**Your job:** Tell the judge what you need and ask for a schedule
that gets you there.
