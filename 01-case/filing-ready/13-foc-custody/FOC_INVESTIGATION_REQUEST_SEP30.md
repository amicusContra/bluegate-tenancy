## THE STRATEGY — WHY FOC FIRST, GAL SECOND

### FOC Investigation (MCL 552.505(1)(g))

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **What it is** | Standard custody investigation by the FOC |
| **Who does it** | FOC investigator (court employee) |
| **Cost** | Free or minimal — part of the court process |
| **What happens** | Both parents interviewed, both homes visited, children's needs assessed, written report to court |
| **DaSean's right** | "If requested by a party, an investigation **shall** include a meeting with the party" — the word "shall" means mandatory |
| **Wayne County version** | **F.A.M.E.** — Family Assessment, Mediation and Evaluation. Full investigation + recommendation. |
| **Trigger** | Can be requested by a party OR ordered by the court |
| **Timeline** | Typically weeks to a few months |

### GAL (MCL 722.24) — The Escalation

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| **What it is** | A licensed attorney appointed to represent the CHILDREN |
| **Who does it** | Private attorney, paid by the parties |
| **Cost** | $2,000–$10,000+ depending on complexity |
| **What happens** | Attorney conducts independent investigation, interviews children, files report with court |
| **Trigger** | Court determines children's interests are "inadequately represented" |
| **When to request** | AFTER the FOC investigation, if findings suggest the children need their own attorney |

### Why this order is better

1. **FOC investigation is expected.** The referee will likely
   order it anyway. DaSean requesting it FIRST shows initiative.

2. **It's free.** The GAL costs thousands. FOC is part of the
   court system. DaSean isn't burdened with attorney fees.

3. **It produces findings.** If Shayla's home at Dresden has
   problems (Leon's assault history, Banks's school involvement,
   gatekeeping), the FOC investigator will find them. DaSean
   doesn't have to prove it — the investigator does.

4. **It makes the GAL request bulletproof.** If DaSean later
   needs a GAL, he says: "The FOC investigation found X, Y, and
   Z. Based on those findings, I believe the children need their
   own attorney." That's not a father complaining — that's a
   father citing a professional investigation.

5. **DaSean walks into Oct 7 already in motion.** He's not
   waiting for the court to tell him what to do. He's already
   called the FOC, sent the written request, and is ready for
   the investigation. The referee sees a prepared, proactive
   father — not someone caught off guard.
