## POTENTIAL NEW COUNT: PATTERN OF DECEPTIVE RECRUITMENT

*[Consider as Count X or as reinforcement of RICO predicates]*

### Analysis:

This count would formalize the pattern: Banks recruits
professionals (parents, board members, attorneys, educators)
without disclosing his criminal history, then uses their
professional standing as a shield. Each recruitment is:
- A false representation (presenting himself as credentialed)
- A material omission (withholding criminal history)
- An act in furtherance of the enterprise (each professional
  adds a layer of legitimacy)

Under MCL 600.2907 (civil action for criminal conduct), each
instance of UPL (MCL 450.681) and fraud (MCL 750.218) in
furtherance of this pattern gives rise to civil liability.

Under 18 USC § 1962 (RICO), the pattern of deceptive
recruitment across state lines (GLLG in Southfield, cases in
Ingham, federal referrals to 8 agencies) constitutes
racketeering activity.

### Standing question:
Kevin's standing for this count comes from his direct injury:
he is the person who has had to inform each professional of
Banks's fraud, at his own expense and time. Every hour Kevin
spends disclosing evidence to GLLG is an hour he would not
have needed to spend if Banks had told the truth at intake.
