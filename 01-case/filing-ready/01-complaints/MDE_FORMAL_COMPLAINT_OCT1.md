## V. SPECIFIC ALLEGATIONS

### A. Unauthorized Practice of Law (New Criminal Conduct)

On **September 9, 2026**, Brian Banks filed DPD Report
#26-0909-0176 against me. In this sworn report, Banks told
Detroit Police officers **"I am a lawyer."**

Banks has never been admitted to the Michigan Bar or any
other bar. (State Bar of Michigan search: zero results.)
This statement constitutes:
- Unauthorized practice of law under **MCL 450.681**
- False statement in a police report under **MCL 750.411a**

On **August 29, 2026**, Banks told me **"I'm a lawyer"** in
front of two members of his staff at Purpose Charter Academy
while denying me access as a legal father's authorized
representative.

These statements were made while Banks was serving as
superintendent of both MacDowell and PCA — schools
receiving public funds and serving vulnerable children.

### B. Violation of TCR 22-12, Condition #4

**Condition #4** provides: Banks *"shall not be the sole
individual responsible for approval of any expenditure
of funds belonging to the school."*

Banks is the **sole member** of The Purpose Group Michigan
LLC. Purpose Group received **$4,285,201 (72.67%)** of
MacDowell's total revenue in FY2024-25. As sole member,
Banks has sole authority over Purpose Group's expenditures.

The entity structure routes public school revenue through a
private LLC where the TCR 22-12 conditions do not facially
apply. Banks controls the expenditure of public funds
through his sole-member LLC while the conditions restrict
his authority at the school level. This structure appears
designed to circumvent Condition #4.

Joseph Holland Jr. — Banks's domestic partner who resides
at the same address (1968 Severn Road, Grosse Pointe Woods)
— holds Secretary/Treasurer positions at Purpose Foundation
and Treasurer of the Bank on Banks for Michigan PAC. Holland
has a criminal conviction for Controlled Substance —
Delivery/Manufacture (2009, Oakland County) and was
discharged from MDOC supervision in 2024 "WITHOUT
IMPROVEMENT." Holland does not appear to provide any
independent financial oversight of Purpose Group LLC.

### C. False Representation in Management Agreement

The Management Agreement between Purpose Group LLC and PCA
contains a representation at **Section 5.2** stating
**"No Related Parties or Common Control"** between Purpose
Group and PCA.

Banks is the sole member of Purpose Group AND the
superintendent of PCA. This representation is **false on
its face.** If this agreement was submitted to or relied
upon by DPSCD as the authorizer, it contains a material
misrepresentation.

### D. Denial of Parental Access (FERPA Standard)

On August 29, 2026, Banks denied a legal father's
authorized representative access to Purpose Charter Academy.
The Affidavit of Parentage establishing DaSean Mitchell as
the legal father is undisputed — the mother has acknowledged
it in her own filings. No court order restricts the father's
rights.

Under FERPA (20 U.S.C. § 1232g), both parents have equal
access rights to educational records unless a court order
specifically restricts them. Banks used false legal authority
("I'm a lawyer") to justify the denial.

### E. Condition #7 Compliance Verifications

**Condition #7** requires written verification of compliance
with Conditions #3 and #4 every six months, signed by the
board president.

Board President Nicole Wells-Stallworth signs these
verifications. Given the Condition #4 concerns described
above — specifically the sole-member LLC structure that
routes 72.67% of revenue through Banks's personal entity —
I respectfully ask whether the compliance verifications
submitted to MDE accurately reflect the financial
arrangements at MacDowell and PCA.
