## LEGAL CONSEQUENCES OF NONCOMPLIANCE

**Michigan Law:**
Under Michigan law, the intentional destruction of evidence relevant
to pending or reasonably anticipated litigation gives rise to an
adverse inference instruction and potential sanctions, including
dismissal or default judgment. MCR 2.313(B)(2)(c); *Bloemendaal v.
Town & Country Sports Center, Inc.*, 255 Mich App 207, 211 (2002).
*Brenner v. Kolk*, 226 Mich App 149 (1997).

**Federal Law:**
Under federal law, the destruction of documents relevant to a federal
investigation constitutes:
- **18 U.S.C. § 1519** (Sarbanes-Oxley): Destruction of records in
  federal investigations — up to 20 years imprisonment
- **18 U.S.C. § 1512(c)**: Tampering with evidence — up to 20 years
- **18 U.S.C. § 1001**: False statements — up to 5 years
- **18 U.S.C. § 1503**: Obstruction of justice

**This obligation is ongoing.** It extends to all documents and data
in existence as of the date of this notice and any documents or data
created thereafter that fall within the scope described above.

**This obligation cannot be satisfied by passive inaction.** You must
take **affirmative steps** to identify, segregate, and preserve all
responsive materials, including instructing any IT staff, custodians,
