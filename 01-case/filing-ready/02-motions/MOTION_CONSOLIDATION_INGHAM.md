### B. Wayne County Cannot Provide a Fair Forum for These Claims

11. Plaintiff raises this issue not to impugn any individual
    Wayne County judicial officer, but to place on the record
    the structural conflicts that make Wayne County an improper
    forum for the broader claims in these consolidated cases.

12. **Brian Roderick Banks' Wayne County Judicial Connections:**

    a. Banks operated Purpose Charter Academy at 19750
       Greenfield Rd, Detroit — authorized by the Detroit
       Public Schools Community District, a Wayne County
       entity.

    b. Banks previously operated MacDowell Preparatory Academy,
       also in Detroit, under a charter from Wayne RESA —
       a Wayne County entity.

    c. Banks' political consulting firm, Banks Strategy &
       Consultants, received **44.9% of Wayne County
       Commissioner Tenisha Yancey's 2022 campaign
       expenditures** — a sitting Wayne County elected
       official who also serves as a 36th District Court
       judge.

    d. Wayne County Circuit Court Judge Cylenthia Miller —
       who sits in the Family Division of the same 3rd
       Circuit Court where the custody case is pending —
       has documented property transactions through the
       Wayne County Register of Deeds office of Eric Sabree.
       Judge Miller's property at [address] went through six
       tax forfeiture and redemption cycles through Sabree's
       office, with assessed value increasing from ~$40,000
       to ~$370,000.

    e. Judge Miller also serves as Board Chair of the National
       Action Network — the same organization network in
       which Banks has cultivated political relationships,
       including through the Congressional Black Caucus
       Foundation events where Banks was recently scheduled
       to appear.

    f. Attorney Sean Perkins — who has appeared in Banks-
       related matters and received campaign contributions
       from the Sabree family — practices in Wayne County
       courts.

13. **The risk is structural, not personal.** Plaintiff does
    not allege that Judge Triplett (who currently holds the
    custody case) has any improper connection. However:

    a. Wayne County's docket directive system routinely
       reassigns cases among Family Division judges. The
       2022-2026 docket directives show multiple
       reassignments (Elder→Triplett, Harb→Williams-
       Claybourne, etc.). There is no guarantee this case
       remains with Judge Triplett.

    b. Any reassignment to a judge within the Banks/Yancey/
       Miller/Sabree orbit — even inadvertently — would
       compromise the proceedings.

    c. In Ingham County, **no judge on either division** has
       any documented connection to Banks, the Smith family,
       Yancey, Miller, Sabree, or any entity in the network.

14. **The RICO dimension.** Plaintiff's claims, when fully
    developed, will establish that Banks operates within a
    racketeering enterprise involving:
    - Charter school fraud (diverting public education funds)
    - Property fraud (laundering money through distressed
      Wayne County real estate via Sabree's office)
    - Judicial capture (maintaining bench power through
      campaign finance, political appointments, and
      reciprocal favors)

    These claims cannot receive a fair hearing in the county
    where the enterprise operates and where its beneficiaries
    hold judicial office. **MCR 2.222(A)** permits venue change
    "when an impartial trial cannot be had where the action
    is pending." That standard is met here — not because of any
    individual judge, but because the enterprise IS Wayne County
    government.
