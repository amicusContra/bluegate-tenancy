ORDER AUTHORIZING ALTERNATIVE SERVICE ON BRIAN RODERICK BANKS
AND DEEMING PRIOR SERVICE EFFECTIVE


The Court, having considered Plaintiff/Petitioner's Motion for
Alternative Service, the attached exhibits documenting multiple
independent channels of service attempted on September 18, 2026,
and being otherwise fully informed:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

1.  Service of the PPO Hearing Notice in Case No. 26-0000004435-PH
    on Respondent Brian Roderick Banks is DEEMED EFFECTIVE as of
    September 18, 2026. The hearing shall proceed as scheduled on
    September 29, 2026 at 1:30 PM.

2.  Plaintiff/Petitioner may serve all future papers on Respondent
    Banks by first-class mail, email, or fax to any known address
    or contact, deemed effective on the date of sending.

3.  Respondent Banks shall identify his legal counsel, if any,
    within seven (7) days of this Order, or be deemed to be
    appearing pro se.


IT IS SO ORDERED.
