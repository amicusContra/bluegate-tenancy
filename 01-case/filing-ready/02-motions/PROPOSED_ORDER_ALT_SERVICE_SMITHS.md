ORDER AUTHORIZING ALTERNATIVE SERVICE ON SMITH RESPONDENTS


The Court, having considered Plaintiff's Motion for Alternative
Service, the attached exhibits, and being otherwise fully informed:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

1.  Plaintiff may serve all papers on Respondents Leon A. Smith,
    Marquita L. Smith, Shayla Smith, and Simone R. Smith by

    constitute effective notice to all Respondents.

4.  This Order applies to all pending and future filings in the
    above-captioned cases.


IT IS SO ORDERED.
