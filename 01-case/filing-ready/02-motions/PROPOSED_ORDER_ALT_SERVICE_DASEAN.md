ORDER AUTHORIZING ALTERNATIVE SERVICE
AND DEEMING PRIOR SERVICE EFFECTIVE


The Court, having considered Petitioner's Motion for
Alternative Service, the attached exhibits documenting
multiple independent channels of service attempted, and
being otherwise fully informed:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

1.  Service of the PPO Hearing Notice on Respondent is
    DEEMED EFFECTIVE as of the date of service documented
    in the attached affidavit. The hearing shall proceed
    as scheduled on September 29, 2026 at 1:30 PM.

2.  Petitioner may serve all future papers on Respondent
    by first-class mail, email, fax, or any combination
    thereof, to any known address or contact information,
    deemed effective on the date of sending.

3.  This Order applies to all pending and future filings
    in the above-captioned case.


IT IS SO ORDERED.
