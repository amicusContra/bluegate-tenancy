## II. LEGAL STANDARD

4. **MCR 2.222(A):** "The court may order a change of venue of
   a civil action... for the convenience of parties and witnesses
   or when an impartial trial cannot be had where the action
   is pending."

5. **MCR 2.222(B):** "If the venue of the action is proper,
   the court may not change the venue on its own initiative,
   but may do so only on motion of a party." Defendant is a
   party and files this motion.

6. **MCL 722.26(2):** Custody actions "shall be submitted to
   the circuit court of the county where the child resides or
   may be found." The children resided in Ingham County at the
   time of filing.

7. **MCL 600.1023:** "Whenever practicable," matters within the
   Family Division involving members of the same family shall
   be assigned to a single judge.

8. **SCAO FOCB Memorandum 2025-01** confirms: "A prejudgment
   transfer is governed by MCR 2.222(B) (change of venue)."
