ORDER AUTHORIZING ALTERNATIVE SERVICE


The Court, having considered Plaintiff's Motion for
Alternative Service, the attached exhibits documenting
multiple independent channels of service attempted, and
being otherwise fully informed:

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

1.  Plaintiff may serve all papers on Defendants/Respondents
    by first-class mail, email, fax, or any combination
    thereof, to any known address or contact information.

2.  Service shall be deemed effective on the date of mailing,
    sending, or transmission by Plaintiff.

3.  Prior service attempts, including certified mail, first-
    class mail, fax, email, and personal delivery, are deemed
    effective as of their respective dates.

4.  This Order applies to all pending and future filings in
    the above-captioned case and related proceedings.


IT IS SO ORDERED.
