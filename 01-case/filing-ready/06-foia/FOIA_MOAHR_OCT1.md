## INTERNAL NOTES — DO NOT SEND

### Why the MOAHR record matters

1. **The hearing transcript** has the FULL testimony —
   Meihn's exact words, Shulman's exact words, Leno's
   exact words, Vitti's letter. Not the summary in the
   Final Decision — the actual testimony under oath.

2. **The ALJ recommended approval WITHOUT conditions.**
   Rice overruled this and added 7 conditions. The ALJ's
   reasoning — and what the AG argued against — is in the
   proposal for decision and exceptions.

3. **The AG's exceptions** will show exactly what the
   department argued against. The AG OBJECTED to granting
   the certificate. Their arguments are the state's own
   assessment of Banks's fitness.

4. **Exhibits** may include documents Banks submitted in
   support — financial records, character references,
   treatment records. These are discoverable and can be
   compared to what has actually happened since.

5. **Cross-examination** — if the AG cross-examined Meihn,
   Shulman, Leno, or Vitti, that testimony may reveal
   admissions or weaknesses not captured in the Final
   Decision summary.
