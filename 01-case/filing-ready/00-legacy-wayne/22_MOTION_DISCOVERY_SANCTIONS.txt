SANCTIONS REQUESTED

6. Pursuant to MCR 2.313(B)(2), Defendant requests that the
Court impose the following sanctions:

   (a) An order that the matters regarding which the discovery
       was sought, or any other designated facts, be taken as
       established for purposes of this action in accordance
       with the claim of the party obtaining the order
       (MCR 2.313(B)(2)(a));

   (b) An order refusing to allow Plaintiff to support or
       oppose designated claims or defenses, or prohibiting
       Plaintiff from introducing designated matters in
       evidence (MCR 2.313(B)(2)(b));

   (c) An order striking Plaintiff's pleadings or parts
       thereof (MCR 2.313(B)(2)(c));

   (d) An order requiring Plaintiff to pay the reasonable
       expenses, including attorney fees (or pro se equivalent
       costs), caused by Plaintiff's failure to comply
       (MCR 2.313(A)(4));

   (e) Such other and further relief as this Court deems just
       and equitable.

PATTERN OF NON-COMPLIANCE

7. Plaintiff's failure to comply with discovery is part of an
established pattern of delay and obstruction:

   (a) Plaintiff failed to respond to Defendant's Counterclaim
       for 31 days, responding only after a Motion for Default

   (b) Plaintiff's Verified Statement (FOC 23) leaves her
       employer, occupation, and gross income fields blank
       despite admitting she is the "breadwinner";

   (c) Plaintiff submitted Defendant's MDHHS wage records
       while withholding her own corresponding records;

   (d) [Additional specific non-compliance to be inserted
       based on which discovery she fails to answer.]

8. Plaintiff's pattern demonstrates willful refusal to
participate in the discovery process rather than inadvertent
oversight.

CONCLUSION

WHEREFORE, Defendant respectfully requests that this Court
impose sanctions as set forth above and grant such other and
further relief as this Court deems just and equitable.
