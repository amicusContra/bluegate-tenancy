WITHDRAWAL

3. Defendant does not seek to prevent Plaintiff from
participating in this proceeding. Accordingly, Defendant

PLAINTIFF'S PROPOSED TERMS AS EXPLICIT ADMISSIONS

(Doc 3, filed August 12, 2026) proposes eleven (11) terms for a
Temporary Order. Eight (8) of those terms are substantively
identical to terms Defendant already proposed. Defendant does not
ask this Court to disregard these proposals. To the contrary,
Defendant asks this Court to treat each proposed term as
Plaintiff's explicit admission of the applicable standard of
conduct — and to note that Plaintiff has already violated each
standard she now proposes.

   (a) Plaintiff proposes: "Neither parent enrolls children in
       a different school without agreement or court order."
       ADMISSION: Unilateral school enrollment is wrong.
       RECORD: Plaintiff enrolled both children at Purpose Charter
       Academy on July 22, 2026 — without any notice to Defendant.
       Plaintiff secretly explored enrollment at Most Holy Trinity
       Catholic Academy from February through July 2026 without
       informing Defendant. Her own Exhibit (Doc 4) proves both.

   (b) Plaintiff proposes: "Both parents communicate directly
       regarding the children."
       ADMISSION: Direct communication between parents should occur.
       RECORD: Plaintiff blocked Defendant's phone number. On

DELAY AS CONTINUED HARM

11. From the date of Plaintiff's filing (June 13, 2026) to
the Case Management Conference (October 9, 2026), one hundred
and eighteen (118) days will have elapsed without any judicial
oversight of the parties' conduct. MCR 2.501(B)(2) provides
that courts "shall give precedence to actions involving a
contest over the custody of minor children." Despite this
directive, Plaintiff's pattern of delay — filing thirty-one
(31) days late, preparing a 192-page response that required
analysis, and taking no steps to expedite proceedings —
has created a window of unchecked unilateral action.

12. During the 118-day period between filing and the scheduled
Case Management Conference, Plaintiff has, without notice to
Defendant or court authorization:

   (a) Enrolled the children at a new school (July 22, 2026);
   (b) Scheduled dental appointments on Defendant's parenting
       time (July 29 and August 7, 2026);
   (c) Moved the children from Eastpointe to Dresden (June 2026);
   (d) Traveled internationally with no notice (Senegal,
       June 1-12, 2026);
   (e) Blocked Defendant's phone number while claiming to
       support communication;
   (f) Cut off Defendant's designated communication contact
       (August 11, 2026);
   (g) Demanded financial contributions ($160 for hair,
       August 16, 2026) without any co-parenting discussion,
       while voluntarily forgoing parenting time to stay
       at a hotel (August 18-19, 2026);
   (h) Continued to route all communication through family
       intermediaries rather than communicating with Defendant;
   (i) Retained exclusive possession of the children's birth
       certificates, Social Security cards, immunization
       records, and medical records.

13. Each of these actions occurred in the absence of a

children's stability.

14. Defendant respectfully requests that this Court consider
