[VOIDED] PROPOSED CONSENT ORDER RE: CUSTODY, PARENTING TIME,
AND RELATED MATTERS

    The parties, having reached agreement, stipulate and consent
to the entry of the following Order:

CUSTODY

1. Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff DaSean Mitchell shall have SOLE
   LEGAL CUSTODY of the minor children:
   - Sahana Saige Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld])
   - Allanna Star Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld])

2. DaSean Mitchell shall have final decision-making authority on
   all major decisions regarding the children's health, education,
   and welfare. Defendant shall consult with Plaintiff before
   making major decisions, but Defendant's decision shall be
   final.

3. DaSean Mitchell shall have PRIMARY PHYSICAL CUSTODY. The
   children's primary residence shall be [REDACTED_ADDR_2],
   Lansing, MI 48910.

PARENTING TIME

4. Plaintiff Shayla Smith shall have parenting time on an
   alternating-week basis. This parenting time schedule is
   established by Defendant pursuant to his sole legal custody
   and may be modified by Defendant in writing upon a material
   change in circumstances, including but not limited to:
   Plaintiff's failure to comply with the terms of this Order,
   Plaintiff's failure to communicate directly with Defendant,
   Plaintiff's interference with Defendant's parenting time,
   or any conduct inconsistent with the children's best
   interests. Any such modification shall be prospective and
   communicated in writing with fourteen (14) days' notice.

5. Transportation to and from parenting time exchanges shall
   be provided by Plaintiff.

5a. Exchanges shall occur on Sunday at 6:00 PM at a mutually
    agreed neutral location, or as otherwise agreed in writing.

6. Plaintiff shall have the children on Mother's Day regardless
   of the regular parenting time schedule. All other holidays
   and school breaks follow the regular alternating-week
   schedule as established by Defendant under Paragraph 4.

7. RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL: If Plaintiff is unable to personally
   care for the children during her parenting time for a period
   exceeding twenty-four (24) hours, she shall first offer that
   time to Defendant before arranging alternative childcare.
   Defendant shall respond within two (2) hours of receiving
   the offer.

8. Each parent shall have the right to reasonable telephone
   and video contact with the children during the other parent's
   parenting time, at reasonable hours.

EDUCATION

9. The children shall remain enrolled at Purpose Charter Academy
   for the Fall 2026 semester. This is a one-time concession
   and does not establish precedent or limit Defendant's
   authority under Paragraph 2.

10. All education decisions after the Fall 2026 semester —
    including school enrollment, withdrawal, transfer, and
    extracurricular activities — shall be made by Defendant in
    his sole discretion pursuant to his sole legal custody.
    Defendant shall consult Plaintiff before making such
    decisions, but Defendant's decision shall be final.

COMMUNICATION

11. Plaintiff shall not block Defendant's phone number or
    restrict his ability to contact her regarding the children.

12. All communication regarding decisions about the children's
    health, education, welfare, or schedule shall be direct
    between the parties or through mutually agreed neutral third
    parties (currently: Kevin Mok and Heather Hannon, House 2
, Lansing, MI 48910).

13. Family members on either side (including but not limited to
    Deanna Mitchell, Simone Smith, Leon Smith, and Marquita
    Smith) shall not serve as intermediaries for decision-making
    communications. Day-of logistics (e.g., notifying the other
    household of a delay in pickup) may be communicated through
    any available adult, but no decision regarding the children
    shall be made or communicated through family intermediaries.

14. Defendant establishes and maintains the communication
    structure. Plaintiff may contact Defendant directly or
    through the designated neutral third parties identified
    above. Defendant may update the designated contacts in
    writing at any time.

15. Each parent shall notify the other within 24 hours of
    scheduling, rescheduling, or canceling any medical
    appointment, school event, or change to the children's
    schedule during the other parent's parenting time.

MEDICAL AND INSURANCE

16. Plaintiff shall maintain the children on her employer-
    provided health insurance and shall provide Defendant with
    current insurance cards and policy information within
    fourteen (14) days of entry of this Order.

17. Medical decisions shall be made by Defendant pursuant to his
    sole legal custody. Defendant shall consult Plaintiff before
    major medical decisions (surgery, ongoing treatment, mental
    health). Routine and emergency care may be obtained by either
    parent without prior consultation.

VITAL DOCUMENTS

18. Within fourteen (14) days of entry of this Order, Plaintiff
    shall provide Defendant with originals or certified copies
    of: birth certificates, Social Security cards, immunization
    records, and health insurance cards for both children.

TRAVEL

19. Neither parent shall take the children outside the State of
    Michigan without fourteen (14) days' prior written notice to
    the other parent, including destination, dates, and
    itinerary.

20. Neither parent shall apply for or obtain a passport for
    either child without the written consent of both parents or
    further order of this Court.

CHILD SUPPORT

21. Child support shall be calculated by the Friend of the Court
    pursuant to the Michigan Child Support Formula, based on both
    parties' verified income.

22. Plaintiff shall file an Amended Verified Statement (FOC 23)
    with complete and accurate income information within fourteen
    (14) days of entry of this Order.

23. Defendant waives retroactive child support for the period
    prior to entry of this Order. Prospective support shall run
    from the date of this Order forward.

ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RELIANCE (ESTOPPEL)

24. Plaintiff acknowledges that during the parties' relationship,
    Defendant relied upon Plaintiff's representations of
    commitment (including two commitment ceremonies) and
    Plaintiff's role as primary earner in forgoing independent
    employment and career development in order to serve as
    primary caregiver to the children.

25. Plaintiff acknowledges this reliance was reasonable and
    resulted in economic disadvantage to Defendant.

26. This acknowledgment does not constitute a waiver of any
    separate civil claim by either party. The parties agree that
    any separate civil matter shall be resolved independently.

DEVICE MONITORING

27. Neither parent shall monitor, record, or intercept the other
    parent's communications through the children's electronic
    devices or any other means.

SOCIAL MEDIA AND PUBLIC STATEMENTS

28. Neither parent shall post, share, or publish on any social
    media platform, blog, website, or public forum any
    information about the other parent, the children, or this
    case, including but not limited to:
    (a) photographs or videos of the children without the
        other parent's written consent;
    (b) statements about the other parent's character,
        parenting, finances, or personal life;
    (c) details of this legal proceeding or any related
        legal matter;
    (d) statements intended to solicit sympathy, support,
        or public opinion regarding the custody dispute.

29. Neither parent shall permit family members or associates to
    make such posts on their behalf or with their encouragement.

30. Violation of this provision may be raised as a change in
    circumstances warranting modification.

OTHER PROVISIONS

31. Neither parent shall disparage the other in the presence of
    the children or permit others to do so.

32. Both parents shall cooperate in maintaining the children's
    relationships with extended family on both sides.

DISCOVERY AND PENDING MOTIONS

33. Upon entry of this Order, all pending discovery shall be
    deemed withdrawn. All pending motions shall be deemed moot.

34. The preservation obligations established by Defendant's
    August 16, 2026 Preservation Requests shall remain in effect
    for six (6) months following entry of this Order.


IT IS SO ORDERED.
