STIPULATED ORDER REGARDING CUSTODY,
PARENTING TIME, AND CHILD SUPPORT

The parties, Shayla Smith (Plaintiff) and DaSean Mitchell
(Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff), having reached an agreement on all
matters of custody, parenting time, child support, and related
issues, and the Court being otherwise advised in the premises,

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

CUSTODY

1. LEGAL CUSTODY: Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff DaSean Mitchell
shall have sole legal custody of the minor children, Sahana Saige
Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld]) and Allanna Star Mitchell (DOB
[date of birth withheld]), with a consultation requirement: Defendant shall
have final decision-making authority on all major decisions
regarding the children's health, education, religion, and general
welfare, with a requirement to consult with Plaintiff before
making such decisions.

2. PHYSICAL CUSTODY: Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff DaSean Mitchell
shall have primary physical custody of the minor children. The
children's primary residence shall be [REDACTED_ADDR_2], Lansing,
MI 48910. The children's secondary residence shall be XXXX
Cornerstone Blvd, Detroit, MI 48201 (paternal grandmother,
Deanna Mitchell).

PARENTING TIME

3. REGULAR SCHEDULE: Plaintiff Shayla Smith shall have parenting
time on an alternating-week basis, beginning on _____________.
Exchanges shall occur on Sundays at 5:00 PM. Plaintiff shall
pick up the children at [REDACTED_ADDR_2], Lansing, MI 48910 or
at XXXX Cornerstone Blvd, Detroit, MI 48201 as coordinated with
Defendant. Defendant shall have the children ready for pickup at
the designated time.

4. HOLIDAYS: Holidays and school breaks shall be shared equally
per the Wayne County Friend of the Court Standard Holiday
Parenting Time Schedule, alternating annually. The holiday
schedule shall supersede the regular schedule.

5. SUMMER: Summer parenting time shall follow the alternating-
week schedule unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

6. COMMUNICATION DURING PARENTING TIME: Each parent shall
facilitate reasonable phone or video communication between the
children and the other parent during their respective parenting
time. Neither parent shall monitor, record, or interfere with
such communication. Neither parent shall use the children's
phones or devices to communicate scheduling, logistics, or case-
related matters to the other parent.

COMMUNICATION BETWEEN PARENTS

7. All communication regarding the children's schedule, medical
care, education, and welfare shall be made directly between the
parties or through Kevin Mok (Defendant's designated
representative, available at [PHONE NUMBER]). Communication
regarding the children shall not be routed through third parties,
extended family members, or the children's devices except for
day-of pickup and dropoff coordination.

8. Schedule changes, medical appointments, and events affecting
the other parent's parenting time require at least 24 hours
advance notice to the other parent.

TRANSPORTATION

9. Plaintiff shall be responsible for transportation at the
beginning and end of her parenting time. Plaintiff shall pick
up the children from the Defendant's designated location and
return the children to the same location at the conclusion of
her parenting time.

CHILD SUPPORT

10. Child support shall be calculated per the Michigan Child
Support Formula based on the actual, verified incomes of both
parties and the actual parenting-time overnights as established
by this order. Support shall be paid through the Michigan State
Disbursement Unit as directed by the Friend of the Court.

11. Plaintiff's income for purposes of this order shall be
verified through the Friend of the Court and shall include all
income from employment at Lake Trust Credit Union and any other
sources.

12. Defendant's income for purposes of this order shall include
all income from employment, self-employment, and any VA benefits.

PASSPORT RESTRICTION

13. Neither parent shall apply for, obtain, or possess passports
for the minor children without the written consent of the other
parent or further order of this Court. Any existing passport
applications shall be disclosed to both parties within 14 days
of entry of this order.

DOCUMENT PRODUCTION

14. Within fourteen (14) days of entry of this order, Plaintiff
shall produce to Defendant the following documents: original or
certified copies of birth certificates, Social Security cards,
medical records, immunization records, school records, and
insurance documentation for both minor children.

SCHOOL ENROLLMENT

15. The minor children shall be enrolled in and attend school in
the Lansing School District (Ingham County), consistent with
their primary residence. Any request to change school enrollment
shall require the written consent of Defendant or further order
of this Court.

MUTUAL PROVISIONS

16. Neither parent shall make disparaging remarks about the other
parent, the other parent's family, or the other parent's
household members in the presence of the minor children.

17. Both parents shall keep the other informed of any change in
address, phone number, or employment within 14 days of such
change.

18. Both parents shall provide access to school records, medical
records, and extracurricular information upon request.

19. Defendant, as sole legal and primary physical custodian,
shall not relocate the minor children's primary residence more
than 100 miles from the current residence without prior notice
to Plaintiff and approval of this Court, consistent with
MCL 722.31.

DISMISSAL

20. Upon entry of this order, all pending motions in this case
are deemed resolved. Plaintiff's Complaint for Custody and
Defendant's Counterclaim for Custody are both resolved by this
stipulated agreement.

IT IS SO ORDERED.
