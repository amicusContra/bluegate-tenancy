BASIS

1. MCR 2.305(A)(1) provides that an unrepresented party may
move the court for issuance of non-party discovery subpoenas.

2. Defendant has concurrently filed a Motion for Non-Party
Discovery Subpoenas seeking production from four individual
non-parties (Simone Smith, Leon A. Smith, Marquita L. Smith,
and Tunezha Crowder). This supplemental motion seeks subpoenas
to institutional record holders whose documents are directly
relevant to contested issues that Plaintiff has refused to
address in her own filings.

leaves her employer, occupation, and gross income fields blank.
Plaintiff admits in her Answer (Doc 1, Para 10, filed August 12,
2026) that she is the "breadwinner." She submitted Defendant's
MDHHS wage records while withholding her own. Institutional
records are necessary to obtain information Plaintiff has
deliberately concealed.
