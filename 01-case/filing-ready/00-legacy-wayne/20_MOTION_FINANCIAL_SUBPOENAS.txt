BASIS

1. MCR 2.305(A)(1) provides that an unrepresented party may
move the court for issuance of non-party discovery subpoenas.

2. Defendant has previously filed two motions for non-party
discovery subpoenas: one seeking production from four individual
non-parties and one seeking production from six institutional
record holders. This second supplemental motion seeks subpoenas
to financial institutions and government agencies to obtain
information Plaintiff has deliberately concealed.

3. Plaintiff's Verified Statement (FOC 23) filed June 17, 2026
leaves her employer, occupation, and gross income fields blank.
Plaintiff admits in her Answer (Doc 1, Para 10, filed August 12,
2026) that she is the "breadwinner." Defendant has concurrently
filed a Motion to Compel Amended Verified Statement and has
filed his own Amended Verified Statement with updated income
and employment information. Plaintiff has not amended hers.

4. Plaintiff's income, assets, debts, and financial capacity
are directly relevant to:
   (a) Child support calculation under the Michigan Child
       Support Formula;
   (b) Plaintiff's capacity to provide for the children's
       material needs under MCL 722.23(b);
   (c) Plaintiff's credibility, given the discrepancy between
       her admitted "breadwinner" status and her blank Verified
       Statement;
   (d) The established custodial environment under MCL 722.23(d),
       including the stability of Plaintiff's housing and
       financial arrangements.

5. Defendant has pursued financial disclosure through
party-to-party discovery (Interrogatories, Requests for
Production, Request for Admissions). Institutional subpoenas
are necessary because Plaintiff has demonstrated a pattern of
concealing her financial information while disclosing
Defendant's.
