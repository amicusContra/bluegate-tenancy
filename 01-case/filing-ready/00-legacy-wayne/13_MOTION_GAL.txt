DEFENDANT'S MOTION FOR APPOINTMENT OF
GUARDIAN AD LITEM FOR THE MINOR CHILDREN

    Defendant DaSean Mitchell, appearing pro se, respectfully
moves this Court to appoint a Guardian ad Litem to represent the
best interests of the minor children, Sahana Saige Mitchell (DOB
[date of birth withheld]) and Allanna Star Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld]), pursuant
to MCL 722.24, and states as follows:

BASIS FOR APPOINTMENT

1. This is a contested custody action in which the parties
dispute primary physical custody and the structure of parenting
time. No prior custody order exists. The children have been
without a governing legal structure since the Complaint was filed
on June 13, 2026 — over two months.

2. The parties maintain separate households in different cities.
Defendant resides at [REDACTED_ADDR_2], Lansing, MI 48910 (Ingham
County), with a signed residential lease and dedicated space for
the children. Plaintiff resides at XXXXX Dresden St, Detroit, MI
48205 (Wayne County), which she describes as a "temporary"
arrangement at her parents' home.

3. Significant factual disputes exist regarding the children's
best interests that an independent investigation would help
resolve, including:

    (a) The respective roles of each parent in the children's
        daily care — Defendant asserts he has been the primary
        daily caregiver for approximately nine years; Plaintiff's
        filings describe herself as the "breadwinner" and
        document provider/decider activities rather than daily
        caregiving;

    (b) The suitability and stability of each household — the
        parties dispute whether the children should reside
        primarily in Lansing (Defendant's home, single-family
        with dedicated children's room, one adult household) or
        Detroit (Plaintiff's parents' home, 3-bedroom/1-bathroom
        with multiple adult occupants, described by Plaintiff as
        temporary);

    (c) The children's educational stability — Plaintiff
        unilaterally changed the children's school enrollment
        on July 22, 2026 without notice to Defendant, after
        secretly exploring a different school from February
        through July 2026. The school selected, Purpose Charter
        Academy (10101 E. Canfield, Detroit), is a brand-new
        institution that had never enrolled a student at the time
        of registration. Its founder's other school (MacDowell
        Preparatory Academy) ranks in the bottom 3% of Michigan
        schools. A review of the campus and its surroundings
        reveals no visible playground or outdoor recreation area
        for children, and the adjacent structures are largely
        vacant and boarded up. Plaintiff appears to have enrolled
        the children without visiting the site. The Guardian ad
        Litem should conduct an independent physical assessment
        of this school and compare it to the established schools
        within walking distance of Defendant's home, all of which
        have documented track records, playground facilities, and
        student-teacher ratios between 12:1 and 17:1;

    (d) Communication and co-parenting — Plaintiff blocked
        Defendant's phone, routes communication through third
        parties and the children's electronic devices, and
        rejected Defendant's written communication protocol;

    (e) Plaintiff's candor with the Court — Plaintiff's Verified
        Statement contains blank fields for her own employment
        and income while listing Defendant's income as "N/A,"
        despite Plaintiff's possession of state employment
        notifications documenting Defendant's earnings;

    (f) Potential misuse of the children's identifying information
        — Plaintiff possesses the children's Social Security
        numbers, birth certificates, and all identifying
        documents and has refused to share them with Defendant.
        Plaintiff listed Defendant's residence as XXXX
        Cornerstone Blvd, Detroit, on the Verified Statement
        (FOC 23), which is Defendant's mother's Section 8
        address. Plaintiff is employed at Lake Trust Credit Union
        and has professional access to financial instruments.
        Defendant is concerned that the children's identities
        may have been used to obtain benefits, open accounts, or
        establish credit without his knowledge or consent. The
        Guardian ad Litem should verify whether any accounts,
        loans, benefits cases, or financial instruments exist in
        the children's names or Social Security numbers, and
        whether the children's identities have been used in
        connection with any government benefits applications.

4. Both parents profess to act in the children's best interests.
An independent Guardian ad Litem can investigate both households,
interview both parents, speak with the children in an age-
appropriate manner, review school and medical records, and provide
this Court with an objective recommendation.

5. Defendant welcomes and invites full investigation of his home,
parenting, employment, support network, and character. Defendant
has nothing to hide and believes independent scrutiny will confirm
his position as the appropriate primary custodial parent.

FACTORS SUPPORTING APPOINTMENT

6. The following factors, taken together, create the type of
contested case in which a GAL appointment serves the children's
interests:

    (a) No existing custody order and prolonged uncertainty;
    (b) Disputed custodial history (nine years of caregiving
        vs. breadwinner role);
    (c) Separate households in different counties;
    (d) Concerns about one parent's candor on sworn filings;
    (e) Unilateral major decisions (school enrollment, travel)
        made without the other parent's knowledge;
    (f) Communication breakdown requiring independent assessment;
    (g) Children's ages (9 and 7) — old enough to have
        preferences and observations, young enough to need
        protection from parental conflict;
    (h) Concerns about the physical suitability of the school
        selected by Plaintiff — no visible playground, vacant
        and boarded surroundings, untested institution with
        founder whose prior school ranks bottom 3% statewide;
    (i) Concerns about the security of the children's
        identifying information — Plaintiff controls all
        documents (SSN cards, birth certificates) and has refused
        to share them with Defendant, raising questions about
        whether the children's identities have been used for
        financial or benefits purposes without Defendant's
        knowledge.

REGARDING COSTS

7. Defendant acknowledges that appointment of a Guardian ad Litem
may involve costs. Defendant requests that the Court allocate GAL
costs equitably between the parties, taking into account the
relative financial positions of the parties — Plaintiff is
employed full-time at Lake Trust Credit Union and has been the
household "breadwinner" by her own admission; Defendant is the
historically unpaid primary caregiver who is rebuilding financial
independence.

8. In the alternative, Defendant requests that the Court consider
assigning the initial cost to Plaintiff, as the party who
initiated this action and whose conduct (unilateral decisions,
communication refusal, document withholding, and incomplete
sworn filings) has created the factual disputes that necessitate
independent investigation.

RELIEF REQUESTED

WHEREFORE, Defendant respectfully requests:

A. That this Court appoint a qualified Guardian ad Litem to
   represent the best interests of Sahana Saige Mitchell and
   Allanna Star Mitchell in this proceeding;

B. That the Guardian ad Litem be directed to investigate both
   parents' homes, interview both parents and the children,
   review school and medical records, conduct an independent
   physical assessment of Purpose Charter Academy (10101 E.
   Canfield, Detroit) including the school campus and
   surrounding neighborhood conditions, compare PCA to the
   established schools available near Defendant's residence,
   verify whether any accounts, benefits, or financial
   instruments exist in the children's names or Social Security
   numbers, and file a report with recommendations to this
   Court;

C. That the costs of the Guardian ad Litem be allocated
   equitably between the parties or assigned to Plaintiff;

D. Such other and further relief as this Court deems just and
   equitable.
