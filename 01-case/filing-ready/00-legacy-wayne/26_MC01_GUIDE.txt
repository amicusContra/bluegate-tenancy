=========================================================
WHAT TO BRING TO THE COURTHOUSE
=========================================================

IMPORTANT: MCR 2.102(B) requires a SEPARATE SUMMONS for
each defendant. You need 4 MC 01 forms, each naming one
defendant. The complaint and exhibits are the same for
all — only the MC 01 changes per defendant.

=========================================================
AT THE CLERK'S WINDOW
=========================================================

1. Say: "I'm filing a new civil complaint, pro se."

2. Hand them:
   - The original MC 01 + complaint + exhibits (stapled)
   - The copies

3. Pay $175

4. The clerk will:
   - Assign a case number
   - Assign a judge
   - Fill in the summons expiration date (91 days)
   - File-stamp all copies
   - Return your stamped copies

5. WRITE DOWN the case number and judge name immediately

6. Ask: "Do I need to file anything else today?"
   (The answer should be no — complaint + summons is
   sufficient to initiate)

7. Ask: "What are the service requirements?"
   (They'll confirm: personal service or certified mail,
   plaintiff cannot serve — must use process server or
   any person over 18 who is not a party)

=========================================================
AFTER FILING
=========================================================

- The summons expires in 91 days — defendants must be
  served before it expires (MCR 2.102(D))
- After service, the process server provides a Proof of
  Service / Affidavit of Service
- You file the Proof of Service with the clerk
- Defendants have 21 days after service to respond
  (MCR 2.108)
- If they don't respond → Motion for Default Judgment
