    RELEVANT ADMISSIONS (Factor b):

    "During FaceTime and other communications with the
    children, Plaintiff has personally overheard Defendant
    make negative statements about Plaintiff within the
    children's hearing..."

    "Plaintiff has also personally overheard Defendant
    make sexually derogatory comments about Plaintiff
    during a FaceTime communication with Sahana, including
    a statement to the effect that Plaintiff was 'for the
    community' and was 'giving it away.'"

    Defendant Shayla Smith admits — under oath — to
    monitoring private conversations through the minor
    children's electronic devices. Plaintiff Kevin Mok
    was a party to those conversations. He did not
    consent to being overheard.

    See MCL 750.539c (felony eavesdropping) and
    MCL 750.539h (civil cause of action).
