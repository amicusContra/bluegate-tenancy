    care for the children on an alternating-week basis since
    that time. List every public benefit you have received or
    currently receive since January 2026, including type,
    amount, dates, and address on each application, and state
    what portion of those benefits, if any, was provided to or
    shared with Defendant during his parenting time to support
    the children's care.

5.  Your FOC 23 was signed "under penalty of perjury." You are
    employed at a federally insured financial institution where
