BASIS

1. MCR 2.305(A)(1) provides that an unrepresented party may
move the court for issuance of non-party discovery subpoenas.
MCR 2.306 authorizes depositions on oral examination, and
MCR 2.305(A)(2) permits subpoenas compelling both testimony
and document production.

information directly relevant to the contested issues in this
custody case, including the children's living arrangements,
caregiving, financial arrangements, the circumstances of
service of process, and the veracity of Plaintiff's sworn
statements.

requests on each individual non-party by United States Certified

their obligation to preserve relevant materials. (See Notice
of Service of Preservation Requests, filed August 18, 2026.)

4. Plaintiff's own Answer (Doc 1) and attached exhibits reference
each of the following non-parties by name or role, making their
testimony directly relevant to verifying Plaintiff's sworn
claims.
