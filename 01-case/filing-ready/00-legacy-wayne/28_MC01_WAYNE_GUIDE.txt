FEE WAIVER: Deanna may qualify under MCR 2.002 as an
indigent party (Section 8 housing, limited income). If
she qualifies, ALL fees are waived — filing fee,
electronic fee, jury fee. Petition the Chief Judge
(7th Floor, CAYMC) or e-file the waiver request via
MiFILE using event code "Fee Waiver Request & Order,
Signed and Filed." File the fee waiver SEPARATELY from
the complaint.
