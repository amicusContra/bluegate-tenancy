COUNT I — EAVESDROPPING (Against Defendant Shayla Smith)
(MCL 750.539c / MCL 750.539h)

25. Plaintiff re-alleges and incorporates Paragraphs 1-24.

26. MCL 750.539c provides: "Any person who is present or who is
    not present during a private conversation and who willfully
    uses any device to eavesdrop upon the conversation without
    the consent of all parties thereto" is guilty of a felony.

27. MCL 750.539h provides a civil cause of action for any person
    whose conversation is eavesdropped upon in violation of
    MCL 750.539c: "Any person who is damaged or whose
    conversation is overheard, recorded, or divulged as a
    result of any violation of this act... may bring a civil
    action... and recover in addition to any other damages, all
    actual damages plus punitive damages in the amount of
    $100.00 or $1,000.00, whichever is greater."
