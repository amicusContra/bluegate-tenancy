DEFENDANT'S MOTION TO COMPEL PLAINTIFF TO FILE
AMENDED VERIFIED STATEMENT

    Defendant DaSean Mitchell, appearing pro se, respectfully
moves this Court to compel Plaintiff Shayla Smith to file an
amended Verified Statement (FOC 23) with complete and accurate
information, and states as follows:

1. On June 17, 2026, Plaintiff filed a Verified Statement
(FOC 23) under penalties of perjury. That statement contains
the following material omissions and misrepresentations:

    (a) Plaintiff left her own employer field BLANK. Plaintiff
        is employed at Lake Trust Credit Union, a fact she admits
        in her Answer filed August 12, 2026, where she describes
        herself as the "breadwinner."

    (b) Plaintiff left her own gross weekly income field BLANK.
        Plaintiff has been continuously employed throughout this
        proceeding and has verifiable income.

    (c) Plaintiff left her own occupation field BLANK.

    (d) Plaintiff wrote "N/A" for Defendant's income. Plaintiff
        was in possession of Michigan Department of Health and
        Human Services Wage Match Client Notices dated February
        20, 2026 documenting Defendant's quarterly earnings of
        $4,362 from multiple employers (Plaintiff's own Exhibit,
        Doc 6, pp.30-36, filed August 12, 2026). "N/A" is not
        accurate.

    (e) Plaintiff left health insurance company and policy
        number fields blank for both children despite carrying
        employer-provided coverage through Blue Cross Blue Shield.

2. The Verified Statement is the primary financial document the
Friend of the Court relies on for child support calculations,
income verification, and initial case assessment. Plaintiff's
blank fields prevent the Court and the Friend of the Court from
performing these functions.

3. In contrast, Defendant filed a complete Verified Statement on
July 10, 2026 listing his employment, income, residence, and all
requested information. Defendant is filing an Amended Verified
Statement updating his employment information.

4. Plaintiff's own August 12 filings confirm she has the
information to complete these fields. Her Answer admits she is
the "breadwinner." Her exhibits include MDHHS documents showing
she has detailed knowledge of both parties' employment and
benefits. Her failure to disclose is deliberate, not inadvertent.

5. Nearly two months have elapsed since Plaintiff filed the
incomplete Verified Statement. She has not amended it.

RELIEF REQUESTED

WHEREFORE, Defendant respectfully requests:

A. That this Court ORDER Plaintiff to file an Amended Verified
   Statement (FOC 23) with complete and accurate information,
   including her employer, occupation, gross weekly income, and
   the children's health insurance details, within fourteen (14)
   days of this Order;

B. That this Court find Plaintiff's Verified Statement is
   materially incomplete and cannot be relied upon for child
   support calculations in its current form;

C. Such other and further relief as this Court deems just and
   equitable.
