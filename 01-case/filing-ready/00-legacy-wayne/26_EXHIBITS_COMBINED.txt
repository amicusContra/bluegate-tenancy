              EXHIBIT A — EAVESDROPPING ADMISSION
=========================================================

    SOURCE: Plaintiff/Counter-Defendant's Answer to
    Defendant's Counterclaim, filed by Shayla Smith in
    Wayne County Circuit Court Case No. 26-108221-DC,
    Third Judicial Circuit, Family Division.
    Hon. Regina Triplett.
    Filed on or about August 12, 2026.
    PAGE 5 OF COUNTER-RESPONSE (attached).

    RELEVANT ADMISSIONS (Factor b):

    Defendant Shayla Smith states under oath:

    "During FaceTime and other communications with the
    children, Plaintiff has personally overheard Defendant
    make negative statements about Plaintiff within the
    children's hearing..."

    "Plaintiff has also personally overheard Defendant
    make sexually derogatory comments about Plaintiff
    during a FaceTime communication with Sahana, including
    a statement to the effect that Plaintiff was 'for the
    community' and was 'giving it away.'"

    SIGNIFICANCE: Defendant Shayla Smith admits — under
    oath — to monitoring private conversations through the
    minor children's electronic devices. Plaintiff Kevin
    Mok was a party to those conversations. He did not
    consent to being overheard. Neither did DaSean Mitchell.

    This admission supports Count I (Eavesdropping, MCL
    750.539c) and Count II (Invasion of Privacy). The
    eavesdropping statute provides a civil cause of action
    under MCL 750.539h.

    Shayla Smith wrote this. Shayla Smith signed it under
    oath. Shayla Smith filed it with the court.

    EXHIBIT B — MAIL INTERCEPTION EVIDENCE
=========================================================

    SOURCE: USPS PS Form 3811 (Domestic Return Receipt)
    cards for Certified Mail Restricted Delivery sent by
    Plaintiff Kevin Mok on August 18, 2026.

    WHAT IS SHOWN:

    Page 1: Signature sides of return receipt cards showing:

    - Marquita L. Smith's AUTHENTIC signature on her own
      card (delivered to XXXXX Dresden St, Detroit MI,
      August 19, 2026 — LEGITIMATE baseline)

    - MATCHING "S Smith" signatures on cards addressed to
      Shayla Smith (x2), Leon A. Smith, and Simone Smith
      — all picked up at the Harper Woods MI post office
      on August 21, 2026

    - Marquita signed under the ADDRESSEES' names, not her
      own, and not as an identified agent

    Pages 2-3: Additional card detail

    SIGNIFICANCE: Defendant Marquita L. Smith traveled to
    the Harper Woods MI post office — the workplace of her
    husband, Defendant Leon A. Smith, a USPS Mail Carrier
    assigned to that facility — and signed for four (4)
    pieces of Certified Mail Restricted Delivery addressed
    to other family members. No PS Form 3801 or
    authorization letter exists on file.

    This constitutes four (4) federal felony counts under
    18 USC 1702 (obstruction of correspondence) and
    18 USC 1708 (theft of mail).

    A complaint has been filed with the United States
    Postal Service Office of Inspector General on
    August 27, 2026 (Reference #202990).

    This evidence supports Count V (Interference with
    Communications) and Count VI (Civil Conspiracy).
