COUNT I — UNJUST ENRICHMENT (Against Defendant Shayla Smith)

20. Plaintiff re-alleges and incorporates Paragraphs 1-19.

21. Plaintiff conferred substantial benefits upon Defendant by:
    (a) Providing her property at below-market rent for use
        as the primary residence of Defendant's children
        during their father's parenting time;
    (b) Purchasing food, groceries, and household supplies
        for the children;
    (c) Providing childcare, supervision, and emotional
        support for the children;
    (d) Maintaining the property to a child-appropriate
        standard at Plaintiff's expense.

22. Defendant received and retained these benefits. Every
    dollar Plaintiff spent on Defendant's children was a
    dollar Defendant did not spend. Every hour of care
    Plaintiff provided was care Defendant did not arrange
    or pay for.

23. Defendant's retention of these benefits is inequitable
    because:
    (a) Defendant has contributed nothing to Plaintiff;
    (b) Defendant has shown no interest in the children's
        life at Plaintiff's property;
    (c) Defendant has attempted to exclude Plaintiff from
        any role in the children's lives;
    (d) Defendant has disparaged Plaintiff's household and
        the people in it;
    (e) Defendant has the financial means to contribute —
        she is the self-admitted "breadwinner" with full-time
        employment at a financial institution — but chooses
        not to.

24. Under Michigan law, unjust enrichment requires: (1) receipt
    of a benefit by the defendant from the plaintiff; (2) an
    inequity resulting to the plaintiff because of the retention
    of the benefit by the defendant. Barber v. SMH (Pontiac),
    202 Mich App 366 (1993). Both elements are satisfied here.

29. Under Michigan law, unjust enrichment requires: (1) receipt

    inequity resulting to the plaintiff because of the retention
    of the benefit by the defendant. Barber v. SMH (Pontiac),
    202 Mich App 366 (1993). Both elements are satisfied as to
    Defendants Leon A. Smith, Marquita L. Smith, and Simone
