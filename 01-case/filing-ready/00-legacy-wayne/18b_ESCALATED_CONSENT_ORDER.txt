AND RELATED MATTERS (REVISED)

    The parties, having reached agreement, stipulate and consent
to the entry of the following Order:

CUSTODY

1. Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff DaSean Mitchell shall have SOLE
   LEGAL CUSTODY of the minor children:
   - Sahana Saige Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld])
   - Allanna Star Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld])

2. DaSean Mitchell shall have exclusive decision-making authority
   on all major decisions regarding the children's health,
   education, welfare, and residence. Plaintiff shall be notified
   of major decisions within a reasonable time, but Defendant's
   decision shall be final. No prior consultation is required.

3. DaSean Mitchell shall have PRIMARY PHYSICAL CUSTODY. The
   children's primary residence shall be [REDACTED_ADDR_2],
   Lansing, MI 48910, or such other address as Defendant
   determines with fourteen (14) days' notice to Plaintiff.

PARENTING TIME

4. Plaintiff Shayla Smith shall have parenting time every other
   weekend, Friday at 6:00 PM through Sunday at 6:00 PM.

5. Plaintiff's parenting time is contingent upon compliance with
   the terms of this Order. Defendant may suspend Plaintiff's
   parenting time upon written notice for any of the following:
   (a) Plaintiff blocks Defendant's phone number or restricts
       direct communication;
   (b) Plaintiff schedules appointments, events, or activities
       during Defendant's parenting time without 48 hours'
       written notice;
   (c) Plaintiff enrolls, transfers, or withdraws either child
       from any school, program, or activity without Defendant's
       written consent;
   (d) Plaintiff fails to produce the children's vital documents
       as ordered;
   (e) Plaintiff monitors Defendant's household through the
       children's electronic devices or any other means;
   (f) Plaintiff delegates parenting decisions or communications
       to family members or third parties in violation of this
       Order.
   Suspension shall remain in effect until Plaintiff demonstrates
   compliance to Defendant's reasonable satisfaction or obtains
   relief from this Court.

6. Transportation to and from all parenting time exchanges shall
   be provided by Plaintiff. Plaintiff shall pick up the children
   from and return the children to the children's primary
   residence ([REDACTED_ADDR_2], Lansing, MI 48910). Defendant
   shall not be required to transport the children to Plaintiff
   or to meet at any alternate location.

7. Plaintiff shall not exercise parenting time at XXXXX Dresden
   St, Detroit, MI 48205 (her parents' home, which she describes
   as "temporary") until Plaintiff establishes independent
   housing with a signed lease or mortgage, a separate bedroom
   for the children, and a stable address for a minimum of sixty
   (60) consecutive days. Until that time, Plaintiff may exercise
   parenting time at a location approved by Defendant in writing.

8. RIGHT OF FIRST REFUSAL: If Plaintiff is unable to personally
   care for the children during her parenting time for a period
   exceeding twelve (12) hours, she shall first offer that time
   to Defendant before arranging alternative childcare. Defendant
   shall respond within two (2) hours.

9. Each parent shall have the right to daily telephone or video
   contact with the children during the other parent's time, at
   reasonable hours (between 8:00 AM and 8:00 PM).

HOLIDAYS AND SPECIAL DAYS

10. The following holidays shall be alternated annually, with
    Defendant having priority in even-numbered years:
    - Thanksgiving (Wednesday 6:00 PM through Sunday 6:00 PM)
    - Christmas/Winter Break (split: Dec 24 noon through Dec 26
      noon / Dec 26 noon through Dec 28 noon)
    - Spring Break (alternating full weeks)

11. Plaintiff shall have Mother's Day regardless of the regular
    schedule. Defendant shall have Father's Day, the children's
    birthdays, and Defendant's birthday (August 8) regardless of
    the regular schedule.

12. Summer: Plaintiff shall have two (2) non-consecutive weeks
    during the summer, to be selected by June 1 of each year with
    Defendant's approval. Defendant retains all remaining summer
    time.

EDUCATION

13. All education decisions — including school enrollment,
    withdrawal, transfer, tutoring, special education, and
    extracurricular activities — shall be made by Defendant in
    his sole discretion. Plaintiff shall be notified of enrollment
    decisions but her consent is not required.

14. Plaintiff shall not contact, visit, or communicate with any
    school, teacher, administrator, or staff regarding the
    children without Defendant's prior written consent, except
    to attend school events open to all parents.

COMMUNICATION

15. Plaintiff shall unblock Defendant's phone number within
    twenty-four (24) hours of entry of this Order and shall not
    block Defendant at any time thereafter.

16. All communication regarding the children shall be in writing
    (text message or email) unless an emergency requires a phone
    call. Plaintiff shall respond to communications regarding the
    children within twenty-four (24) hours.

17. Plaintiff shall not use family members (including but not
    limited to Simone Smith, Leon Smith, Marquita Smith, or
    Deanna Mitchell), the children, or third parties as
    communication intermediaries for any matter regarding the
    children's health, education, welfare, or schedule.

18. Defendant may designate additional authorized contacts
    (currently Kevin Mok and Heather Hannon) who may communicate
    with Plaintiff on Defendant's behalf regarding scheduling and
    logistics. Plaintiff shall respond to these contacts as she
    would to Defendant.

MEDICAL AND INSURANCE

19. All medical, dental, and mental health decisions shall be
    made by Defendant. Plaintiff shall be notified of non-routine
    medical decisions within a reasonable time, but her consent is
    not required. Routine and emergency care may be obtained by
    either parent.

20. Plaintiff shall maintain the children on her employer-provided
    health insurance and shall provide Defendant with current
    insurance cards and policy information within seven (7) days
    of entry of this Order.

21. Plaintiff shall not schedule medical, dental, or other
    appointments for the children during Defendant's parenting
    time without Defendant's prior written consent.

VITAL DOCUMENTS

22. Within seven (7) days of entry of this Order, Plaintiff shall
    provide Defendant with originals of: birth certificates,
    Social Security cards, immunization records, and health
    insurance cards for both children.

23. Failure to comply with Paragraph 22 within the stated time
    shall constitute contempt of court and shall be grounds for
    immediate suspension of Plaintiff's parenting time.

TRAVEL

24. Plaintiff shall not take or attempt to take the children
    outside the State of Michigan without Defendant's prior
    written consent.

25. Neither parent shall apply for, obtain, possess, or renew a
    passport for either child without the written consent of both
    parents or further order of this Court. Any existing passports
    shall be surrendered to Defendant within seven (7) days.

26. Defendant may take the children outside the State of Michigan
    upon fourteen (14) days' written notice to Plaintiff.
    Plaintiff's consent is not required.

CHILD SUPPORT

27. Child support shall be calculated by the Friend of the Court
    pursuant to the Michigan Child Support Formula, based on both
    parties' verified income.

28. Plaintiff shall file an Amended Verified Statement (FOC 23)
    with complete and accurate income information within seven (7)
    days of entry of this Order. Failure to comply shall result in
    income imputation based on available evidence, including
    Plaintiff's self-described role as "breadwinner."

ACKNOWLEDGMENT OF RELIANCE (ESTOPPEL)

29. Plaintiff acknowledges that during the parties' relationship,
    Defendant relied upon Plaintiff's representations of
    commitment (including two commitment ceremonies) and
    Plaintiff's role as primary earner in forgoing independent
    employment and career development in order to serve as primary
    caregiver to the children.

30. Plaintiff acknowledges this reliance was reasonable and
    resulted in economic disadvantage to Defendant.

DEVICE MONITORING

31. Neither parent shall monitor, record, or intercept the other
    parent's communications through the children's electronic
    devices or any other means.

32. Plaintiff shall not use monitoring software, parental control
    features, or any device capability to surveil Defendant's
    household, conversations, or activities.

SOCIAL MEDIA AND PUBLIC STATEMENTS

33. Neither parent shall post, share, or publish on any social
    media platform any information about the other parent, the
    children, or this case.

34. Neither parent shall permit family members or associates to
    make such posts on their behalf.

OTHER PROVISIONS

35. Neither parent shall disparage the other in the presence of
    the children or permit others to do so.

36. Plaintiff shall not file any motion to modify custody or
    parenting time provisions of this Order for a period of six
    (6) months from the date of entry, absent a showing of
    emergency or danger to the children. This does not restrict
    motions regarding child support.

37. Both parents shall cooperate in maintaining the children's
    relationships with extended family on both sides.

DISCOVERY AND PENDING MOTIONS

38. Upon entry of this Order, all pending discovery shall be
    deemed withdrawn. All pending motions shall be deemed moot.

39. The preservation obligations established by Defendant's
    August 16, 2026 Preservation Requests shall remain in effect
    for twelve (12) months following entry of this Order.


IT IS SO ORDERED.
