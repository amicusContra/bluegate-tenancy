## EMAIL TEMPLATE — TO DISTRICT LEGISLATORS (Carter & Geiss)

SUBJECT: MacDowell Preparatory Academy — Credential Compliance Failures Affecting Your Constituents

---

[Representative Carter / Senator Geiss]:

I am writing to bring to your attention a serious pattern of credential compliance violations at MacDowell Preparatory Academy (Entity Code 82747), a charter school at 4301 Livernois Ave, Detroit — in your district.

**The facts, all from public records:**

1. **Brian Banks** (PIC 287338) — the school's sole administrator — has 9 criminal convictions (ICHAT SID 2029469K), including fraud, larceny, and identity theft. He operates under a fabricated J.D. from a diploma mill.

2. **A MOECS credential audit** I conducted on September 26, 2026 found that between 43% and 86% of MacDowell's instructional staff have credential violations — wrong endorsements, expired credentials, or no teaching credentials at all. Screenshots from the public MOECS database are attached.

3. **MDE's own investigation was killed.** In October 2022, MDE analyst Katie Schmiedeknecht (OEE) opened a certification compliance investigation after receiving an Educator Report Form on Banks. By February 2023, Schmiedeknecht emailed the school's managing company that Banks was "no longer placed at the school" — despite Banks continuing as the sole administrator through the present day. No MCL 388.1763 salary deductions were ever applied.

4. **Academic outcomes are catastrophic.** MacDowell students score 3% proficient in math (vs. 27% statewide). The school receives $4.9 million annually in state aid.

5. **The February 2026 Auditor General Follow-Up Report** identified OEE deficiencies in Findings 2 and 3 (professional development and evaluation compliance). The MacDowell case is a concrete, documented example of the enforcement failures the AG identified.

**What I am asking:**

- A formal legislative inquiry to MDE/OEE requesting all investigation records for MacDowell Prep (Entity 82747) and Banks (PIC 287338)
- An explanation from OEE Director Kelli Brozanski for why the investigation was closed without enforcement
- Verification of whether MCL 388.1763 salary deductions have been applied for uncredentialed staff
- Referral to the Auditor General for review in connection with AG Findings 2 and 3

**Supporting evidence is publicly available at detroit.primals.eco**, including the credential audit, financial analysis, MDE FOIA documents, and complete actor profiles.

Senator Slotkin's office submitted a congressional inquiry to the U.S. Department of Education on September 24, 2026. The OIG received a federal referral on September 22. This matter is escalating, and your constituents' children are the ones being harmed.

I am available to provide any additional documentation. Thank you for your time.
