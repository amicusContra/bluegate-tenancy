# APPEAL 1 — SHAYLA R. SMITH (Case No. 26-4432-PP) — Domestic PPO

## Motion for Reconsideration

**Petitioner DaSean Eddie Mitchell respectfully moves the Court to
reconsider its ruling regarding the Domestic Personal Protection Order
against Respondent Shayla R. Smith and states:**

### 1. Petitioner Is and Has Always Been the Legal Father

An Affidavit of Parentage was signed by both parents at the hospital at
birth for both Sahana Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld]) and Allanna Mitchell
(DOB [date of birth withheld]). Under MCL 722.1004, the AOP has the same effect as a
court order of parentage. Petitioner has been the legal father with full
parental rights and duties since the day each child was born.

This is confirmed by:
- Both children's certified birth certificates listing Petitioner as father
- Respondent's own Complaint for Custody (26-108221-DC, ¶6): "The plaintiff
  and the defendant acknowledged paternity by signing an affidavit of
  parentage"
- Respondent's Answer to Counterclaim (¶1): "paternity was acknowledged"

(See Notice of Correction and Clarification filed September 30, 2026,
and exhibits attached thereto.)

### 2. Respondent Entered Petitioner's Mother's Home When He Had Custody

On September 2, 2026, the children were at Petitioner's mother's home
at XXXX Cornerstone Boulevard. Petitioner had **physical and legal
custody** at that time — the children were in his care, at his family's
home, and no court order existed granting Respondent superior custody
rights. The AOP was in effect. Respondent knew this — she acknowledged
the AOP in her own complaint.

Respondent entered Petitioner's mother's home uninvited. Her own
exhibits show she coordinated the September 2 pickup through
Petitioner's mother Deanna and through the parties' nine-year-old
daughter Sahana — not through Petitioner. She brought her father
Leon Smith, who physically assaulted Petitioner while Respondent
removed the children. This constitutes:

- **MCL 600.2950(1)(a)** — entering premises where Petitioner was present
- **MCL 600.2950(1)(b)** — assault through an agent (Leon Smith)
- **MCL 600.2950(1)(d)** — removing minor children from the legal parent
  who had physical custody at that time

### 3. Respondent's Broader Pattern

Because the AOP was in effect, Respondent also had no unilateral right to:

- **Remove the children** from the family home in February 2026 without
  a court order
- **Change the children's school** to Purpose Charter Academy without
  Petitioner's knowledge or consent
- **Withhold the children** from Petitioner since September 2, 2026 —
  now over 30 days with zero contact, no court order authorizing it

### 3. Petitioner No Longer Feels Safe at His Mother's Home

Petitioner's mother's home at XXXX Cornerstone Boulevard has been a
central location in the children's lives — it is their grandmother's
house. On September 2, 2026, Respondent came to this home uninvited,
accompanied by her father Leon Smith, who physically assaulted Petitioner
while Respondent removed the children.

Since that date, Petitioner has a **reasonable apprehension of violence**
every time he is at his mother's home. He does not know whether
Respondent will send Leon again. Under **MCL 600.2950(1)(i)**, a domestic
PPO may issue to prevent "any other specific act or conduct that imposes
upon or interferes with personal liberty or that causes a reasonable
apprehension of violence."

Petitioner's mother's home — where his daughters should be able to safely
visit their grandmother — has been made unsafe by Respondent's actions.

### 4. Petitioner Acted for the Children at His Own Expense

Petitioner did not file a police report against Leon Smith on September 2
because his daughters were present and he prioritized their safety and
emotional wellbeing over his own legal interests. The Court noted this
response favorably at the September 29 hearing.

Every action Petitioner has taken — seeking custody, filing for PPOs,
trying to learn about his children's school, obtaining birth certificates —
has been the work of a legal father trying to protect and maintain his
relationship with his daughters.

### 5. This Conduct Will Continue Without Court Intervention

There is no indication Respondent will voluntarily change her behavior:

- As of this filing, Petitioner has had **zero contact** with his
  daughters for over 30 days. Respondent has not voluntarily offered
  parenting time or communication.
- On September 22, 2026, Respondent and three family members filed 72
  pages of coordinated legal documents on the same day — an organized
  effort to maintain the status quo.
- Respondent has not produced the Affidavit of Parentage despite
  acknowledging it in her own filings.
- The children remain enrolled at Purpose Charter Academy, a school
  Petitioner was not consulted about and where a 9x felon

Without a PPO, Respondent has no legal obligation to stop withholding
the children, to stop sending Leon Smith to Petitioner's family home,
or to stop interfering with Petitioner's parental rights.

# APPEAL 3 — BRIAN R. BANKS (Case No. 26-4435-PH) — Harassment PPO

## Motion for Reconsideration

**Petitioner DaSean Eddie Mitchell respectfully moves the Court to
reconsider its ruling regarding the Personal Protection Order against
Respondent Brian R. Banks and states:**

### 1. Petitioner Had Standing — FERPA Required the School to Comply

The Affidavit of Parentage establishes Petitioner as the legal father of
the children enrolled at Purpose Charter Academy. Under the **Family
Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), 20 U.S.C. § 1232g**, both
parents have equal rights to access their children's education records
regardless of custody status, unless a court order specifically removes
that parent's rights. **No such court order exists.** Respondent Shayla
Smith admitted under oath (RFA #18) that no custody order has been entered.

At a prior proceeding, FERPA was cited as justification for denying
Petitioner access. With the AOP now established on the record, the
opposite is true: **FERPA required the school to provide Petitioner
access to his children's records.** Mr. Banks's refusal to do so —
including when Petitioner arrived with a police escort — was not a
FERPA-compliant action. It was a FERPA violation.

Respondent Shayla Smith enrolled the children at this school without
Petitioner's knowledge or consent. As the legal father, Petitioner had
every right to inquire about his children's school and the individuals
responsible for their education.

When Petitioner's witness Kevin Mok visited Purpose Charter Academy, he
carried written authorization from Petitioner and documentation of
Petitioner's parental rights. (See Notice of Correction, Section II-E.)

### 2. Who Brian Banks Is

Brian R. Banks is the superintendent of Purpose Charter Academy. He has:

- **Nine felony convictions** (ICHAT SID# 2029469K), including convictions
  related to operation of a criminal enterprise (Exhibit A)
- **No license to practice law** — State Bar of Michigan has no record of
  Brian R. Banks (Exhibit B)
- Told Detroit Police officers on September 9, 2026: "I am an attorney" —
  a violation of MCL 450.681

Respondent Shayla Smith enrolled Petitioner's children at a school run by
this individual without informing Petitioner — the legal father — or
giving him an opportunity to evaluate the school or its leadership.

### 3. Repeated Blocking of Petitioner's Access to His Children

Mr. Banks has personally blocked Petitioner's access to his daughters'
school on more than one occasion:

- When Petitioner's witness Kevin Mok visited Purpose Charter Academy
  with written parental authorization, Mr. Banks refused access.
- **When Petitioner himself visited Purpose Charter Academy accompanied
  by a police escort, Mr. Banks still denied him access.**

Petitioner is a legal father. His name is on both children's birth
certificates. The Affidavit of Parentage was signed at birth and has
been on file with MDHHS since 2017. Petitioner arrived at his
children's school with law enforcement present and was still turned
away by a man with nine felony convictions who has no legal authority
to override a parent's rights.

### 4. Pattern of Harassment Directed at Petitioner

Mr. Banks's conduct extends beyond the school:

- At the September 29, 2026 PPO hearing before this Court, Mr. Banks
  **admitted full knowledge** of the custody dispute
- Mr. Banks **claimed to possess video and signed witness affidavits** —
  evidence he has not produced
- Mr. Banks has made statements **directly to Petitioner in person**
  about Kevin Mok and the custody case, repeatedly inserting himself
  into a family dispute

Mr. Banks has used his position as school superintendent to:
1. Block a legal father from his children's school — even with police present
2. File false police reports against the father's authorized representative
3. Claim attorney status to law enforcement to add credibility to his complaints
4. Repeatedly confront Petitioner about the case in person

A man with nine felony convictions who does all of the above is engaging
in conduct that would cause a reasonable person to feel intimidated and
harassed under **MCL 600.2950a(1)**.

### 4. Claimed Evidence

At the September 29 hearing, Mr. Banks stated he possessed two witnesses
with signed affidavits and video of Kevin Mok's behavior at the school.
Petitioner's witness Mr. Mok has filed a demand for production of this
evidence in Ingham County Case No. 2026-4301-CZ. If this evidence exists,
it should be produced and can be evaluated. If it does not exist, Mr. Banks
made false statements to this Court.
