nation. At any given time, tens of thousands of cases are pending. For
most residents — overwhelmingly Black, overwhelmingly low-income — this
means functionally that they will never get a trial.

### The Mechanism:

than wait 18 months. A criminal defendant pleads to a lesser charge rather
than sit in jail awaiting trial. A homeowner accepts foreclosure rather
than fight the county for years. The backlog IS the punishment.
