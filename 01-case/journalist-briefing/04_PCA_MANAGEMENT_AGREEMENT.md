## THE MONEY: HOW BANKS EXTRACTS PUBLIC FUNDS

### §5.1(a) — The Management Fee: 10% Off the Top

> "For each school year Management Company shall receive as compensation
> for its services a fee equal to **ten percent (10%)** of the Academy's
> Gross SSA."

- Gross SSA = State School Aid minus DPSCD's oversight fee
- FY26-27 State Revenue: **$1,312,000**
- Estimated DPSCD cut (~3%): ~$39,360
- **Estimated Gross SSA: ~$1,272,640**
- **Banks' management fee: ~$127,264/year**

Budget line "General Administration: $125,060" — **THIS IS THE MANAGEMENT FEE**

> "Management Company may also receive as compensation for its services
> a fee equal to **ten percent (10%) of grant funds** that it acquires
> for the Academy"

- Federal revenue: **$150,000**
- **Potential additional fee: up to $15,000**

### §5.3 — Cost Reimbursement: Banks Gets Paid TWICE

> "In addition to the fee described in Section 5.1, the Academy shall
> reimburse Management Company for such costs... Such costs include, but
> are not limited to, **salaries** of Management Company employees,
> curriculum and instructional materials, textbooks, library books,
> computer and other equipment, software, supplies, **marketing and
> development**, food service, transportation, special education,
> psychological services and medical services for students"

So Banks gets:
1. **10% management fee** (~$127K)
2. **10% grant fee** (up to $15K)
3. **Reimbursement for ALL operating costs** he incurs through his LLC

### §9.5 — ALL Staff Are Banks' Employees

> "All teaching, support staff, and other non-teaching personnel
> performing behalf of the Academy shall be employees of **Management Company.**"

Every teacher, every janitor, every administrator = employees of The Purpose Group LLC.
They are NOT employees of the school. Banks is their employer.

### §3.15 — Banks Keeps Extra Program Revenue

> "If approved by the Board, **Management Company may retain the full amount
> of any and all revenue** collected from or for such additional programs"
