### The Allegation (from complaint form, page 3):

Banks used **official Michigan House of Representatives letterhead,
envelopes, postage, and staff** to promote his "Called To Care" event
held in Detroit on May 28, 2016. At the event, Banks and all supporters
and volunteers — **including Representative Sherry Gay-Dagnogo** — wore
"Re-Elect Brian Banks" campaign t-shirts.

**Statutes violated:**
- MCFA §169.247 (political disclaimers)
- MCFA §196.257 (use of public resources for campaign purposes)

### The Evidence (from document, pages 5-31):

**Page 5:** Official Michigan House of Representatives letter on
state letterhead, signed by "Brian Banks, M.Ed., J.D., State
Representative, District 1, Chair, Detroit Caucus" — inviting
churches to the "Called To Care 2016 Health Expo" at East English
Preparatory Academy, 5020 Cadieux, Detroit. Mailed in official
House envelopes using state postage.

**Page 6:** Scan of the actual House of Representatives envelope
used to mail the invitation — state seal, state address, state
postage.

**Page 7:** Photo of Banks at the event podium wearing a "RE-ELECT
BRIAN BANKS" campaign t-shirt, behind a "Thank You Sponsors" sign.

**Page 8:** Photo of Banks wearing campaign t-shirt with another
person at the event.

**Pages 9-31:** Additional photos and documentation of the event
showing campaign t-shirts, sponsor signage, and the blending of
state resources with campaign activity.

### Key Findings:

1. **Banks signed the letter "M.Ed., J.D."** — using the J.D.
   credential on official state letterhead. This is the same
   credential fraud he continues today, just on a state document.

2. **Dagnogo was there wearing his campaign shirt.** The same
   Dagnogo from the FBI wiretap. The same Dagnogo who later
   authorized his charter school. She was actively campaigning
   for him at a state-funded event.

3. **21456 Newcastle Road, Harper Woods** — this was Banks'
   address in 2016. He later moved to 1968 Severn Rd in Grosse
   Pointe Woods. Newcastle Road is a property that was
   quit-claimed in the Banks/Holland property chain.

4. **The event was at East English Preparatory Academy** — a
   school. Banks has been using schools as venues for his
   political operations since at least 2016.

### Banks' Response (pp 18-19, dated June 27, 2016):

Banks called the allegations "frivolous" and argued:
- The event was "NOT a campaign event" — it was FREE
- Campaign t-shirts were "freedom of speech"
- He blamed the complaint on **Mike Norris**, boyfriend & campaign
  manager of **Pamela Sossi**, who was running against Banks in the
  District 1 Democratic primary
- He said **Rosemary Regan** was a supporter/volunteer of Sossi
- **BUT HE ADMITTED**: *"volunteers left the event, in 2 hour
  intervals, to go out in the community/district to canvass"*

### Tim Bowlin Letter (p 20, dated June 7, 2016):

Tim L. Bowlin, Chief Financial Officer and Director of the
House Business Office, cleared Banks, stating: *"it has been
determined that the content of the recent mailing by
Representative Banks is compliant with House policy."*

Added: *"while the House would not sanction the use of state
resources to promote campaign activities, it does not have
the ability to monitor what individuals wear to an event."*

Note: Bowlin later calculated the total expense as **$0.44**
for "1 piece of stationery (.07), 1 manila envelope (.11),

was mailed using state resources.

### Regan's Rebuttal (pp 22-23, dated July 11, 2016):

Rosemary Regan filed an excellent rebuttal:
1. The MCFA makes **no exception** for free events — it
   prohibits use of public resources period
2. Banks himself chose to wear a campaign t-shirt to a
   state-funded event — proving intent
3. In a group of 20 people choosing between 2+ shirts,
   the probability all independently choose the same shirt
   is **0.00000954%** — proving coordination
4. Banks' own admission about canvassing proves campaign intent

### SOS Finding (pp 24-25, dated September 15, 2016):

The Department of State **FOUND A VIOLATION:**

> *"It is uncontroverted that you used House funds or resources
> to mail invitations to the Health Expo. Once you and your
> volunteers, staff, or expo workers wore identical t-shirts

> converted what was an accepted method of constituent outreach
> under House rules — i.e., an event promoting health and
> wellness — into an improper expenditure of public funds

> *"The wearing of the t-shirts advocating your re-election is

The SOS ordered Banks to calculate total House resources expended

169.257(4)) if he didn't cooperate.

### Conciliation Agreement (pp 30-31, signed October 6/14, 2016):

**Banks signed a conciliation agreement** "without admitting any
