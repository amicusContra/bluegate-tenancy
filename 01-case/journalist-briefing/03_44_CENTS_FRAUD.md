## THE SETUP

In Regan v. Banks (SOS Bureau of Elections, 2016), Rosemary Regan
filed a campaign finance complaint: Banks used Michigan House of
Representatives letterhead, envelopes, postage, and staff to mass-
mail invitations to his "Called to Care" health expo — where he and
all his volunteers (including Rep. Sherry Gay-Dagnogo) wore
**"Re-Elect Brian Banks"** campaign t-shirts.

The SOS **found a violation.** Their own ruling said Banks "converted
what was an accepted method of constituent outreach under House rules
into an improper expenditure of public funds under the MCFA."

Then the SOS asked Banks to calculate the cost.

Banks forwarded a memo from **Tim L. Bowlin**, Chief Financial
Officer and Director of the House Business Office, claiming:

```
1 piece of stationery:    $0.07
1 manila envelope:        $0.11
1 minute of staff time:   $0.26
─────────────────────────────
TOTAL:                    $0.44
```

Banks signed a conciliation agreement. **Zero fine.** Zero criminal
referral. Zero penalty. The agreement expired October 2020.

The SOS accepted this.

## WHY $0.44 IS A LIE — SINGLE PIECE MATH

Even if Banks had mailed only ONE letter (he didn't), Bowlin's
calculation is fraudulent on its face:

### Item 1: Stationery — NOT $0.07

Bowlin priced "1 piece of stationery" at $0.07. That's blank
copier paper. Banks sent a **full Michigan House of Representatives
letterhead** with:
- State seal
- Committee assignments
- "Brian Banks, M.Ed., J.D." credential line
- Full formatting, layout, and design
- Printed on House letterhead stock (not blank paper)

| Cost component | Bowlin's number | Real cost |
|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| Letterhead stock (pre-printed) | $0.07 | $0.15–0.30/sheet |
| Design/typesetting of letter | $0.00 (omitted) | Staff time (see below) |
| Printing/copying the letter | $0.00 (omitted) | $0.05–0.15/page |

**Real stationery cost per piece: $0.20–0.45**

### Item 2: Envelope — NOT $0.11

The document shows a **manila envelope with Michigan House
of Representatives return address and state seal** — not a
blank #10 from Office Depot.

| Cost component | Bowlin's number | Real cost |
|---------------|----------------|-----------|
| Pre-printed House envelope | $0.11 | $0.20–0.35/envelope |
| Addressing (label/print) | $0.00 (omitted) | Staff time (see below) |

**Real envelope cost per piece: $0.20–0.35**

### Item 3: Staff Time — NOT 1 MINUTE

Bowlin claimed **1 minute** of staff time at $0.26/minute
($15.60/hour). To produce a mass mailing, staff had to:

| Task | Time (minimum) |
|------|---------------|
| Draft the letter content | 30–60 min |
| Format on House letterhead | 15–30 min |

At $15.60/hr (Bowlin's own rate): **$47–78 in staff time**

At the 2016 Michigan state employee average (~$22/hr with
benefits): **$66–110 in staff time**

**Bowlin didn't include postage at all.**

2016 USPS First-Class letter rate: **$0.47 per piece**

Even for ONE letter, the postage alone ($0.47) exceeds
Bowlin's entire claimed cost ($0.44).
