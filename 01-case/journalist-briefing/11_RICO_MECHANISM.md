### How the Backlog Creates Leverage

**The 3rd Circuit's criminal backlog is not a bug — for
captured judges, it's a feature.**

When 47,000 cases compete for 315 jury verdicts:
1. **99.3% of cases CANNOT go to trial** in a given year
2. **Guilty pleas become the only realistic disposition**
3. **Judges control which cases move and which languish**
4. **Defendants on bond for years may re-offend** (Prosecutor
   Worthy's own observation, WDIV 2021) — creating more cases
5. **Family court matters get pushed further back** — mothers,
   fathers, children wait months or years for resolution
