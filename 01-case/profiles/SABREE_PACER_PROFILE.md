### What a County Treasurer Actually Does (Relevant to Our Network)

- **Certifies** that no delinquent county taxes block a property transfer (appears on warranty deeds, QCDs)
- **Signs forfeiture certificates** when owners fail to pay — property enters county foreclosure pipeline
- **Signs redemption certificates** when owners pay back taxes and recover property
- **Administers** Wayne County tax auctions — one of the largest municipal foreclosure operations in the U.S.

A treasurer has **discretionary judgment** in timing, certification, and enforcement — not merely clerical stamping.

### 5.3 Cross-Contamination — The Endorsement Problem

**Legitimate reasons a treasurer might certify a deed or sign a forfeiture:** statutory duty, no discretion, clerical verification of tax ledger.

**No legitimate reason a treasurer has to endorse a felon's political campaign** while simultaneously:
- Certifying that felon's $232K property purchase
- Signing redemption certificates for a judge on that felon's school development team
- Forfeiting property belonging to the judge who chairs that felon's other school board

The endorsement creates an **appearance of reciprocal benefit** — political loyalty traded for favorable treatment in the tax pipeline. Even if each individual tax action was technically lawful, the **pattern** undermines public confidence and supports discovery into whether enforcement was selective.
