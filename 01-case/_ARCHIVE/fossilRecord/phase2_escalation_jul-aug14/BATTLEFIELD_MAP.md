### What a CMC Is

A Case Management Conference is NOT a trial. It is a status
conference where Judge Triplett reviews the case, addresses
pending motions, and sets the case on a track. She will want
to know:
- What's been filed and what's pending
- Whether the parties have attempted resolution
- What scheduling needs to happen (referee hearing, mediation, etc.)
- Whether there are urgent issues (the pending Temp Order motion)
