## CRITICAL LEGAL CORRECTION

### Michigan RICO Cannot Be Filed as Private Civil Suit

**MCL 750.159u** explicitly bars private civil causes of action under Michigan's racketeering statute. The RICO complaint draft (citing MCL 750.159i) must be restructured:

| Path | Statute | Forum | Damages |
|------|---------|-------|---------|
| **Federal civil RICO** | 18 USC § 1962(c)/(d), § 1964(c) | E.D. Michigan or MI state court | **Treble damages** + attorney fees |
| **State tort claims** | MCL 600.2919a (conversion), common law fraud/conspiracy | Wayne County Circuit | 3x actual damages for conversion |
| **Criminal RICO referral** | MCL 750.159i (AG/prosecutor only) | AG or County Prosecutor | Criminal prosecution, forfeiture |
| **Federal criminal referral** | 18 USC § 1962 | FBI/DOJ | Criminal investigation |

**Action needed:** Swap MCL 750.159i → 18 USC § 1962 in RICO complaint before filing.
