Plaintiff wrote "N/A" for Defendant's employer and income --

obligations.

Defendant was enrolled in DSDT College's VA VET TEC 2.0 program --
a federally funded career training program with a housing allowance

Plaintiff signed the Complaint -- a member of Defendant's support
network informed Plaintiff in person that Defendant would be
attending classes Monday through Wednesday in Detroit. This
conversation occurred at XXXX Cornerstone Blvd in the presence of
multiple members of Plaintiff's family. Plaintiff filed the next
day. Despite knowing Defendant was an enrolled student, Plaintiff
wrote "N/A" for Defendant's occupation. Further, Plaintiff's
proposed parenting time schedule (Option A) assigns Father
parenting time on Wednesday -- the same day Defendant would be in
class in Detroit -- effectively forcing Defendant to choose between
his VA-funded education and time with his children. This schedule
was crafted using information received the previous day. Defendant
is now also employed at Homegrown in Lansing, MI.

Plaintiff rather than being used for the children's food needs.

10. As to all remaining allegations not specifically addressed

AFFIRMATIVE DEFENSES

1. Improper Venue. This action was filed in Wayne County. Neither
Defendant nor the minor children reside in Wayne County. Defendant
and the children reside in Ingham County. Defendant reserves the
right to challenge venue at a later date.

2. Material Omission and Selective Disclosure. Plaintiff's Verified
Statement contains deliberate omissions and selective disclosures,
signed under penalty of perjury. Plaintiff disclosed Defendant's
unemployment ("N/A") while leaving her own income and employer
fields entirely BLANK -- concealing her employment at Lake Trust
Credit Union. Plaintiff also concealed the children's SNAP benefits
from the Court. This pattern of selective disclosure was designed
to create a misleading picture of the parties' relative financial
positions and avoid child support obligations.

3. Best Interests of the Children. The children's best interests,
as evaluated under MCL 722.23, are served by remaining in the
custody of Defendant, who has been their primary caregiver since
birth and who currently provides a stable, documented, and
supportive home environment in Lansing, Michigan.

WHEREFORE, Defendant respectfully requests that this Court deny
Plaintiff's Complaint in its entirety and grant Defendant's
Counterclaim as set forth herein.
