## The Consultation Requirement as a Trust Dial

The "sole legal custody with consultation requirement" is not a
permanent lockout. It is a **graduated trust mechanism**.

**At minimum** (day one, post-order): DaSean makes all major
decisions. Shayla is informed and heard. Her input is received
but she has no veto. This protects the girls from disruption.

**As trust rebuilds**: If Shayla stabilizes — gets therapy,
communicates honestly, contributes constructively, shows up
reliably — DaSean will naturally give her input more weight. He
already consults his daughters on everything. He is a collaborative
decision-maker by nature. If Shayla's input is good, he will
probably take it. Not because a court orders him to. Because he
values it.

**At full trust**: The consultation requirement functions like
joint custody in practice. Shayla's voice carries real weight.
DaSean still has final authority — but if she's healthy and
engaged, he's not overriding her. They're co-parenting.

**The critical difference from actual joint custody**: Shayla
cannot veto, obstruct, or weaponize legal authority. She can't
use joint custody to block a school enrollment, delay a medical
decision, or disrupt the girls' stability. She can only contribute.
If her contribution is good, it's welcomed. If it's destructive,
DaSean has the authority to protect his daughters.

**This is what makes the ask generous, not restrictive**: DaSean
is not asking for the power to exclude Shayla. He is asking for
the power to protect his daughters while including their mother.
The consultation requirement guarantees her voice is always heard.
DaSean's character guarantees it will be valued — if it's worth
valuing.

**For the attorney**: This framing is powerful in court. DaSean
is not seeking to silence the mother. He is seeking authority
with a built-in mechanism for her participation. The dial starts
protective and opens as trust is earned. No judge objects to a
parent who builds in the other parent's voice from day one.
