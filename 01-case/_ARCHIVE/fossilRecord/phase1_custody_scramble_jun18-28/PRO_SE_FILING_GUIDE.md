## CRITICAL: Venue Challenge Must Be Filed With the Answer

**MCR 2.221(A)**: A motion to change venue **must be filed before or
at the time the answer is filed.** If DaSean files his answer without
the venue motion, **the objection is waived permanently.**

**MCR 2.223(A)**: When venue is improper, the court **SHALL** order
a change of venue on timely motion of a defendant. This is mandatory,
not discretionary.

**MCL 600.1651**: The venue statute — only the defendant can move for
change of venue when the plaintiff filed in the wrong county.

**Case law**: *Dawley v. Hall*, 501 Mich. 166 (2018) — only the
defendant or the court on its own initiative can change venue. The
plaintiff cannot. DaSean has the exclusive right to challenge.

**The argument**: Shayla filed in Wayne County. The children currently
reside in Ingham County with DaSean. DaSean resides in Ingham County.
The address on the complaint (17370 Dresden, Detroit) is Shayla's
parents' house, not DaSean's residence. The children were physically
in Ingham County on the date of filing and have been continuously
since early June. Venue is improper in Wayne County.

**If venue is found improper**: the case transfers to Ingham County.
Shayla must pay the $150 filing fee to the receiving court (MCR
2.223). If she doesn't pay within 28 days, the case is dismissed.
