## Factual Basis for Temporary Custody

### 1. Status Quo — DaSean Is the Custodial Parent

The minor children, Sahana Mitchell (DOB [date of birth withheld]) and Allanna
Mitchell (DOB [CONFIRM]), have been residing with their father,
DaSean Mitchell, at [REDACTED_ADDR_2], [REDACTED_CITY] (Ingham
County) continuously since on or about June 13, 2026.

DaSean has been the primary caregiver of both children since birth.
The mother, Shayla Smith, was the household breadwinner (employed
at Lake Trust Credit Union) throughout the children's lives.
DaSean provided full-time childcare, managed all domestic
responsibilities, and was the children's primary attachment figure.

A temporary custody order preserving this status quo is in the
children's best interest because:
- The children are settled in a stable home with their primary
  caregiver
- They have integrated into the neighborhood (friends, activities)
- They are zoned for a significantly higher-rated school (Cavanaugh
  STEAM Elementary, GS 6/10) than the school zone at the mother's
  address (Brenda Scott Academy, GS 3/10)
- Disrupting the current arrangement pending trial would cause
  unnecessary instability for the children

### 2. Undisclosed International Travel — Flight Risk Concern

The mother traveled internationally to **Senegal** in or around
early June 2026 without informing DaSean. The children remained
with DaSean during this trip. DaSean was not told the travel was
international — he learned of it through third parties.

In her Complaint for Custody, the mother specifically requests
**passport authority** for the minor children. This request, viewed
alongside her demonstrated pattern of undisclosed international
travel, creates a reasonable concern that the mother may attempt
to remove the children from the jurisdiction — either domestically
or internationally — without the father's knowledge or consent.

**The combination of undisclosed international travel and a request
for unilateral passport authority establishes a factual basis for
the Court to act now**, before the final hearing, to prevent
potential removal of the children from the jurisdiction.

### 3. No Existing Custody Order

The parties were never legally married. No prior custody order
exists. Currently, neither parent has a court order establishing
custody, parenting time, or child support. This means:

- Either parent could retain the children without legal consequence
- No mechanism exists to compel the return of the children if one
  parent refuses
- The children are at risk of being caught in an extralegal custody
  dispute without court protection

A temporary order eliminates this gap and protects the children.

### 4. Deliberate Service Timing — Father's Day

The mother signed the Complaint on **June 13, 2026**. The Summons
was issued **June 17, 2026**. The mother served Defendant on
**June 21, 2026 — Father's Day** — 8 days after signing.

**The day before signing (on or about June 12, 2026)**: The mother
returned from **international travel to Senegal**. During the
days she was abroad, the children were in the physical custody of
their father, DaSean Mitchell, at his mother's residence (XXXX
Cornerstone Blvd, Detroit). They were **not** at the mother's
parents' residence (17370 Dresden) — the address she listed as
the children's residence on the Complaint. The mother did not
have physical custody of the children for approximately one week
prior to signing the Complaint.

Upon her return from Senegal, the mother came to Cornerstone —
where the children were with their father — and asked Defendant's
support network (Kevin Mok) about Defendant's summer plans for
Lansing. She disclosed nothing about her intent to file. She
gathered intelligence about the father's plans while Kevin was
simultaneously offering her family **housing, free childcare, and
camping trips** for the summer. **She signed the Complaint the
next day.**

The children then moved to Lansing (Ingham County) with their
father. On **June 17**, the mother picked up the daughters from
**Lansing** — demonstrating she knew they were in Ingham County.
She had the issued Summons in hand that day. She did not serve it.
She waited until **June 21 — Father's Day** — and served Defendant
at her parents' residence (17370 Dresden, Detroit) via her sister.

**Summary of deliberate conduct**:
1. Filed claiming children reside at Dresden — they were at
   Cornerstone with their father, and she knew it
2. Intelligence-gathered the day before signing
3. Had the summons June 17, picked up children from Lansing that
   day, chose not to serve
4. Waited for Father's Day to serve — weaponizing the holiday
5. Served at her family's home (her turf), not at neutral ground

This pattern of calculated conduct — combined with the undisclosed
international travel, the income misrepresentation, and the
passport demand — supports the need for immediate judicial
oversight through a temporary order.

### 5. Income Misrepresentation Raises Credibility Concerns

The mother's Verified Statement, signed under penalty of perjury,
lists her income as **"N/A"** despite current employment at Lake
Trust Credit Union — a federally insured financial institution.
This misrepresentation on a sworn court document raises concerns
about the mother's willingness to comply with court processes and
obligations. A temporary order provides judicial oversight during
the pendency of this case.

### 5. Improper Venue

The Complaint was filed in Wayne County. The children reside in
Ingham County. The Defendant resides in Ingham County. A Motion
to Change Venue (MCR 2.223) is being filed simultaneously. A
temporary order from this Court — or from the Ingham County Court
upon transfer — protects the children during the venue challenge.

## Requested Temporary Orders

DaSean Mitchell respectfully requests the Court enter the following
temporary orders pending final hearing:

### A. Temporary Physical Custody

Award temporary primary physical custody of the minor children to
DaSean Mitchell at [REDACTED_ADDR_2], [REDACTED_CITY].

### B. Temporary Legal Custody

Award temporary sole legal custody to DaSean Mitchell, with a
requirement that DaSean consult with the mother on all major
decisions regarding the children's health, education, and welfare.

### C. Temporary Parenting Time for Mother

Order reasonable and generous parenting time for the mother,
including but not limited to:
- Alternating weekends (Friday evening to Sunday evening)
- One weeknight per week
- Shared holidays per standard Friend of the Court guidelines
- Transportation to be provided by the mother to and from the
  children's residence in Ingham County

### D. Mutual Passport Restriction

Order that **neither parent** shall apply for, obtain, or possess
passports for the minor children without written consent of the
other parent and/or further order of the Court.

Order that any existing passports for the minor children be
surrendered to the Court or to the Defendant's attorney pending
resolution of this matter.

### E. Mutual Restraining Provisions

Order that neither parent shall:
- Remove the minor children from the State of Michigan without
  written consent of the other parent or Court order
- Disparage the other parent in the presence of the children
- Interfere with the other parent's parenting time
- Destroy, conceal, or alter any documents, records, or evidence
  relevant to this proceeding

### F. Temporary Child Support

Order temporary child support from the mother to the father per
the Michigan Child Support Formula, based on the mother's
**verified income** from Lake Trust Credit Union — not the "N/A"
reported on her Verified Statement.
