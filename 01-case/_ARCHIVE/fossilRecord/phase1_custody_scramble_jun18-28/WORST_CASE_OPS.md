### Ground Rules

1. **DaSean and Kevin are NEVER hostile.** Warm, calm, firm.

### Scenario: Shayla arrives and says "I'm here to pick up my girls"

**DaSean's response** (calm, friendly, practiced):

> spend some time with them here?"

**If she escalates** ("These are MY children, I'm taking them"):

> "I hear you, and I'm not trying to keep you from seeing them.
> But there's no custody order and no agreed schedule. I'm not
> comfortable with them leaving without a plan for when they come
> back. You're welcome to visit with them here as long as you want."

**If she threatens to call police**:

> "You can absolutely call them. I want to do this the right way
> for everybody."

Police response in a no-order situation: officers will confirm
no custody order exists, document that both parents are present,
and typically advise the parties to resolve it through the court.
They will NOT remove the children from a safe home to hand to the
other parent. Per Cushman: no judge in Michigan grants custody
without a hearing. Police know this.

**If she actually calls police**:
- Kevin stays calm and visible (homeowner, not a party to custody)
- DaSean is cooperative, shows his ID, explains he's the father
- Both parents are legal parents; no order exists
- Officers will likely tell Shayla to file for a custody order
  (which she already has — but no ORDER has been GRANTED)
- Kevin documents the officer's name and badge number afterward
- This interaction actually HELPS DaSean: he was calm, in his
  home, with his children, and she escalated

**If she brings someone with her** (family member, friend):
- Same approach. Stay warm. "Hey, good to see you. Come on in,
  the girls are playing in the yard."
- More witnesses = more people who see DaSean being the calm,
  cooperative parent
