## CLAIMS TO ADD — BRIAN BANKS

### The Core Theory: Hold Him to the Station He Claimed

Banks did not simply lie about being a lawyer. He **acted as one.**
He introduced himself as "Dr. Banks." He told Kevin "I'm a lawyer."
He then reviewed DaSean's parental authorization for approximately
10 seconds, evaluated it as a legal document, and rendered a legal
opinion — that Kevin did not have sufficient authority to be there.
He made this determination from a position of institutional power
(school administrator), after being informed that DaSean was at
work and that there was an active custody case.

This is not a passive lie. This is the unauthorized practice of law
producing concrete harm to three people:

1. **DaSean** — whose fundamental right as a father to participate
   in his children's education was denied by a non-lawyer making a
   legal determination he had no authority to make

2. **Kevin** — whose lawful presence as DaSean's authorized
   representative was blocked by a person falsely claiming legal
   authority

3. **The children** — whose father's involvement was suppressed by
   a school administrator who chose to act as gatekeeper rather
   than facilitator

**The civil accountability frame:** Banks claimed the station of
attorney. He exercised the authority of attorney. He made a legal
decision that caused harm. In the civil suit, we hold him to the
standard of the station he claimed.

If a licensed attorney had done what Banks did — evaluated a
parental authorization for 10 seconds, denied a father's
representative access without legal basis, failed to contact the
father, had a pre-existing relationship with the opposing parent,
and failed to disclose that conflict of interest — that attorney
would face:

- Malpractice (negligent legal evaluation)
- Breach of fiduciary duty (to the children and to DaSean as a
  parent with legal rights at the school)
- Conflict of interest (undisclosed relationship with Shayla)
- Ethics violation (MI Rules of Professional Conduct)

Banks gets no credit for NOT being a lawyer. He claimed to be one.
He acted as one. The harm is real. The civil claims measure that
harm against the standard he held himself out to meet.

The criminal UPL charge (MCL 450.681) runs separately. The civil
claims don't depend on criminal prosecution. They hold Banks
accountable for the consequences of his unauthorized practice.

### Count VI: Unauthorized Practice — Denial of Parental Rights

**Against:** Brian Roderick Banks, individually; The Purpose Group
Michigan LLC; Purpose Charter Academy (as entities whose agent
acted under color of institutional authority)

**Three rights violated:**

**(a) DaSean's right as a father to participate in his children's
education.**

A parent has a fundamental constitutional right to direct the
upbringing and education of their children (Troxel v. Granville,
530 U.S. 57 (2000)). Michigan law provides that both parents have
equal access to school records, school staff, and school events
regardless of custodial status (MCL 380.1137). No custody order
exists restricting DaSean's rights. Banks, as school administrator,
had a legal duty to facilitate — not obstruct — both parents'
involvement.

Banks denied DaSean's exercise of this right by:
- Evaluating DaSean's signed parental authorization
- Rendering a legal opinion that it was insufficient
- Blocking Kevin from accessing information about the children
- Doing so without contacting DaSean to verify

He did this while claiming to be a lawyer. He had no legal
authority to evaluate the document or render any opinion on its
legal sufficiency.

**(b) DaSean's right to designate a representative.**

A person has the right to designate an agent to act on their
behalf. DaSean signed a written authorization for Kevin, referencing
the active custody case (26-108221-DC), the children's names and
dates of birth, and DaSean's contact information. Banks evaluated
that document — making a legal determination about its validity —
and rejected it. He had no legal training, no legal license, and
no legal authority to make that determination. The rejection was
an unauthorized legal act that denied DaSean's right to designate
his own representative.

**(c) Kevin's right to act under DaSean's authorization.**

Kevin was at PCA lawfully, at a public event (Back to School Bash),
carrying signed authorization from a parent with active legal
rights at the school. Banks denied Kevin's access by falsely
claiming legal authority. This is tortious interference with
Kevin's authorized role — and the interference was accomplished
through fraud (the false attorney claim) and institutional power
(Banks' position as school administrator).

**The malpractice measure:**

Banks claimed to be an attorney. He acted as an attorney. The civil
damages are measured against the standard he held himself out to
meet. A licensed attorney who:

| Malpractice element | What Banks did |
|--------------------|----|
| Duty of care | Claimed "I'm a lawyer" — assumed the duty |
| Negligent evaluation | Reviewed authorization for ~10 seconds, rejected it without legal basis |
| Failure to consult | Did not call DaSean (number was on the authorization) |
| Conflict of interest | Pre-existing relationship with Shayla (she enrolled children there; Banks called her immediately after) |
| Non-disclosure | Did not disclose his relationship with Shayla or his lack of license |
| Harm | DaSean's parental rights denied; Kevin's authorized role blocked; children's father excluded from school event |

**Damages available:**
- Actual damages (travel, time, lost access to the children's
  school event, costs of re-efforts, emotional distress)
- Exemplary/punitive damages (20-year pattern of the same false
  claim; 8 felony convictions for fraud; deliberate and willful)
- Injunction (requiring PCA to recognize DaSean's parental rights
  and Kevin's authorization)

**Damage estimate:**

| Category | Basis | Range |
|----------|-------|-------|
| Actual | Travel (Lansing→Detroit round trip), time, emotional distress, re-efforts, lost access | $5,000-15,000 |
| Malpractice-equivalent | Measured against what a licensed attorney's negligence would cost — denied parental access, conflict of interest, no consultation | $25,000-75,000 |
| Exemplary/punitive | 20-year pattern, 8 felonies, willful, from position of institutional authority over children | $50,000-150,000 |

## THE TIMELINE — DRAGGING IT OUT IS THE STRATEGY

Kevin is not in a hurry. Every month the case is open:

1. **More discovery flows in** — subpoenas produce new records,
   new records reveal new patterns, new patterns support new
   claims or referrals

2. **Defendants' legal costs compound** — each discovery response,
   each motion, each hearing costs them money. Kevin pays nothing.

3. **Criminal referrals mature** — State Bar complaint, DPSCD
   complaint, AG referral on campaign finance, OIG complaint on
   mail — each one generates independent investigation that may
   produce admissible evidence for the civil case

4. **Press coverage escalates** — Elrick publishes, public
   pressure builds, regulatory attention increases, new sources
   come forward

5. **New defendants can be added** — as discovery reveals new
   actors (Banks' associates, Purpose Group directors, board
   members who facilitated his hire), they can be amended into
   the complaint

6. **Banks' entities deteriorate** — public exposure of 3% math
   scores, felon principal, sham charity history will affect
   enrollment, which affects per-pupil funding, which affects
   Banks' revenue stream

### What "winning" looks like

Kevin's case is "first in, last out." It never settles. But that
doesn't mean it doesn't produce value:

- **Every dollar recovered goes to Deanna** — Kevin has stated
  this. The judgment, when collected, supports the family
  member who needs it most.

- **Discovery feeds other cases** — DaSean's custody case,
  Deanna's civil case, the children's case all benefit from
  what Kevin's subpoenas uncover.

- **Regulatory action has its own value** — if Banks loses his
  admin cert, if PCA loses its charter, if the AG investigates
  the campaign finance violations — those outcomes don't
  produce cash but they protect the community.

- **The process is the punishment** — for defendants who cannot
  afford ongoing litigation, the pressure of Kevin's case
  creates settlement leverage in the OTHER cases (Deanna's,
  DaSean's). Kevin's willingness to never settle is what makes
  settlement attractive on the other fronts.
