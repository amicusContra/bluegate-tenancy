## WHY THIS WORKS

### Different plaintiffs, different standing
- **Kevin** was eavesdropped on, had his mail intercepted, was enriched against. His harm is independent.
- **Deanna** had her EBT stolen, her labor exploited, her housing endangered. Her harm is independent.
- **The children** had their identities used for fraud, their documents withheld. Their harm is independent.
- **DaSean** has the custody case — his standing is parental rights.

Each person has their own cause of action. This is not one case split into four — these are four genuinely separate injuries inflicted by the same family on different victims.

### Different courts, different judges
- **Ingham County** — Kevin's suit + Children's suit + DaSean's civil suit (all three: DaSean and kids live at [REDACTED_ADDR_2], Kevin at [REDACTED_ADDR_1], Lansing). The Smiths defend THREE separate civil cases in Lansing — three dockets, three judges, three response deadlines.
- **Wayne County** — Deanna's suit + DaSean's custody/estoppel + estoppel motion. Different judges, different dockets, different discovery schedules.
- **Federal** — OIG investigation runs on its own timeline, outside the Smiths' control entirely.
- **State** — MDHHS investigation is administrative, not court. No ability to "defend" it — it's an audit.

The Smiths are defending in TWO counties simultaneously across TEN fronts. Ingham hearings don't align with Wayne hearings. They need counsel in both jurisdictions.

### The Smiths cannot efficiently defend
- One attorney cannot represent all four defendants (Shayla, Leon, Marquita, Simone) because their interests conflict:
  - Marquita's best defense is "Shayla told me to pick up the mail" → implicates Shayla
  - Leon's best defense is "I didn't know about any of this" → implicates Marquita
  - Shayla's best defense is "my parents handled it, not me" → implicates Leon and Marquita
  - Simone's best defense is "I'm a victim too" → implicates everyone
- Each defendant needs their own attorney for each case they're named in
- At minimum: 4 defendants × 4 civil cases = 16 attorney retainers (Kevin, Deanna, Children, DaSean civil)
- Plus the custody case attorney, the OIG response, the MDHHS response
- Estimated legal costs for the Smiths: $60,000-$150,000 in attorney fees before trial

### Discovery compounds across cases
- Kevin's Ingham subpoenas (Lake Trust, USPS, phone records) → data feeds Deanna's Wayne case
- Deanna's Wayne subpoenas (EBT records, Walmart invoices, Medicaid records) → data feeds Kevin's Ingham case
- Children's Ingham subpoenas (SSN usage history, benefits applications) → data feeds MDHHS complaint
- DaSean's Ingham subpoenas (bank records, employment records, safe contents) → data feeds all Wayne cases
- MDHHS investigation findings → admissible evidence in all civil cases
- OIG investigation findings → admissible evidence in all civil cases

Each case is a shovel. Each discovery response is dirt. The hole gets deeper from six directions simultaneously.
