STIPULATED ORDER REGARDING CUSTODY, PARENTING TIME,
CHILD SUPPORT, AND PROTECTIVE PROVISIONS

The parties, Shayla Smith (Plaintiff) and DaSean Mitchell
(Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff), having reached an agreement on
all matters of custody, parenting time, child support, and
related issues, and the Court having reviewed the file and being
otherwise advised in the premises,

THE COURT FINDS:

A. Plaintiff engaged in electronic eavesdropping in violation of
MCL 750.539c by monitoring and intercepting private communications
between Defendant and a third party through the minor children's
electronic devices, as admitted in Plaintiff's own sworn filing
dated August 12, 2026.

B. A member of Plaintiff's household, Simone Smith, intercepted
United States Postal Service Certified Mail Restricted Delivery
addressed solely to Plaintiff on at least two occasions (August
21, 2026), in potential violation of 18 USC 1702 and 18 USC 1708.

C. Plaintiff has demonstrated a pattern of using family members
as intermediaries and proxies for legal, custodial, and personal
interactions rather than engaging directly with Defendant,
including: directing Simone Smith to serve custody papers on
Defendant on Father's Day at the Smith family residence rather
than serving Defendant personally; and permitting Simone Smith
to sign for Plaintiff's restricted delivery mail rather than
receiving her own legal correspondence.

D. These findings support the conclusion that the minor children's
best interests, as defined by MCL 722.23, are served by the
provisions below.

IT IS HEREBY ORDERED:

CUSTODY

1. SOLE LEGAL CUSTODY is awarded to Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff
DaSean Mitchell. Defendant shall have final decision-making
authority on all major decisions regarding the minor children's
health, education, religion, extracurricular activities, and
general welfare.

2. SOLE PHYSICAL CUSTODY is awarded to Defendant/Counter-Plaintiff
DaSean Mitchell. The minor children's primary residence shall be
[REDACTED_ADDR_2], [REDACTED_CITY], or such other residence as
Defendant may establish.

3. The children's secondary residence shall be XXXX Cornerstone
Blvd, Detroit, MI 48201 (paternal grandmother, Deanna Mitchell).

MENTAL HEALTH EVALUATION

4. Plaintiff shall complete a comprehensive psychological
evaluation conducted by a licensed psychologist or psychiatrist
approved by the Court or by stipulation of both parties within
sixty (60) days of entry of this Order. The evaluation shall
include, at minimum:
    (a) Clinical interview;
    (b) Standardized psychological testing;
    (c) Assessment of parenting capacity;
    (d) Assessment of boundary recognition and impulse control;
    (e) Evaluation of Plaintiff's pattern of using third parties
        as proxies for direct interpersonal interactions.

5. The evaluator's report and recommendations shall be provided
to the Court, Defendant, and the Friend of the Court. The
evaluator's recommendations regarding treatment, therapy, or
further evaluation shall be binding conditions for any
modification of parenting time under this Order.

6. Plaintiff shall execute all necessary releases to permit the
evaluator to obtain records relevant to the evaluation, including
but not limited to medical, mental health, and substance abuse
records.

PARENTING TIME — PHASE 1: SUPERVISED

7. Until Plaintiff has completed the psychological evaluation
required by Paragraph 4 and the evaluator has provided written
clearance for unsupervised contact, Plaintiff's parenting time
shall be SUPERVISED.

8. Supervised parenting time shall occur at a neutral, Court-
approved location. Parenting time shall NOT occur at XXXXX
Dresden St, Detroit, MI 48205 or at any residence occupied by
Simone Smith. Parenting time shall NOT occur at any location
outside the State of Michigan.

9. Supervised parenting time shall be:
    (a) Every other Saturday, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM;
    (b) At a neutral location agreed upon by both parties or
        designated by the Court or Friend of the Court;
    (c) Supervised by a professional supervisor approved by the
        Court, or by a neutral adult agreed upon by both parties
        in writing. Members of Plaintiff's immediate family
        (Simone Smith, Leon Smith, Marquita Smith) shall NOT
        serve as supervisors.

10. Plaintiff shall bear the cost of professional supervision.

PARENTING TIME — PHASE 2: GRADUATED UNSUPERVISED

11. Upon written clearance from the evaluator under Paragraph 5,
and upon Plaintiff's completion of any recommended treatment
program, Plaintiff may petition the Court for modification to
unsupervised parenting time. The Court shall consider:
    (a) The evaluator's written clearance and recommendations;
    (b) Plaintiff's compliance with all terms of this Order;
    (c) Plaintiff's demonstrated ability to communicate directly
        with Defendant without intermediaries;
    (d) The children's expressed preferences, if age-appropriate;
    (e) Whether Plaintiff has engaged in any further monitoring
        or interception of Defendant's communications.

12. If unsupervised parenting time is granted, it shall begin as:
    (a) Alternating weekends (Saturday 10:00 AM to Sunday 5:00 PM);
    (b) Graduating to alternating weeks after six (6) months of
        compliance, upon further petition to the Court.

RESTRICTED PERSONS

13. Simone Smith shall have NO role in custody exchanges, parenting
time supervision, transportation of the minor children, or
communication between the parties regarding the children. Simone
Smith shall not be present at custody exchanges. Simone Smith
shall not serve, deliver, or receive legal documents, mail, or
communications on behalf of either party.

14. Custody exchanges shall NOT occur at XXXXX Dresden St, Detroit,
MI 48205 (Leon and Marquita Smith residence). Exchanges shall
occur at a neutral public location (police station, fire station,
or other agreed location) or at Defendant's designated residence.

15. Leon Smith and Marquita Smith may have contact with the minor
children during Plaintiff's supervised parenting time, provided
such contact occurs at the approved supervised location and under
the supervision required by this Order.

COMMUNICATION

16. Plaintiff shall communicate directly with Defendant regarding
the children. Plaintiff shall NOT use Simone Smith, Leon Smith,
Marquita Smith, or any other family member as an intermediary
for custody-related communications.

17. If direct communication between the parties is not feasible,
all communication shall be routed through Kevin Mok (Defendant's
designated representative) or through the OurFamilyWizard
application (or similar Court-approved co-parenting application).

18. Neither parent shall monitor, record, or intercept the other
parent's private communications by any means, including through
the children's electronic devices, third parties, or any
electronic surveillance.

19. Violation of Paragraph 18 shall constitute grounds for
modification of custody and parenting time and may be referred
by the Court for criminal investigation under MCL 750.539c.

ELECTRONIC DEVICES

20. Plaintiff shall not install monitoring software, enable
location tracking beyond standard parental controls, or access
the children's devices for the purpose of monitoring Defendant's
communications during Defendant's parenting time.

21. The children's electronic devices shall not be used as a
conduit for communication between the parties regarding
scheduling, logistics, or case-related matters.

VITAL DOCUMENTS

22. Within fourteen (14) days of entry of this Order, Plaintiff
shall provide Defendant with originals or certified copies of:
birth certificates, Social Security cards, immunization records,
health insurance cards, and school enrollment records for both
minor children.

23. Plaintiff shall not apply for, obtain, or use the children's
Social Security numbers, birth certificates, or identifying
information for any purpose not directly related to the
children's welfare without Defendant's written consent.

SCHOOL ENROLLMENT

24. The minor children shall be enrolled in and attend school in
the Lansing School District, consistent with their primary
residence. The children shall be enrolled for the 2026-2027
school year within thirty (30) days of entry of this Order, or
at the start of the next enrollment period, whichever is sooner.

25. All education decisions shall be made by Defendant pursuant to
his sole legal custody.

TRAVEL AND PASSPORT

26. Neither parent shall take the children outside the State of
Michigan without fourteen (14) days' prior written notice to the
other parent.

27. Neither parent shall apply for or obtain a passport for either
child without the written consent of both parents or further
order of this Court.

CHILD SUPPORT

28. Child support shall be calculated by the Friend of the Court
pursuant to the Michigan Child Support Formula, based on both
parties' verified income.

29. Plaintiff shall file an Amended Verified Statement (FOC 23)
with complete and accurate income information within fourteen (14)
days of entry of this Order.

30. Defendant waives retroactive child support for the period prior
to entry of this Order.

ACKNOWLEDGMENTS

31. Plaintiff acknowledges that she monitored and intercepted
private communications between Defendant and a third party
through the minor children's electronic devices, and that this
conduct was admitted in her sworn filing dated August 12, 2026.

32. Plaintiff acknowledges that during the parties' nine-year
relationship, Defendant served as primary caregiver to the minor
children, maintained the household, and forgone independent
employment and career development in reasonable reliance on
Plaintiff's representations of commitment.

33. These acknowledgments do not constitute a waiver of any
separate civil claims by either party.

ENFORCEMENT

34. Violation of any provision of this Order may result in a
finding of contempt and sanctions including but not limited to:
modification of parenting time, fines, and referral for criminal
investigation where applicable.

35. The Court retains jurisdiction to enforce and modify this
Order.

RESOLUTION OF PENDING MATTERS

36. Upon entry of this Order, all pending motions in this case
are deemed resolved. Plaintiff's Complaint for Custody and
Defendant's Counterclaim for Custody are both resolved by this
stipulated agreement.

37. The preservation obligations established by Defendant's
August 16, 2026 Preservation Requests shall remain in effect
for six (6) months following entry of this Order.


IT IS SO ORDERED.
