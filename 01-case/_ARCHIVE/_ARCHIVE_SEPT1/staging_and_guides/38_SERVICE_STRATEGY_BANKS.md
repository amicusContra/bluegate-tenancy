## THE RULES

**MCR 2.105(A)(1):** Personal service on an individual —
deliver a copy of the summons and complaint to the defendant
personally, or serve a person of suitable age at the
defendant's usual place of residence or business.

**MCR 2.105(D):** Service on an LLC or corporation — serve
an officer, director, resident agent, or any person in charge
of the office.

**MCR 2.102(D):** 91 days from filing to complete service.

**MCR 2.108:** 21 days to answer after service.

## WHAT BANKS FACES AFTER SERVICE

1. **Two separate answer deadlines** — 21 days each from
   service. Miss either one → default judgment on that case.

2. **Two separate discovery tracks** — interrogatories,
   document requests, depositions in each case.

3. **Two separate default judgment opportunities** — if he
   ignores one case, the other still proceeds.

4. **No unified settlement** — settling the Smith conspiracy
   doesn't touch the independent UPL/Fraud/IIED claims.
   Settling the Banks case doesn't release the Smith
   conspiracy. He must deal with each separately.

5. **Corporate counsel mandate** — Purpose Group and PCA
   cannot appear pro se. They MUST hire attorneys. Banks
   personally pays while his entities bleed separate
   retainers.

6. **Staff awareness** — PCA staff witnessed the school
   serve. Word spreads. Morale drops. Teachers update
   their resumes. DPSCD notices.
