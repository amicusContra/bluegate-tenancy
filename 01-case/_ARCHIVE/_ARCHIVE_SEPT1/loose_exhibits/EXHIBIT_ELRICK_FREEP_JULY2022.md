"Here's the record Brian Banks doesn't want you to know"
By M.L. Elrick, Detroit Free Press
Published: July 3, 2022

---

Every time I write about Brian Banks, I have to ask: "Was he
convicted of eight felonies — or nine?"

When I heard that this prolific lawbreaker and former lawmaker
is running for Wayne County commissioner, my first thought —
at least that's fit for print — was that I should check his
record to be sure how many times he has pleaded guilty to a
crime.

[KEY EXCERPTS — CRIMINAL RECORD]

Banks had been convicted of multiple felonies involving bad
checks and unpaid bills.

Between 1998 and 2004, Banks was convicted eight times for
felonies in which he wrote dishonored checks and committed
credit card fraud.

[KEY EXCERPTS — FBI WIRETAP]

Banks was one of several public officials the FBI was
suspicious of while running a wiretap on towing titan Gasper
Fiore's phone in 2016. He was described in an application for
the wiretap as one of a dozen "target subjects."

An affidavit written by FBI Special Agent Bob Beeckman
includes excerpts from transcripts of several conversations
between Banks and Fiore in which they discuss Banks' efforts
to help Fiore secure a Michigan Department of Transportation
contract.

Beeckman wrote: "I believe that 'paperwork' in this context
means a cash bribe payment."

[KEY EXCERPTS — SEXUAL HARASSMENT]

His relationship with legislative aide Tramaine Cotton was
problematic on multiple levels. Cotton [...] sued Banks,
alleging sexual harassment. He accused Banks of pressuring
him to engage in sex acts.

The lawsuit was settled in late 2015 when the state of
Michigan [...] paid Cotton about $12,000. Taxpayers still
had to foot a $85,000 legal bill to defend Banks.

[KEY EXCERPTS — CHARITY FRAUD]

The IRS dissolved The Right Turn Project in 2019.

Campaign finance reports show that Michigan Deserves Better
[...] gave The Right Turn Project $20,000 in 2015, $2,700
in 2016 and $2,000 in 2018. Operating Engineers Local 324
PAC gave $1,000 in 2019.

[KEY EXCERPTS — CAMPAIGN FINANCE]

Bank on Banks for Michigan political action committee [...]
owing $14,500 in fines for failing to file campaign finance
reports. On May 12, the Michigan Secretary of State referred
the PAC to the attorney general.

[KEY EXCERPTS — OUTFRONT MEDIA]

In 2018, while running for the state Senate, Banks tried to
give Outfront Media a $6,875 check for billboards. But the
check bounced. [...] A consent judgment of about $9,000 was
entered against Banks.

Banks was subpoenaed to appear with financial records at a
May 25 hearing. He never showed.

[KEY EXCERPTS — ENDORSEMENTS]

Banks' endorsers include Mayor Mike Duggan, Wayne County
Executive Warren Evans, Wayne County Treasurer Eric Sabree,
Detroit City Council President Mary Sheffield, and state
Sen. Stephanie Chang.

Duggan: "He's a hard worker who focuses on constituent
service."

[KEY EXCERPTS — UPL PATTERN]

Banks "has claimed in the past to be an attorney."
