ADDITIONAL FACTUAL BACKGROUND

3. DaSean Mitchell is the father of Sahana Saige Mitchell (DOB:
   [date of birth withheld]) and Allanna Star Mitchell (DOB: [date of birth withheld]). DaSean Mitchell is a party to Case No. 26-108221-DC
   in the Wayne County Circuit Court, a contested custody action
   filed by Defendant Shayla Smith. DaSean Mitchell filed a
   Counterclaim for Custody on July 14, 2026.

4. On or about August 29, 2026, DaSean Mitchell executed a
   written authorization designating Plaintiff Kevin Mok as his
   authorized representative for purposes of attending school
   events, obtaining school information, and acting on DaSean
   Mitchell's behalf regarding the children's education. The
   authorization expressly referenced the active custody case
   (No. 26-108221-DC).

5. On August 29, 2026, Plaintiff traveled to Purpose Charter
   Academy to attend PCA's "Back to School Bash" as DaSean
   Mitchell's authorized representative. Upon arrival, Plaintiff
   presented himself and the authorization to PCA staff.
   Defendant Banks approached Plaintiff, introduced himself as
   "Dr. Banks," reviewed the authorization for approximately
   ten (10) seconds, and stated: "I'm a lawyer." Based on this
   self-proclaimed legal authority, Defendant Banks rendered a
   legal opinion that the parental authorization was
   insufficient and initially denied Plaintiff access to the
   school event. Defendant Banks is not a lawyer. He has never
   been admitted to the Michigan Bar.

The Coordinated Response

6. Upon information and belief, Defendant Shayla Smith was
   physically present at Purpose Charter Academy on August 29,
   2026 during Plaintiff's visit. DaSean Mitchell's father,
   Leon Smith (a Defendant in this action), confirmed during a
   conversation with DaSean Mitchell on August 30, 2026 that
   Shayla and the children saw Plaintiff at the school.
   Plaintiff — a white man in professional attire arriving in
   a conspicuous vehicle at a Detroit charter school — was
   readily identifiable.

7. Within hours of the August 29, 2026 encounter at PCA,
   Defendant Leon Smith was texting DaSean Mitchell about the
   visit — demanding that DaSean's representative "back off"
   and accusing DaSean of disrespect.

8. Defendant Shayla Smith had firsthand knowledge of Plaintiff's
   visit. She was present. She saw Plaintiff. She could have
   approached Plaintiff and communicated the school information
   she had promised to provide on August 11, 2026 ("school
   information will be communicated directly to their father").
   Instead, the response was hostile intimidation through her
   father, Leon Smith, directed at DaSean Mitchell — not
   cooperation.

9. Whether the communication chain ran from Banks to Shayla to
   Leon, or from Shayla directly to Leon after observing
   Plaintiff herself, or both — the result was the same:
   coordinated suppression of DaSean Mitchell's parental
   participation. Banks denied access using false legal
   authority. Shayla, who was present and witnessed the
   encounter, did not intervene, did not correct Banks' false
   legal claim, and did not facilitate DaSean Mitchell's
   parental rights. Instead, Leon Smith delivered hostile
   messages within hours. The coordination is evidenced by the
   conduct, the timing, and the uniformity of purpose.

10. This coordination is part of a broader pattern. Defendant
    Shayla Smith enrolled the children at PCA on July 22,
    2026 — eight (8) days after DaSean Mitchell's Counterclaim
    for Custody was filed. She never told DaSean the name or
    location of the school. When DaSean's representative
    appeared, Banks denied access using false legal authority,
    and Shayla — present at the scene — allowed it to happen
    without objection. The relationship between Banks and
    Shayla Smith was not incidental.

11. On August 30, 2026, Defendant Shayla Smith's father, Leon
    Smith, told DaSean Mitchell that PCA is "a better school"
    than the children's prior school (Michigan Math and Science
    Academy). This claim is objectively false — MMSA has 11%
    math proficiency; PCA, a Year 1 school with no academic
    record, has 3% proficiency at its predecessor campus. The
    Smith family's active defense of Banks' institution
    demonstrates their investment in the Banks-Smith alignment
    and their willingness to repeat false claims to justify it.

COUNT VII — CIVIL CONSPIRACY
(Against Defendants Brian Roderick Banks and Shayla Smith)

12. Plaintiff re-alleges and incorporates all preceding
    paragraphs and all paragraphs of the original Complaint.

13. Defendants Banks and Shayla Smith, acting in concert, engaged
    in a civil conspiracy to deny DaSean Mitchell his parental
    rights and to deny Plaintiff his authorized representative
    role, through the following coordinated actions:

    (a) Defendant Shayla Smith enrolled the children at PCA on
        July 22, 2026 — eight days after DaSean Mitchell's
        Counterclaim for Custody — without informing DaSean of
        the school's name, location, or any enrollment details;

    (b) Defendant Shayla Smith never disclosed the enrollment
        to DaSean Mitchell despite her own August 11, 2026
        promise that "school information will be communicated
        directly to their father";

    (c) Defendant Banks denied Plaintiff access at PCA on
        August 29, 2026, using false legal authority, without
        any inquiry into whether DaSean Mitchell — the father —
        had authorized the visit;

    (d) Defendant Shayla Smith was physically present at PCA
        during the August 29 encounter. She witnessed
        Plaintiff's visit and Banks' denial of access. She
        did not intervene. She did not correct Banks' false
        claim of legal authority. She did not facilitate
        DaSean Mitchell's parental rights. She had promised
        on August 11, 2026 to communicate school information
        to the children's father — and when his representative
        appeared, she allowed a convicted felon to deny him
        access using false legal authority;

    (e) Within hours of the August 29 encounter, Defendant
        Leon Smith (Shayla's father, a Defendant in this
        action) was texting DaSean Mitchell hostile messages
        demanding that DaSean's representative "back off."
        Whether Shayla communicated directly with Leon after
        witnessing the encounter, or Banks communicated with
        Shayla who then contacted Leon, or both — the result
        was coordinated intimidation, not cooperation;

    (f) Defendant Leon Smith stated on August 30, 2026 that
        PCA is "a better school" than the children's prior
        school — a false claim demonstrating the Smith family's
        active defense of and investment in Banks' institution;

    (g) The coordination of enrollment concealment, denial of
        access, presence without intervention, and immediate
        hostile response demonstrates a pre-existing
        relationship between Banks and Shayla Smith that was
        deployed to suppress DaSean Mitchell's parental
        participation.

14. The conspiracy between Banks and Shayla Smith amplifies the
    liability of each. Under Michigan law, each co-conspirator
    is jointly and severally liable for the tortious acts of the
    other committed in furtherance of the conspiracy. Admiral
    Ins. Co. v. Columbia Cas. Ins. Co., 194 Mich App 300 (1992).

15. Banks is jointly and severally liable for all damages arising

    I through VI of the original Complaint), to the extent those

    representative and family member.

16. Shayla Smith is jointly and severally liable for all damages
    arising from Banks' tortious acts against Plaintiff, to the

    conspiracy.

17. The conspiracy described herein is part of the broader
    pattern of coordinated action described in Count VI of the
    original Complaint, and the addition of Defendant Banks as

    include his use of false legal authority and institutional
