- 2024-2025

### 2. Annual Career Authorizations (ACA)

All **Annual Career Authorization** applications submitted by MacDowell Preparatory Academy to the Michigan Department of Education, for all school years from 2020-2021 through 2025-2026, including:

- Assignment (subject/grade)
- Date of application
- Date of approval or denial
- Whether a mentor teacher was assigned
- Whether the individual was enrolled in an approved teacher preparation program
- Duration/renewal history of each ACA

### 3. Permits and Authorizations

All **permits, emergency authorizations, or other exceptions** granted to MacDowell Preparatory Academy to employ non-certificated or non-endorsed teachers, for all school years from 2020-2021 through 2025-2026, including any permits issued under MCL 380.1233b or MCL 380.1531.

## FEE WAIVER REQUEST

I request a waiver of fees pursuant to MCL 15.234(1), as disclosure of this information is in the public interest. MacDowell Preparatory Academy is a publicly funded charter school receiving approximately $5 million annually in state per-pupil funding. Published data (U.S. News Education) indicates that only 26% of the school's full-time teachers hold valid certifications. Public accountability for the use of taxpayer funds and the quality of education provided to 362 predominantly low-income, minority students requires transparency regarding teacher qualifications.

If a fee waiver is not granted, please advise me of the estimated cost before processing the request. I am willing to pay reasonable fees up to $50.00 without prior notification.
