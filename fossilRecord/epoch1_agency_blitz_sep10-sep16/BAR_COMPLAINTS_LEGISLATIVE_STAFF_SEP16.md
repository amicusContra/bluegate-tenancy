### A. Identification

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| **Name** | Brian Schmidtke, Esq. |
| **Bar Status** | Active (to be verified via State Bar directory) |
| **Role** | Legislative staff member under State Rep. Brian Banks (2013-2017) |
| **Book Citation** | Named in Acknowledgments, p. 4 |

### B. Book Evidence

Banks names Schmidtke in his Acknowledgments as a staff member:

> "To my staff and interns — Brian Schmidtke, Esq., Ron Kelly,
> Rebecca Fadel, J.D., Kyle Giallonardo, J.D., Justin Gardner,
> and Jazmine Fews."
— Acknowledgments, p. 4

Banks identifies Schmidtke as "Esq." — confirming Schmidtke is
a licensed attorney. Schmidtke worked directly under Banks in
the Michigan House of Representatives from approximately 2013
to February 2017 (Banks' resignation).

### C. What Schmidtke Knew

As a licensed attorney working in a state legislator's office,
Schmidtke would have known:

1. **Banks' felony history** — publicly documented and covered
   in media (Elrick, Detroit Free Press; Morse podcast)
2. **Banks' lack of bar admission** — as a bar member himself,
   Schmidtke could verify bar status in seconds
3. **Banks' use of "J.D."** — Banks used this credential
   throughout his legislative career
4. **The distinction between J.D. and bar admission** —
   Schmidtke holds the "Esq." title precisely because he
   IS admitted; he understands the difference

An attorney serving as legislative staff under a non-attorney
who uses "J.D." has direct, personal knowledge of the
unauthorized credential claim.

### D. Rules Violated

**MRPC 8.3(a):** Schmidtke had knowledge that Banks was using
a legal credential ("J.D.") without bar admission — a violation
that raises substantial questions as to honesty and fitness.
He failed to report this to the appropriate professional
authority.

**MRPC 8.4(a):** By serving as legislative staff under Banks
and lending his "Esq." credential to the office environment,
Schmidtke assisted Banks' misconduct through institutional
proximity.
