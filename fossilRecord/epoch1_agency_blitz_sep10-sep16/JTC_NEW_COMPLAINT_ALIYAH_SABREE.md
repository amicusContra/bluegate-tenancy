## VI. CANON VIOLATIONS

**Canon 1 — Integrity and Independence.** Two children of the same
county Treasurer sitting as judges on two courts where Banks network
members also preside undermines public confidence in judicial
independence. The appearance of a family-controlled judicial presence
across multiple courts is incompatible with Canon 1.

**Canon 2 — Impropriety and Appearance of Impropriety.** Judge
Aliyah Sabree now sits on the same bench as Judge Miller, whose PCA
Board Chair role her father endorsed. A reasonable observer would
question whether she can act independently of the family and
political relationships that connect her to the Banks enterprise.

**Canon 3 — Impartiality.** In the Family Division — Domestic
Section, any case involving Banks' schools, network-connected
parties, or the Treasurer's office requires impartiality that is
compromised by her family's documented connections.

**Canon 4 — Extrajudicial Activities.** The public swearing-in
ceremonies between family members holding judicial and elected
offices blur the line between family loyalty and judicial
independence.
