**RE: Assistant Attorney General Lamar Moreland — Conflict of Interest

---

Dear Attorney General Nessel:

I write to bring to your attention a matter involving one of your
Assistant Attorneys General that raises serious questions of conflict
of interest, professional responsibility, and public trust.

## I. THE ISSUE

**Assistant Attorney General Lamar Moreland serves as Board Vice Chair
of Purpose Charter Academy (PCA)**, a K-8 charter school located at
10101 E. Canfield Street, Detroit, MI 48214. PCA was founded and is
managed by **Brian Banks**, a convicted felon who is not a licensed
attorney but who represents himself as one.

This information is publicly available at:
https://www.purposecharteracademy.com/boardofdirectors

## II. BRIAN BANKS — BACKGROUND

Brian Banks served as a Michigan State Representative until his
resignation in 2017 due to pending criminal charges. He was convicted
of **embezzlement of public funds** in Wayne County Circuit Court
(Case No. 14-006194-FH, 2016).

Banks holds a J.D. from Michigan State University College of Law (2010)
but was **never admitted to the State Bar of Michigan**. Despite this,
Banks:

- Uses the credential "Brian Banks, J.D., Ph.D." on all professional materials
- Founded and operates Purpose Charter Academy through "The Purpose Group"
- Serves as Superintendent of MacDowell Preparatory Academy
- Has been the subject of a **State Bar UPL complaint** filed August 31, 2026

**Most recently, on September 9, 2026**, Banks told Detroit Police
Department officers that he was **"an attorney"** during a custodial
dispute at PCA. He used that false claim to direct police to escort
a father away from his children. This incident is documented in
**DPD Report #26-0909-0176** (Officer M. Maciejewski #2726, Fifth Precinct).

## III. AAG MORELAND'S ROLE

Mr. Moreland serves as **Board Vice Chair** of PCA — the second-highest
governance position at the school. In this capacity, he:

- Participates in oversight of a school managed by a convicted felon
- Lends his title as an **Assistant Attorney General** to the school's
  public credibility
- Has a professional obligation under MRPC 8.3(a) to report known
  misconduct to the appropriate professional authority
- Has a professional obligation under MRPC 8.4(a) not to knowingly
  assist or induce another to violate the Rules of Professional Conduct

As a member of the Attorney General's office, Mr. Moreland's association
with Banks' operation raises additional concerns:

1. **Institutional Credibility**: The public sees "Assistant Attorney General"
   on a charter school board and draws reasonable conclusions about the
   school's legitimacy and the fitness of its leadership. Mr. Moreland's
   title functions as an implicit endorsement of Banks and his operations.

2. **Duty to Report**: As a lawyer — and specifically as a state prosecutor —
   Mr. Moreland has a heightened obligation to report criminal conduct
   and professional misconduct. Banks' unauthorized practice of law is
   both a violation of MCL 600.916 and a matter that a prosecutor is
   particularly positioned to identify and report.

3. **Conflict of Interest**: If any matter involving PCA, Banks, or related
   parties comes before the Attorney General's office, Mr. Moreland's
   board role creates an immediate conflict. Given the breadth of the AG's
   jurisdiction — including charitable trust oversight, consumer protection,
   education law, and criminal referrals — this conflict is not hypothetical.

## IV. THE ANALOGY

To illustrate the gravity of this situation:

This is the equivalent of a state health department official serving as
Vice Chair of a hospital board that allows an unlicensed person — with
a medical degree but no license, and a prior conviction for misconduct —
to perform examinations on patients under the implied authority of the
board's credentials.

No health department would tolerate one of its officials lending their
title to such an operation. The Attorney General's office should not
tolerate it either.

## V. THE BOARD COMPOSITION

The full PCA Board of Directors, as publicly listed:

| Role | Name | Title |
|------|------|-------|
| Board Chair | Hon. Cylenthia LaToye Miller | Judge, 3rd Circuit Court |
| Board Vice Chair | **Mr. Lamar Moreland** | **Asst. Attorney General, State of Michigan** |
| Board Secretary | Hon. Latisha Johnson | Detroit City Council Member |
| Board Treasurer | Mr. Devin Hutchings | Founder, DH Strategies |
| Board Asst. Treasurer | Mr. Major Clora | President/CEO, Clora Funeral Home |

The Board Chair is a sitting Wayne County Circuit Court judge. The Vice
Chair is an Assistant Attorney General. A school governed by a judge and
a state prosecutor, managed by a convicted felon who practices law without
a license, raises institutional integrity concerns that extend beyond any
individual member.

## VI. REQUEST

I respectfully request that the Attorney General's office:

1. **Review AAG Moreland's board service** for compliance with AG office
   ethics policies and the Michigan Rules of Professional Conduct

2. **Determine whether AAG Moreland's association with PCA and Banks
   creates conflicts of interest** that affect his duties as an AAG

3. **Assess whether AAG Moreland fulfilled his obligation** under MRPC 8.3
   to report Banks' unauthorized practice of law

4. **Take appropriate action** to protect the integrity and public trust
   of the Attorney General's office

I have separately filed a Request for Investigation with the Michigan
Attorney Grievance Commission regarding Mr. Moreland's obligations under
the Michigan Rules of Professional Conduct.
