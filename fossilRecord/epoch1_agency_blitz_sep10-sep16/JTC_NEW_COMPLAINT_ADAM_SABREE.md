## IV. CANON VIOLATIONS

**Canon 1 — Integrity and Independence.** The appearance that a
judge's father — a county-wide elected official — endorsed a
convicted felon for public office while the judge sits on a bench
where related matters may arise undermines public confidence in
judicial integrity.

**Canon 2 — Impropriety and Appearance of Impropriety.** Judge
Sabree's prior professional role litigating on behalf of his
father's department, combined with his father's endorsement of
Banks and Miller, creates an appearance of impropriety. A
reasonable observer would question whether Judge Sabree can act
independently in matters involving the Treasurer's office, Banks
network members, or charter school disputes.

**Canon 3 — Impartiality.** The family, professional, and
political connections between Judge Sabree, his father, and the
Banks network raise questions about impartiality. Should any
matter involving Banks, PCA, MacDowell, or network-connected
parties come before the 36th District Court, Judge Sabree's
ability to act impartially would be compromised.
