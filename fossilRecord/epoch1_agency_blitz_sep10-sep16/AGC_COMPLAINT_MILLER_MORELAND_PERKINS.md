## I. INTRODUCTION

I respectfully submit this Request for Investigation against three members of the State Bar of Michigan who, in my view, have failed to report and/or have actively assisted the unauthorized practice of law by Brian Roderick Banks — a convicted felon who holds a J.D. but has never been admitted to practice law in this State.

This is the equivalent of a hospital board chaired by a licensed physician and a state health official, allowing an unlicensed person with a medical degree but no license — and a prior conviction for misconduct — to perform examinations on patients while wearing a lab coat, under the implied authority of the board's credentials. No medical board would tolerate this. The State Bar should not tolerate it either.

Each respondent holds a license to practice law in Michigan. Each respondent occupies a position of public trust. Each respondent has lent institutional credibility to an enterprise operated by a person who is not licensed to practice law and who repeatedly represents himself as an attorney to law enforcement, parents, and the public. I ask that the Attorney Grievance Commission investigate all three respondents for their respective roles in enabling Mr. Banks' unauthorized practice of law, and take appropriate disciplinary action.

### B. Conduct at Issue

Judge Miller serves as **Board Chair** of Purpose Charter Academy — the governing body of the school founded and managed by Brian Banks. This is not a passive association. As Board Chair, she presides over board meetings, signs governance documents, and oversees school operations in direct coordination with Mr. Banks.

Judge Miller cannot plausibly claim ignorance of Mr. Banks' background:

- Mr. Banks was convicted of embezzlement in Wayne County — the same county in which Judge Miller sits as a circuit judge.
- Judge Miller is a licensed attorney and judge. She can verify bar admission status in minutes.
- Mr. Banks publicly uses "J.D." and attorney-adjacent titles on all school materials.
- On September 9, 2026, Mr. Banks committed unauthorized practice of law **on PCA's campus** — the institution Judge Miller chairs.

Despite these facts, Judge Miller has not reported Mr. Banks' unauthorized practice of law to the State Bar. Instead, she has continued to lend her judicial title and institutional authority to his school board.

### C. Rules Implicated

**MRPC 8.3(a) — Reporting Professional Misconduct.** A lawyer who knows that another lawyer has committed a violation of the Rules of Professional Conduct that raises a substantial question as to that lawyer's honesty, trustworthiness, or fitness as a lawyer in other respects, shall inform the appropriate professional authority. Judge Miller knew or had reason to know that Mr. Banks was not admitted to the bar and was holding himself out as an attorney. She failed to report.

**MRPC 8.4(a) — Violation of Rules Through Another.** It is professional misconduct for a lawyer to violate or attempt to violate the Rules of Professional Conduct, knowingly assist or induce another to do so, or do so through the acts of another. By chairing the board of a school operated by an unlicensed person committing unauthorized practice of law, Judge Miller has lent her credentials and governance authority to Mr. Banks' misconduct.

**MRPC 8.4(c) — Dishonesty, Fraud, Deceit, or Misrepresentation.** Conduct involving dishonesty, fraud, deceit, or misrepresentation constitutes professional misconduct. Permitting — and by governance, endorsing — Mr. Banks' use of legal credentials and attorney status within an institution one chairs raises substantial questions under this rule.

**MRPC 8.3(a) — Reporting Professional Misconduct.** Mr. Moreland, as a licensed attorney and state prosecutor, had a duty to report Mr. Banks' unauthorized practice of law once he knew or reasonably should have known of it. He did not.

Mr. Moreland's continued governance role on Mr. Banks' board, without reporting or remedial action, reflects adversely on Mr. Moreland's fitness as a lawyer and as a state law enforcement official.

**MRPC 8.4(c) — Dishonesty, Fraud, Deceit, or Misrepresentation.** Mr. Moreland's Vice Chair title on PCA's public board page implies institutional legitimacy. That legitimacy is undermined when the school's operator falsely claims attorney status to police and parents. Mr. Moreland's failure to address this conduct implicates this rule.

## VI. THE HOSPITAL ANALOGY — WHY THIS MATTERS

This complaint is not about a technical licensing dispute. It is about licensed professionals lending their credentials to an institution that permits an unlicensed person to perform legal functions under cover of those credentials.

Consider the parallel:

> A hospital board is chaired by a licensed physician and a state health official. The hospital's operator holds a medical degree but no medical license, and has a prior conviction for professional misconduct. That operator examines patients while wearing a lab coat, tells nurses and police that he is a licensed doctor, and uses that false claim to control access to patients and override a parent's rights — all while the physician-chair and health official on the board take no corrective action and continue to govern the institution.
>
> No medical board would tolerate this. No hospital accreditation body would permit it. The public would rightly demand investigation of every licensed professional on that board.
>
> **This is the equivalent of what has occurred at Purpose Charter Academy.** Judge Miller and Assistant Attorney General Moreland chair and vice-chair the board of a school where Brian Banks — unlicensed, convicted, and repeatedly warned — commits unauthorized practice of law on campus, including before police officers. Attorney Perkins represents Mr. Banks while Mr. Banks continues that conduct.
>
> No medical board would tolerate this. **The State Bar should not tolerate it either.**
