## III. RULES IMPLICATED

### A. MRPC 8.3(a) — Failure to Report

Judge Evans had direct personal knowledge — not merely
constructive knowledge — that Banks was a convicted felon who
was not admitted to the bar. She:

1. Was named as his "mentor" (Acknowledgments, p. 4)
2. Was present at his grandfather's deathbed (Ch. 9)
3. Visited him the night before his AG surrender (Ch. 12)
4. Attended his law school graduation dinner (Ch. 5)
5. Endorsed him for public office in 2022

A mentor who watched her mentee through law school, who was
present during his criminal prosecution, who attended his
graduation — this person knew whether her mentee was admitted
to the bar. She knew Banks used "J.D." without admission.
She failed to report.

### B. MRPC 8.4(a) — Assisting Misconduct

By endorsing Banks for Wayne County Commissioner in 2022 —
with full knowledge of his felony history and unauthorized
credential use — Judge Evans actively lent her judicial
authority to his public persona. This endorsement was documented
by M.L. Elrick in the Detroit Free Press alongside endorsements
from Mayor Duggan, Eric Sabree, and Thomas Stallworth.

### C. MRPC 8.4(d) — Conduct Prejudicial to Justice

A retired judge who has spent her career in the administration
of justice — endorsing a convicted felon who impersonates a
lawyer — is conduct prejudicial to the administration of justice.
