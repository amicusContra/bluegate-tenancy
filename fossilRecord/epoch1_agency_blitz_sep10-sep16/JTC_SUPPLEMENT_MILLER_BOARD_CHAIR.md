## IV. CANONS VIOLATED

Judge Miller's conduct as Board Chair of PCA implicates the Michigan Code
of Judicial Conduct as follows:

**Canon 1 — Integrity and Independence of the Judiciary.**
A judge shall uphold the integrity of the judiciary. Chairing the board of
a school operated by a convicted felon who falsely claims attorney status
before police officers undermines public confidence in the judicial system.

**Canon 2 — Impropriety and Appearance of Impropriety.**
A judge shall avoid impropriety and the appearance of impropriety in **all
activities** — not only judicial activities. A sitting Criminal Division
judge lending her title and governance authority to a charter school run by
a convicted felon who commits unauthorized practice of law on school
property creates the appearance of impropriety.

**Canon 4 — Extrajudicial Activities.**
A judge's extrajudicial activities shall not:

- **(a)** cast reasonable doubt on the judge's capacity to act impartially;
- **(b)** demean the judicial office; or
- **(c)** interfere with the proper performance of judicial duties.

Judge Miller's active governance role at PCA — presiding over board
meetings, signing governance documents, and overseeing operations in direct
coordination with Mr. Banks — satisfies each of these subsections. The
September 9, 2026 incident demonstrates that Mr. Banks invokes the
institutional authority of PCA's board, which includes a sitting judge, to
exercise control over parents and law enforcement on school property.
