## II. NATURE OF COMPLAINT

This complaint concerns a pattern of undisclosed conflicts of
interest, facilitation of unauthorized practice of law, and the
structural conflict created by Todd Perkins' simultaneous roles
as: (1) personal attorney to a sitting Criminal Division judge,
(2) counsel to a convicted felon operating a charter school under
that judge's board governance, and (3) brother of a judge sitting
on a related bench.

---

## III. FACTS

### A. The Miller Representation (2024)

On June 8, 2024, Judge Cylenthia LaToye Miller was arrested at
Detroit Metropolitan Airport with a loaded, unregistered .380
caliber Smith & Wesson handgun. She initially told police the
gun belonged to "her brother" in Ohio. Todd Perkins, described
as a "personal friend," represented Judge Miller in the resulting
criminal proceeding.

Judge Miller pleaded no contest on September 16, 2024, in the
34th District Court (case referred from Wayne County due to
Prosecutor Kym Worthy's recusal). She received 90 days probation
and $1,400 in fines.

Todd Perkins admitted during the proceeding that Judge Miller's
initial statement to police — that the gun belonged to a
relative — was false.

**Source:** WXYZ-TV Detroit; FOX 2 Detroit; MLive; Belleville
Independent.

### B. The Evans Murder Case — Undisclosed Conflict (Dec 2025)

On December 8, 2025, Judge Miller dismissed *People v. Charles
Evans* (Felony Murder and Child Abuse First Degree — the killing
of nine-month-old Kinsley Evans) **with prejudice**.

The defense attorney in the Evans case was affiliated with
**Perkins Law Group PLLC** — Todd Perkins' firm.

This means Perkins Law Group simultaneously:
1. Represented Judge Miller personally (2024 gun case)
2. Appeared before Judge Miller as defense counsel (Evans 2025)

**Neither Judge Miller nor defense counsel disclosed this prior
attorney-client relationship to the prosecution, the victim's
family, or the public record.**

Wayne County Prosecutor Kym Worthy stated: "There is no such
thing as dismissal with prejudice in criminal law." She further
stated: "In all of my years of practice I have never seen a
judge behave like this."

The Wayne County Prosecutor's Office filed an appeal, which
remains pending.

**Todd Perkins' obligation:** As the supervising attorney of
Perkins Law Group PLLC, Todd Perkins had a professional
obligation to ensure that his firm's appearance before a judge
who was a current or recent client of the firm was disclosed. The
failure to disclose this conflict resulted in the most extreme
defense outcome — dismissal with prejudice of a murder charge
involving an infant victim.

**Source:** WXYZ-TV Detroit; Wayne County Prosecutor's Office
press release.

### C. Brian Banks Representation

Todd Perkins represents and/or has represented Brian Roderick
Banks in various matters, including business entity formation
and legal consultations. Banks is not licensed to practice law
in Michigan (confirmed by State Bar P-number search). Banks
presents himself as "Brian Banks, J.D., Ph.D." and operates
under the title "attorney" in public representations.

Judge Miller, Todd Perkins' personal client, serves as
**Chairperson of the Board of Directors** of Purpose Charter
Academy — a school Banks founded and leads as superintendent.

Todd Perkins' dual role — as Banks' attorney and Miller's
personal attorney — creates a structural conflict: Perkins has
privileged knowledge of both the school's founder/operator and
the judge who governs it. Any legal advice Perkins provides to
either client is colored by his obligations to the other.

### D. Brother on the Bench

Todd Perkins' brother, **Sean B. Perkins**, was appointed to the
36th District Court on May 5, 2021, by Governor Whitmer. Before
his appointment, Sean Perkins was an attorney at **Perkins Law
Group PLLC** — Todd's firm. They were law partners.

Sean Perkins now sits on the same 36th District bench as:
- **Judge Tenisha Yancey** (MacDowell Board Chair; paid Banks
  $15,750 from campaign)
- **Judge Adam Sabree** (son of Wayne County Treasurer Eric
  Sabree, who endorsed Banks and Miller)

All three were elected on the same November 8, 2022 ballot.

Todd Perkins continues to practice law. Any appearance by Todd
Perkins or any Perkins Law Group attorney before the 36th
District Court — where his brother and former law partner sits
— presents a structural conflict.

### E. Todd Perkins' Mayoral Campaign (2025)

## IV. RULES OF PROFESSIONAL CONDUCT IMPLICATED

**MRPC 1.7 — Conflict of Interest: Current Clients.** Todd
Perkins simultaneously represented Judge Miller (personal
criminal defense) and appeared before her courtroom through his
firm (Evans defense). The interests of these two clients —
keeping Miller on the bench AND winning for the Evans
defendant — converge in a way that creates an impermissible
concurrent conflict.

**MRPC 1.10 — Imputation of Conflicts.** Perkins Law Group's
conflict from the Miller representation extends to all
attorneys at the firm. The firm's appearance before Judge Miller
in the Evans case was an imputed conflict that required
disclosure and, likely, withdrawal.

**MRPC 3.3 — Candor Toward the Tribunal.** The failure to
disclose the prior attorney-client relationship between Perkins
Law Group and Judge Miller during the Evans murder trial
constitutes a failure of candor toward the tribunal and the
opposing party.

**MRPC 5.5 — Unauthorized Practice of Law.** To the extent
Todd Perkins has facilitated, advised, or enabled Brian Banks'
public representation of himself as an attorney (Banks uses
"J.D." and "attorney" without bar admission), Perkins may be
assisting in the unauthorized practice of law.

**MRPC 8.4 — Misconduct.** The pattern of undisclosed conflicts,
combined with the extraordinary outcome in the Evans case
(murder dismissed with prejudice), constitutes conduct
prejudicial to the administration of justice.

## V. PATTERN SUMMARY

Todd Perkins sits at the intersection of three conflict streams:

1. **Judge-to-attorney:** He personally represents Judge Miller,
   then his firm appears before her and wins the most extreme
   outcome possible in a murder case — without disclosure.

2. **Attorney-to-enterprise:** He represents Banks (a convicted
   felon operating schools and entities), while his personal
   client Judge Miller chairs the board of Banks' school.

3. **Family-to-bench:** His brother, his former law partner,
   sits on the 36th District bench alongside two other judges
   with Banks network connections.

These three streams create an environment where judicial outcomes,
school governance, and legal representation are all connected
through one attorney and one law firm.
