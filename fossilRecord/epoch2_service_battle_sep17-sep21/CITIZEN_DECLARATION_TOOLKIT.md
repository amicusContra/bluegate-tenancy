# CITIZEN DECLARATION TOOLKIT
## "They Can't Make All of Detroit a Vexatious Litigant"

### THE CONCEPT

Kevin Mok carries the main RICO complaint, the § 1983, and the Court of Claims.
But the network harmed thousands of people across Detroit — foreclosure victims,
parents at captured schools, litigants in captured courts, people who dealt with
the Ombudsman's office, people who interacted with Banks claiming to be a lawyer.

Each of those people has their OWN story. And each story, told independently
under penalty of perjury, is a brick in the wall of pattern evidence.

**The legal principle:** Pattern evidence (FRE 404(b), FRE 406) is admissible
to show plan, scheme, knowledge, or absence of mistake. When 20 independent
declarants describe the same institutional dysfunction from different angles,
no court can dismiss it as one person's grievance. It IS the pattern.

**The strategic principle:** One filer can be labeled vexatious. A hundred
independent declarations from a hundred different people describing the same
system from a hundred different entry points cannot. That is not vexatious
litigation. That is a community testifying.

## TEMPLATE A: VICTIM / COMMUNITY DECLARATION

```
DECLARATION OF [FULL NAME]
Pursuant to 28 U.S.C. § 1746

I, [FULL NAME], declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the
United States that the following is true and correct:

1. IDENTITY AND STANDING

My name is [full name]. I am [age] years old and reside in [city, county],
Michigan. I am a [resident/parent/litigant/tenant/creditor/other] who was
directly affected by the conduct of [name the specific actor(s) and
institution(s) from your experience].

I am not a party to any pending litigation against these individuals
[OR: I am a party to Case No. ______ in _______ Court]. I file this
declaration voluntarily, on my own behalf, based on my personal knowledge
and experience.

2. MY EXPERIENCE

[In your own words, describe what happened to you. Be specific about:
- WHEN it happened (dates or approximate dates)
- WHERE it happened (which court, school, office, address)
- WHO was involved (full names and titles)
- WHAT happened (what you saw, heard, or experienced directly)
- WHAT HARM resulted (financial loss, custody impact, legal harm,
  educational harm to your children, emotional harm)

Write as much or as little as you need. Use your own voice. Attach
any documents you have (letters, court orders, receipts, photos).]

3. WHAT I DID ABOUT IT

[Describe any complaints you filed, people you contacted, or steps
you took. Did you report it? To whom? What happened?

If nothing happened after you reported, say so. If you were afraid
to report, say why.]

4. HOW THIS CONNECTS TO THE PATTERN

[You don't need to prove the whole pattern. Just explain how YOUR
experience connects to any of the following that you know about:
- Brian Banks claiming to be a lawyer / "Dr. Banks, J.D."
- Judges with connections to Banks (Miller, Yancey, Sabree)
- Wayne County foreclosure issues (Treasurer Sabree)
- Purpose Charter Academy or MacDowell Preparatory Academy
- The Detroit Ombudsman's Office (Dagnogo)
- Todd Perkins as your attorney or opposing counsel
- Holland or any of Banks' business entities
- Campaign finance / political fundraising
- Any bankruptcy involving network members

If you don't know about any of these, that's fine. Your independent
experience is the evidence. The pattern connects itself.]

5. WHAT I AM ASKING

I am filing this declaration to place my experience on the record.
I request that this declaration be considered as evidence of institutional
pattern in any proceeding where the conduct of the above-named actors
or institutions is at issue.

I am willing to testify to the contents of this declaration if called
upon to do so.

I declare under penalty of perjury under the laws of the United States
of America that the foregoing is true and correct.

Executed on [DATE], at [CITY], Michigan.


_________________________________
[PRINTED NAME]
[ADDRESS - optional, can use P.O. Box]
[PHONE - optional]
[EMAIL - optional]
```

You are not being asked to draw legal conclusions. You are being asked
to state what you know from having been inside the institution.]

of America that the foregoing is true and correct.

Executed on [DATE], at [CITY], Michigan.

## LEGAL NOTES

1. **28 U.S.C. § 1746** allows unsworn declarations "under penalty of
   perjury" to substitute for notarized affidavits in federal proceedings.
   No notary needed. Just sign and date.

2. **For state court filings**, Michigan generally requires notarized
   affidavits (MCR 2.119(B)). But for agency complaints (JTC, AG, OIG),
   a sworn statement is sufficient.

3. **AI assistance with drafting is permissible.** The person signing is
   the author. AI is a tool, like a typewriter or a law librarian. The
   filer must verify every fact and sign under penalty of perjury.
   Duty of candor applies — everything in the declaration must be true.

4. **Each declaration is independent.** No coordination between filers is
   needed or desired. Independence is the point. The pattern proves itself
   when people who don't know each other describe the same system.

5. **Retaliation protections exist.** Michigan's Whistleblowers' Protection
   Act, federal False Claims Act, and various sector-specific protections
   apply. Filers who fear retaliation should consult legal aid or file
   anonymously with agencies first.
