## PRINCIPLE: REDUNDANT DELIVERY = GUARANTEED RECEIPT

A single email can be spam-filtered. A single letter can be misrouted.
A fax generates a **transmission confirmation** — admissible proof of
delivery with timestamp. Every agency report should use AT LEAST TWO
channels. Critical reports use THREE.

## COMMUNICATION METHODS AVAILABLE

| Method | Proof of Delivery | Speed | Cost | Best For |
|--------|------------------|-------|------|----------|
| **Certified Mail RD** | Green card signature + USPS tracking | 3-5 days | ~$9/letter | Official complaints, exhibits |
| **Email** | Read receipt + sent copy | Instant | Free | Cover letters, follow-ups, links |
| **Fax** | Transmission confirmation page | Instant | Free (online) or ~$0.10/pg | Hotlines, OIG offices, court clerks |
| **Online Portal** | Confirmation # + screenshot | Instant | Free | OIG hotlines, CFPB, tips.fbi.gov |
| **Phone** | Record date/time/name of agent | Instant | Free | Confirm receipt, escalate |
| **Walk-in** | Stamped copy at counter | Immediate | Gas | Courts, local agencies, USPS |
| **Registered Mail** | Chain of custody at every stop | 5-7 days | ~$16 | Nuclear option — highest proof |

### FREE FAX SERVICES
- **FaxZero.com** — 5 free faxes/day (3 pages + cover)
- **GotFreeFax.com** — 2 free faxes/day
- **FaxBurner** app — free incoming + limited outgoing
- **eFax** — 30-day free trial (150 pages/month)
- **Windows Fax and Scan** — if you have a modem/phone line

### FAX STRATEGY
1. Send fax first (instant delivery + confirmation)
2. Follow with email (digital backup + links/attachments)
3. Follow with certified mail (legal proof + physical package)
4. Screenshot the fax confirmation page → save as exhibit
