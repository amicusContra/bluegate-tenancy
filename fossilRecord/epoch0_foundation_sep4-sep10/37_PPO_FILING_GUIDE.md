## WHAT EACH PPO DOES

Once granted (can be granted EX PARTE — same day, no hearing):

- **Prohibits contact** — no calls, texts, emails, social media, third-party contact
- **Prohibits presence** — cannot come within X feet of petitioner's home, work, school
- **Enforceable immediately** — violation is a CRIMINAL offense (MCL 600.2950(23))
- **Separate from custody case** — independent case number, independent enforcement
- **Creates criminal exposure** — if Leon shows up at Cornerstone again after a PPO, he gets arrested
