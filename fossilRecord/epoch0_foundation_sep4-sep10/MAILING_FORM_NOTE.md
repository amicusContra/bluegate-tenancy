# MAILING PROTOCOL — PS Form 3665 (Certificate of Mailing — Firm)

## Standard Form for All Future Mailings

**Use PS Form 3665** for all mailings of 3+ pieces going forward.

- **PDF Download:** https://about.usps.com/forms/ps3665.pdf
- **Version:** December 2024 (current as of Sep 2026)
- **PSN:** 7530-17-000-5549

## Why 3665 (Not 3817)

| Form | Pieces | Use Case |
|------|--------|----------|
| **PS 3817** | Fewer than 3 | Single letters — one form per piece |
| **PS 3665** ← USE THIS | **3 or more** | Firm sheet — list ALL recipients on one form, postmarked at counter |
| PS 3606-D | Bulk | Bulk mailing — no individual evidence per piece |
| PS 3877 | Bound book | Accountable mail (certified, COD, etc.) |

## How It Works

1. **Print the 3665 PDF** — fill in your name/address at top
2. **List each recipient** — name, address, one row per letter
3. **Take to USPS retail counter** (< 50 pieces) with all letters
4. **Clerk postmarks the form** with date of receipt
5. **You keep the postmarked form** — this is your ONLY receipt
6. **USPS does NOT keep a copy** — scan it when you get home

## Key Rules

- Present **hardcopy** at retail Post Office (under 50 pieces)
- Print in **ink or ballpoint pen**
- Complete return address on each article
- The postmarked form = legal proof of mailing date
- Use for: First-Class Mail, Priority Mail, Media Mail

## For This Case

All future mailings — preservation letters, trustee supplements,
FOIA paper escalations, JTC packets — use the 3665.

One trip. One form. All recipients listed. Postmarked. Done.
