RE: Brian Roderick Banks — School Administrator Certificate
    Violation of Certificate Conditions (Cash-Handling Prohibition)

Dear Office of Educator Excellence:

I am writing to report that Brian Roderick Banks, holder of a
Michigan School Administrator Certificate issued August 7, 2023,
is in active violation of the conditions placed on that certificate
by the Michigan Department of Education.

BACKGROUND:

As reported by M.L. Elrick in the Detroit Free Press (August 27,
2023), MDE State Superintendent Dr. Michael Rice issued Banks'
administrator certificate with specific conditions, including a
prohibition on handling cash. MDE spokesman Bill DiSessa confirmed
the certificate conditions to Elrick.

Banks holds this certificate and currently serves as Superintendent
and Chief Academic Officer of MacDowell Preparatory Academy, a K-8
public charter school at 4201 W. Outer Drive, Detroit, MI 48221.
He is also the founder of Purpose Charter Academy, a K-8 charter
school at 10101 E. Canfield Street, Detroit, MI 48214, managed by
The Purpose Group Michigan LLC.

EVIDENCE OF CASH-HANDLING VIOLATION:

Banks exercises direct financial control over MacDowell Preparatory
Academy's $5,896,918 in annual expenditures (FY2025 audited
financial statements, Alan C. Young & Associates, P.C.):

1. Budget authority: Banks' LinkedIn describes his MacDowell
   Superintendent role as including "Ensure a positive year-end
   fiscal balance" and "Advise on the annual budget development."
   The school ran a $968,295 deficit in FY2025.

2. Mid-year budget override: The Operations & Maintenance budget
   was doubled mid-year from $362,000 (approved) to $762,000+
   (actual) — a $400,000 increase requiring superintendent
   authorization.

3. Vendor selection: The MacDowell SpEd RFP (April 2024) directed
   all proposals to "Dr. Brian Banks, Superintendent." He
   personally selects vendors who receive public education funds.

4. Hiring authority: The Purpose Group career fair (LinkedIn,
   IMG_5817) shows Banks' management company hiring staff for PCA.
   All public education money for PCA flows through The Purpose
   Group — Banks' own entity.

5. School Administration Services: The FY2025 audit shows $667,426
   in "School Administration Services" — Banks' salary is embedded
   in this line item, and he controls the budget category.

6. Vehicle lease: MacDowell has a vehicle lease with Hoekstra
   Leasing at $2,336/month ($28,032/year). The superintendent
   would authorize this expenditure.

7. Building loan: MacDowell has a building loan with Credit Union
   ONE with a $719,737 balloon payment due June 2026. The
   superintendent manages this debt obligation.

8. Management company: The Purpose Group Michigan LLC manages PCA.
   Banks is the presumed managing member. All state per-pupil
   funding for PCA (~$2.16M in Year 1) flows through his company.

The certificate condition's intent was to prevent Banks from having
financial control over public education funds. He has exactly that
control — across two schools and a management company — totaling
approximately $7-8 million per year.

ADDITIONAL CONCERNS:

1. Unauthorized Practice of Law: On August 29, 2026, Banks told me
   "I'm a lawyer" while evaluating a parental authorization document
   at PCA. He has never been admitted to the Michigan Bar. This has
   been reported separately to the State Bar of Michigan on
   August 31, 2026.

2. His LinkedIn profile lists "Law" and "Criminal Law" as active
   skills and his consulting firm advertises legal services.

3. The Pahara Institute lists Banks as a fellow using "J.D."
   credentials without disclosing his criminal history or
   non-bar-admission.

REQUESTED ACTION:

I request that the Department:

1. Conduct a compliance review of Banks' administrator certificate
   conditions, specifically the cash-handling prohibition

2. Determine whether Banks' exercise of financial control at
   MacDowell, PCA, and The Purpose Group violates his certificate
   conditions

3. Take appropriate action to enforce the conditions, up to and
   including certificate revocation
