### II. LEGAL STANDARD

MCR 2.118(a)(2) provides that leave to amend "shall be freely given when justice so requires." Michigan courts apply this standard liberally, particularly at early stages of litigation before defendants have answered or incurred substantial preparation. *See* *Roberts v. Roberts*, 215 Mich App 51, 54 (1995); *In re Estate of Kessler*, 347 Mich App 1, 8 (2022).

### III. GOOD CAUSE FOR AMENDMENT

**A. The case is in its earliest stage.** Plaintiff filed the original Complaint on **September 1, 2026**. As of the date of this motion, **no Defendant has been served** and no responsive pleading has been filed. No scheduling order has issued. No discovery has commenced. Amendment at this juncture cannot prejudice existing Defendants' litigation posture because none has yet entered an appearance or invested in defense of this action.

**B. New parties were identified through lawful post-filing investigation.** After filing the original Complaint, Plaintiff conducted additional open-source and public-records research that revealed:

- **Joseph Holland Jr.**, a convicted drug trafficker discharged from the Michigan Department of Corrections "without improvement" on May 8, 2024, serves as **Secretary and Treasurer** of Purpose Foundation and as **Treasurer** of the "Bank on Banks for Michigan" political action committee associated with Defendant Banks; and  
- **Purpose Foundation**, a Michigan nonprofit corporation formed December 9, 2024, with Defendant Banks as registered agent and President, appears to function as the fundraising and nonprofit arm of Defendant Banks' charter school network.

These parties and their roles were not fully apparent from the record available at the time of the original filing. Plaintiff discovered them through review of LARA corporate filings, MDOC/ICHAT public records, IRS exempt-organization databases, campaign-finance disclosures, and related OSINT compiled between September 1 and September 2, 2026.

**C. The amendment relates to the same transaction and occurrence.** The proposed amendment does not introduce unrelated claims. Holland and Purpose Foundation are integral to the corporate and financial infrastructure through which Defendant Banks operates Purpose Charter Academy and exercises control over public education funds. The August 29, 2026 incident at PCA — the core of this action — occurred at a school operated within that network. Adding Holland and Purpose Foundation clarifies the full scope of the enterprise that enabled Banks' conduct; it does not change the nature of Plaintiff's injury.

**D. No prejudice to existing Defendants.** Because service has not occurred, existing Defendants will receive a single pleading identifying all parties and claims. Plaintiff will serve the First Amended Complaint in lieu of the original Complaint. Existing Defendants gain full notice of the claims against them without incurring duplicate motion practice, duplicate filing fees for separate actions, or fragmented litigation.

**E. Justice requires amendment.** Purpose Foundation solicits tax-deductible donations and sells school-branded merchandise while listing a convicted drug trafficker as its sole financial officer. Holland treasures a PAC owing $14,500 in campaign-finance fines. These facts are material to Plaintiff's fraud, conspiracy, and fiduciary-duty claims and to the public interest in transparency surrounding entities serving Michigan schoolchildren. Dismissal of these claims on procedural grounds — or their deferral to a separate action — would waste judicial resources and permit incomplete adjudication of conduct arising from the same nucleus of operative fact.
