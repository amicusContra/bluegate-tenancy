## CONCERNS

### 1. Related-Party Lending — Employee's Parents as Borrowers

Shayla Nicole Smith (NMLS #2649162) is an active Mortgage Loan Originator at Lake Trust Credit Union. Her parents, Leon A. Smith and Marquita L. Smith, obtained a $68,000 mortgage from Lake Trust on June 24, 2025 — nine months after Shayla received her MLO authorization at Lake Trust (September 30, 2024).

While the recorded originator is Kathy Greenwood (NMLS #538026), not Shayla Smith, the following questions arise:

- **Did Shayla Smith refer her parents to Lake Trust or to Greenwood specifically?** Internal referrals by employees for family members are a compliance red flag.
- **Did Shayla Smith participate in any aspect of the loan process?** Including document preparation, rate discussion, application assistance, or communication with the borrowers.
- **Did Lake Trust's compliance department identify this as a related-party transaction?** An MLO's parents applying for a mortgage at the same institution requires heightened scrutiny.
- **Was Shayla Smith disclosed as a related employee on the loan file?** Federal and state regulations require identification of insider/related-party transactions.

### 2. Borrower Creditworthiness — Tax-Delinquent Borrowers

At the time of the mortgage:
- The property (17370 Dresden) had **delinquent taxes** for tax year 2023
- Wayne County Treasurer records show the property was in **tax forfeiture** (Wayne County ROD Doc #266352220, dated 03/18/2025 — recorded BEFORE the mortgage closing)
- Lake Trust itself paid **$3,010.45** in back taxes on July 2, 2025 — **8 days after the mortgage closed** — to protect its new lien

This raises questions about Lake Trust's underwriting:
- How did tax-delinquent borrowers qualify for a $68,000 mortgage?
- Was Lake Trust aware of the tax forfeiture at closing?
- Did the related employee (Shayla) influence the underwriting decision?

### 3. Borrower's Subsequent Bankruptcy

Marquita L. Smith filed for Chapter 13 bankruptcy on **July 3, 2026** (USBC MIEB Case No. 26-47542) — approximately one year after the mortgage closed. Critically:

- The $68,000 Lake Trust mortgage was **NOT listed on the bankruptcy schedules** as a secured debt
- The Dresden property was **NOT listed as an asset** on Schedule A/B
- This constitutes a material omission on a sworn federal filing

The bankruptcy trustee (Tammy Terry) has been notified of this concealment.

### 4. Serial Bankruptcy History

Marquita Smith has filed **at least six federal bankruptcies** under multiple names:

| Case | Name Used | Year |

| 14-53014 | Marquita Weathington | 2014 |

**This lending history should have been visible to Lake Trust's underwriting department.** A borrower with 5+ prior bankruptcies and tax-delinquent property is not a standard underwriting approval.

### 5. Mortgage Closing in Macomb County

The mortgage was notarized in **Macomb County**, not Wayne County where the property is located. This is consistent with the Smiths' actual residence being in Macomb County (Eastpointe/Roseville area), not Detroit. The notary, Stacey Bethell, is also a Lake Trust MLO (NMLS #2602739), suggesting the closing occurred at a Lake Trust branch location in Macomb County.

## POTENTIAL VIOLATIONS

| Regulation | Description | Applicability |
|------------|-------------|---------------|
| **12 CFR §701.21(c)(8)** | NCUA regulation on insider/related-party transactions | Shayla's parents = insiders under NCUA rules |
| **12 USC §2607** (RESPA) | Prohibition on kickbacks and unearned fees | If Shayla received any compensation/referral benefit |
| **MCL 490.423(3)** | Michigan Credit Union Act — restrictions on loans to officers/employees and their families | Parents of an MLO = covered family under state CU law |
| **12 CFR §741.6** | NCUA safety and soundness — underwriting standards | Lending to tax-delinquent, serial-bankruptcy borrowers |
| **Reg O (12 CFR Part 215)** | Loans to insiders (applies to CUs via NCUA guidance) | Related-party transaction not disclosed or scrutinized |

## REQUESTED ACTIONS

1. **Investigate** whether Lake Trust Credit Union properly identified and documented the Leon/Marquita Smith mortgage as a related-party transaction involving employee Shayla Nicole Smith.

2. **Determine** whether Shayla Smith referred, influenced, or participated in any aspect of the loan origination, processing, or approval.

3. **Examine** Lake Trust's underwriting file for this mortgage to assess whether the loan met standard credit criteria, given the borrowers' tax delinquency, prior bankruptcies, and subsequent bankruptcy filing.

4. **Review** Lake Trust's compliance policies regarding loans to employees' family members and whether those policies were followed.

5. **Refer to NCUA** if federal regulatory violations are identified, as Lake Trust is a federally chartered credit union.
