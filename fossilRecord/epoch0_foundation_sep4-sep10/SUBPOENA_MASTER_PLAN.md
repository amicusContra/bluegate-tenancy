## FILING MECHANICS

### How to Issue a Subpoena in Michigan (MCR 2.305)

1. **Draft the subpoena** using SCAO form MC 11
   (Subpoena - Order to Appear and/or Produce)
2. **Fill in:**
   - Court: Ingham County Circuit Court
   - Case: 2026-4349-CZ (Mok v. Banks)
   - Issued by: Kevin Mok, pro se plaintiff
3. **No court approval needed** -- a party to a pending civil
   action can issue subpoenas for documents
4. **Service:** Personal service or registered mail
5. **Compliance deadline:** At least 14 days from service
6. **Objections:** Recipient has 14 days to object or move to
   quash. If no objection, they must produce.

### Cost
- Subpoena form: Free (SCAO website)
- Service: $15-$50 per subpoena if using process server
- Some institutional records may have copy/retrieval fees
