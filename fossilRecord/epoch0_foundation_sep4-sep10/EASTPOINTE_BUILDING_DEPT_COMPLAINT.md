### What I Am Requesting

1. **Confirm whether XXXXX Stricker Ave is registered as a rental property** under Eastpointe's rental registration ordinance.

2. **If it is NOT registered,** initiate an inspection and enforcement action requiring registration, inspection, and compliance with all applicable rental codes.

3. **If it IS registered,** confirm under which owner name and whether the registration reflects accurate ownership information.

### Eastpointe Rental Registration Ordinance

Under the City of Eastpointe's rental registration requirements, all residential rental properties must be:
- Registered with the Building Department
- Inspected for code compliance
- Maintained to habitability standards
- Operated by a landlord with a current certificate of compliance

Failure to register is a civil infraction subject to fines and potential enforcement action.
