## CALL 2: MACOMB COUNTY TREASURER / EASTPOINTE ASSESSOR
**Macomb Treasurer:** (586) 469-5190
**Eastpointe Assessor:** (586) 445-3661 (same main number, ask for Assessor)
**Purpose:** PRE (Principal Residence Exemption) check on XXXXX Stricker Ave

### Script:

"Good morning, I'm calling to inquire about the Principal Residence Exemption status on a property at XXXXX Stricker Avenue, Eastpointe. Could you tell me whether a PRE is currently in effect on that parcel, and what name the exemption is filed under?"

### Questions to Ask:
1. Is there an active PRE on XXXXX Stricker Ave?
2. What name is the PRE filed under?
3. When was the PRE last rescinded or filed?
4. What is the parcel number? (for future reference)
5. What is the current taxable value vs. assessed value?

### What We're Looking For:
- If PRE is active under "Marquita King" → but she claims to live at Bowman St, Roseville = tax fraud (18 mills improper exemption)
- If PRE is NOT active → property is assessed at full tax rate (less suspicious but still undisclosed on BK)
- If PRE was recently rescinded → she may be aware of scrutiny and trying to clean up

### The Tax Fraud Math:
- Estimated value: $154,300
- Assessed value (50%): ~$77,150
- PRE saves: 18 mills × $77,150 = ~$1,389/year
- If PRE was claimed while not residing there: improper exemption + potential penalty
