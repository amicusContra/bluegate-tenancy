## THE INSIGHT

Every court system has pre-court investigative machinery — referees, trustees,
inspectors, auditors — designed to find facts and make recommendations BEFORE
a judge rules. Judges follow referee recommendations ~80% of the time.

**Old approach:** File motion → argue before judge → hope for ruling
**New approach:** Trigger investigator → feed them public-record data → investigator validates → recommends to judge → judge follows recommendation

The difference: we don't have to prove anything ourselves in front of a judge.
We activate the system's own investigators, feed them the evidence, and let
them do the fact-finding with subpoena power we don't have.

## THE DOCTRINE

### Principle 1: Every Court Has a Referee
- Custody → **FOC (Friend of the Court)** + **GAL (Guardian ad Litem)**
- Bankruptcy → **Trustees** (Ch.13 Tammy Terry, Ch.7 Wendy Turner Lewis) + **US Trustee**
- PPO → **Hearing referee** (upon request)
- Judicial ethics → **JTC investigators**
- Financial regulation → **DIFS examiners**, **NCUA examiners**
- Education → **MDE compliance**, **DPSCD authorizer oversight**
- Property/tax → **County assessors**, **building inspectors**, **code enforcement**
- Benefits → **MDHHS OIG investigators**

### Principle 2: Feed the Referee, Don't Fight the Judge
Our job is NOT to litigate in front of judges.
Our job is to:
1. **Identify** the correct referee for each issue
2. **Trigger** their investigation (complaint, FOIA, trustee letter, praecipe)
3. **Feed** them public-record data they can independently verify
4. **Let them investigate** with powers we don't have (subpoena, sworn examination, home visits)
5. **Their recommendation** becomes the judge's basis for ruling

### Principle 3: Referees Compound
When multiple referees investigate the same network simultaneously,
their findings cross-pollinate:

```
FOC finds Shayla's address fraud
    → validates BK trustee's finding on Simone's false address
        → validates MDHHS OIG finding on Medicaid address fraud
            → validates venue motion argument

Tammy Terry finds $500K concealed property
    → validates Eastpointe Building Dept finding on Stricker
        → validates Macomb Treasurer finding on PRE fraud
            → validates DIFS finding on Lake Trust lending

JTC investigates Miller's board role
    → validates MDE finding on uncertified teachers
        → validates Elrick's reporting on Banks
            → validates DPSCD authorizer failure
```

No single investigator sees the whole picture. But each one validates
a piece, and the pieces build an undeniable pattern.

### Principle 4: Referees Are Credible. We're Not.
A pro se litigant making allegations = "disgruntled party."
A court-appointed FOC investigator making findings = "the court's own evidence."
A federal bankruptcy trustee filing a motion = "officer of the court."
A JTC investigator recommending discipline = "institutional accountability."

Same facts. Different messenger. Different weight.
