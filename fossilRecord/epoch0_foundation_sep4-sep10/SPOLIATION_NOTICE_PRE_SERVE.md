## I. LEGAL DUTY TO PRESERVE

Upon commencement of litigation — and upon receipt of this Notice — you have an immediate, affirmative, and ongoing **legal duty to preserve** all documents, electronically stored information ("ESI"), and tangible things that are relevant, or that may become relevant, to the claims and defenses in this action or any related proceedings.

This duty arises from:

1. The **pending litigation** identified above;
2. Your **actual or constructive knowledge** that documents and ESI in your possession, custody, or control may be relevant to this case;
3. Michigan law governing discovery, spoliation of evidence, and the duty of parties to preserve potentially relevant materials once litigation is reasonably foreseeable or commenced.

**This obligation extends to you individually and to all persons and entities acting on your behalf or under your direction or control.**

You must **immediately suspend** any routine or automatic destruction, deletion, overwriting, or modification of potentially relevant materials. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Email retention and auto-deletion policies
- Text message and instant messaging auto-delete settings
- Cloud storage sync-and-purge routines
- Social media post deletion or archiving settings
- Backup tape rotation and overwrite schedules
- Document shredding or disposal programs
- Temporary file and cache clearing that removes relevant data

Failure to preserve relevant evidence may constitute **spoliation of evidence** under Michigan law and may result in:

- **Adverse inference instructions** to the jury
- **Evidentiary sanctions**, including exclusion of testimony or documents
- **Monetary sanctions** and attorney fees (where applicable)
- **Contempt sanctions**
- **Criminal liability** for obstruction of justice or related offenses, where applicable

This duty applies to **paper records and electronic records alike**, including data stored on personal devices, work devices, cloud accounts, removable media, servers, and third-party platforms.
