## APPLICABLE LAW

### Michigan Public Health Code — MCL 333.16231

Provides for investigation and discipline of licensed health professionals
based on complaints alleging violation of applicable statutes and
administrative rules.

### Michigan Child Protection Law — MCL 722.623

**(1)** An individual who is a [...] licensed master's social worker
[...] who has reasonable cause to suspect child abuse or neglect
**shall** make an immediately, by telephone or otherwise, oral report,
or cause an oral report to be made, of the suspected child abuse
or neglect to the department.

**(3)** The report shall contain [...] the names and addresses of
the child and the child's parent or guardian [...] the nature and
extent of the child abuse or neglect [...] and any other information
that the reporting person believes might be helpful.

### MCL 722.633 — Penalty for Failure to Report

A person who is required to report [...] and who fails to do so
is guilty of a misdemeanor punishable by imprisonment for not more
than 93 days or a fine of not more than $500.00, or both.

### Michigan Administrative Code — R 338.2909 (Social Work Prohibited Conduct)

**(a)** Willful or negligent failure to provide or arrange for provision
of continuity of necessary service.

**(d)** Involvement in a conflict of interest that interferes with the
exercise of professional discretion or makes a client's interests
secondary.

**(e)** Taking advantage of any professional relationship or exploiting
others to further the social worker's personal, religious, political,
and/or business/financial interests.

### NASW Code of Ethics (applicable standard of care)

- **1.01 Commitment to Clients** — Social workers' primary responsibility
  is to promote the well-being of clients. In general, clients' interests
  are primary.
- **1.07 Privacy and Confidentiality** — Includes exception for mandatory
  reporting of child abuse/neglect.
- **6.04 Social and Political Action** — Social workers should act to
  prevent and eliminate [...] exploitation.

## ALLEGATIONS

### Allegation 1: Failure to Report Suspected Child Neglect (MCL 722.623)

**Summary:** Tunezha Crowder, LCSW, had reasonable cause to suspect
child neglect occurring in her own home and failed to report to MDHHS
Children's Protective Services.

**Facts:**

1. Crowder is a licensed clinical social worker (LARA #6801121811,
   #6851110849) with specialized training in child welfare, children's
   home-based therapy, and crisis screening (The Guidance Center,
   2021-2025).

2. Shayla Smith moved into Crowder's home at XXXXX Virginia Ave,
   Eastpointe, MI on or about February 12, 2026, bringing two minor
   children (Sahana, age 9, and Allanna, age 7).

3. During the period February-June 2026, while residing at Crowder's
   home, Shayla Smith:
   - Blocked the children's father (DaSean Mitchell) from phone contact
   - Failed to provide DaSean with her new address or phone number
   - Used false addresses on government benefits applications
   - Filed for government benefits (Medicaid) using an address she had
     vacated (37055 Bradford, Sterling Heights)
   - Filed a USPS Change of Address to a third location (17370 Dresden,
     Detroit) while still living at Crowder's Eastpointe address
   - Left the country (Senegal) for approximately 12 days (June 1-12)
     while the children's custody was unresolved
   - Filed for sole legal and physical custody on June 13, 2026, the
     day after returning from international travel

4. As a licensed clinical social worker with a specialty certification

   Crowder had the professional training to recognize:
   - Parental alienation as a form of emotional harm to children
   - Address fraud involving minor children's government benefits
   - International travel with custody-disputed children
   - A pattern of coercive control over the children's access to their
     father

5. Crowder had direct, personal knowledge of these facts — they were
   occurring in her home.

6. Crowder failed to make any report to MDHHS CPS as required by
   MCL 722.623.

### Allegation 2: Aiding Benefits Fraud While Licensed (R 338.2909(e))

**Summary:** Crowder used her professional knowledge of government
benefits systems to assist or acquiesce to Shayla Smith's address
fraud and benefits manipulation, in violation of R 338.2909(e)
(exploiting professional knowledge for others' benefit in a way that
harms third parties).

**Facts:**

1. Crowder's 4+ years at The Guidance Center's Children's Home-Based
   Services program gave her extensive knowledge of MDHHS systems,
   eligibility requirements, and address reporting obligations.

2. While Shayla was living at Crowder's Eastpointe (Macomb County)
   address, MDHHS records continued to show a Sterling Heights
   (Macomb County) address that Shayla had vacated.

3. On April 21, 2026, Shayla's Medicaid was approved using the
   vacated Bradford address — while Shayla was actually at Crowder's.

4. A social worker with Crowder's training would understand that:
   - MDHHS eligibility depends on accurate address reporting
   - Benefits approved under a false address constitute fraud
   - The children's benefits case (including Medicaid) was based on
     misrepresented household composition

5. Crowder either assisted with this scheme or failed to exercise
   professional responsibility to ensure the children's benefits
   were accurately reported — violating her ethical obligation as
   a licensed professional who was directly observing the fraud.

### Allegation 3: Conflict of Interest / Professional Discretion (R 338.2909(d))

**Summary:** Crowder's personal friendship with Shayla Smith created
a conflict of interest that prevented her from exercising professional
discretion regarding the welfare of the two minor children living in
her home.

**Facts:**

1. Crowder's professional training (MSW, Clinical specialty, BS in
   Human Development & Family Studies, 4+ years children's home-based
   therapy) gave her the knowledge to identify harmful conditions.

2. Her personal relationship with Shayla — close enough to share a
   home — created a conflict between her professional duty to report
   and her personal loyalty to her friend.

3. This conflict resulted in the children being denied contact with
   their father for an extended period, living in a household where
   government fraud was occurring, and being subjected to custody
   manipulation — all while a licensed clinical social worker was
   present and aware.
