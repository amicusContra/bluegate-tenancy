## PRE LAW — MCL 211.7cc

Under MCL 211.7cc, a property is exempt from the tax levied by a local school district if:
- The property is owned and occupied as a **principal residence** by the owner
- The owner claims no other PRE in Michigan

If the owner does not reside at the property, the PRE must be **rescinded**. The Treasurer may:
- Remove the PRE retroactively
- Assess **back taxes** at the non-PRE rate for up to **4 years** (MCL 211.7cc(5))
- Impose **interest and penalties** on the unpaid difference
- Refer for **criminal prosecution** under MCL 211.7cc(5) (false PRE claim = misdemeanor)
