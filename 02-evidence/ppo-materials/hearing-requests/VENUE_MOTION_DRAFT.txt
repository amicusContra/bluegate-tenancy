DEFENDANT'S MOTION TO DISMISS FOR IMPROPER VENUE
OR, IN THE ALTERNATIVE, TO TRANSFER VENUE

    Defendant DaSean Mitchell, appearing in propria persona,
respectfully moves this Court to dismiss this action for improper
venue pursuant to MCL 600.1651 and MCR 2.221, or in the
alternative, to transfer this case to the circuit court of the
county where the minor children actually reside. In support of
this motion, Defendant states as follows:


I.  INTRODUCTION

    1.  This custody action was filed by Plaintiff on June 13, 2026,
in the Wayne County Circuit Court. However, at the time of filing
and for more than six months prior, neither the minor children nor
the Defendant resided in Wayne County. Plaintiff herself did not
maintain an established residence in Wayne County at the time of
filing; her Wayne County address is her parents' home, which she
has described as temporary.

    2.  The Michigan Child Custody Act requires that custody
disputes be resolved in the county where the children reside.
MCL 722.26a(1). Wayne County does not meet this requirement.

III. LEGAL STANDARD

    A.  MCL 722.26a — Proper County for Custody Actions

    14. MCL 722.26a(1) provides:

        "Except as otherwise provided in this section, a child
        custody dispute is filed in the circuit court in the
        county where the child has his or her residence or
        legal domicile."

    15. The children's residence during the six months preceding
the filing was split between Sterling Heights (Macomb County)
and Lansing (Ingham County). At no time during this period did
either child reside in Wayne County. Wayne County is therefore
not the proper venue under MCL 722.26a.

    B.  MCL 600.1651 — Transfer of Venue

    16. MCL 600.1651 provides that a court may change venue "for
the convenience of parties and witnesses and in the interest of
justice." Even if this Court finds it has jurisdiction, the
overwhelming balance of convenience favors transfer.

    17. Factors favoring transfer:

        a. CHILDREN'S CURRENT RESIDENCE: The children currently
           reside at [REDACTED_ADDR_2], Lansing (Ingham County);

        b. DEFENDANT'S RESIDENCE: Defendant resides in Ingham
           County and has a signed lease;

        c. PLAINTIFF'S PRIOR RESIDENCE: Plaintiff's last
           established residence (Bradford) was in Macomb County;

        d. INCIDENT LOCATION: The September 2, 2026 assault by
           Leon A. Smith upon Defendant occurred in Ingham County
           ([REDACTED_ADDR_2], Lansing);

        e. POLICE REPORT: The police report for the September 2
           incident was filed in Ingham County;

        f. ACTIVE PPO PROCEEDINGS: Personal protection order
           proceedings involving the same parties and same children
           are pending in INGHAM COUNTY (30th Judicial Circuit),
           Case Nos. 26-0000004432-PP, 26-0000004433-PH, and
           26-0000004436-PH;

        g. WITNESSES: Defendant's family witnesses reside in
           Ingham County;

        h. PLAINTIFF'S LACK OF ESTABLISHED WAYNE RESIDENCE:
           Plaintiff herself acknowledges her Wayne County
           residence is temporary.


IV. PLAINTIFF'S FILING IN WAYNE COUNTY CONSTITUTES FORUM SHOPPING

    18. The facts establish that Plaintiff deliberately filed this
action in Wayne County despite knowing that neither she, the
Defendant, nor the children had an established six-month
residence in Wayne County. Plaintiff used her parents' address —
which she acknowledges is temporary — to manufacture venue in a
county where no party had proper residence.

    19. This constitutes improper forum shopping. The Michigan
venue statutes exist to ensure that cases are heard in the
county with the most significant connection to the parties and
children. Allowing Plaintiff to manufacture venue by using a
temporary address at her parents' home would undermine this
purpose and prejudice Defendant, who must travel from Ingham
County for every hearing.


V.  RELIEF REQUESTED

    WHEREFORE, Defendant respectfully requests that this Court:

    A.  DISMISS this action for improper venue pursuant to
MCL 600.1651 and MCR 2.221; OR

    B.  In the alternative, TRANSFER this action to the Ingham
County Circuit Court (30th Judicial Circuit), where the children
currently reside, where active PPO proceedings involving the
same parties are pending, and where the September 2, 2026
incident occurred, pursuant to MCL 600.1651; OR

    C.  In the further alternative, TRANSFER this action to the
Macomb County Circuit Court (16th Judicial Circuit), where the
children resided for more than six months prior to the filing
of this Complaint (Bradford Rd, Sterling Heights), pursuant to
MCL 722.26a(1); AND

    D.  Grant such other and further relief as this Court deems
just and equitable.
