III. LEGAL STANDARD

    A.  MCL 722.26a — Proper County for Custody Actions

    24. MCL 722.26a(1) provides:

        "Except as otherwise provided in this section, a child
        custody dispute is filed in the circuit court in the
        county where the child has his or her residence or
        legal domicile."

    25. The children's residence during the six months preceding
the filing (December 17, 2025 through June 17, 2026) was:

    a.  December 2025 — February 2026: Bradford Rd, Sterling
        Heights (MACOMB COUNTY);

    b.  February 2026 — June 2026: Split between Defendant in
        Lansing (INGHAM COUNTY) and various temporary
        arrangements. No residence in Wayne County.

    26. At no point during the six-month look-back period did
either child maintain a residence in Wayne County.


    B.  MCL 600.1651 — Convenience and Justice

    27. Even if this Court finds it has jurisdiction, the
overwhelming balance of convenience favors transfer:

    a.  CHILDREN'S CURRENT RESIDENCE: Lansing, Ingham County.

    b.  DEFENDANT'S RESIDENCE: Lansing, Ingham County (lease).

    c.  PLAINTIFF'S LAST VERIFIED RESIDENCE: Sterling Heights
        then Eastpointe — both MACOMB COUNTY.

    d.  CHILDREN'S SCHOOL HISTORY: MMSA in Warren (MACOMB);
        PCA enrollment post-filing (Wayne) is manufactured.

    e.  MEDICAID RECORDS: Bradford Rd, MACOMB COUNTY through
        at least April 2026.

    f.  POLICE REPORT: September 2, 2026, assault occurred in

    h.  DEFENDANT'S WITNESSES: Reside in Ingham County.

IV.  PLAINTIFF'S FILING CONSTITUTES FORUM SHOPPING

    28. The facts compel the conclusion that Plaintiff
deliberately filed in Wayne County to gain a strategic
advantage, not because Wayne County is the proper venue.

    29. Plaintiff knew that:
    a.  The children were with Defendant in Ingham County;
    b.  Her own last verified residence was in Macomb County;
    c.  Her family's homes are in Macomb County;
    d.  She had no established residence in Wayne County;
    e.  Filing in Wayne County — 90 miles from Defendant —
        would delay Defendant's ability to assert his parental
        rights.

    30. Michigan's venue statutes exist to ensure that custody
disputes are heard in the county with the most significant
connection to the children. MCL 722.26a; Bowie v Arder,
441 Mich 23 (1992). Allowing Plaintiff to manufacture venue
by listing her parents' address — at a home where she has
never demonstrated she actually lives — rewards precisely
the type of forum shopping these statutes were designed to
prevent.

    31. The practical effect of Plaintiff's venue choice has been
to delay Defendant's rights. Defendant's emergency motions have
been pending in this Court while the children remain in
Plaintiff's possession following the September 2 incident.
Had this case been filed in Ingham County — where the children
reside, where the PPOs are pending, and where the assault
occurred — emergency relief would have been consolidated with
the existing proceedings.
