## IV. ADDITIONAL VIOLATIONS

### A. False Statements to Law Enforcement (MCL 750.411a)

Banks' claim to police that he was "an attorney" was a false
statement made to officers acting in their official capacity.
This is a criminal offense under MCL 750.411a.

### B. Custodial Interference (MCL 750.350a)

Banks used his false attorney status to direct police to prevent
a father from accessing his children. He is not a party to the
custody proceeding (Case No. 26-108221-DC, Wayne County), not
counsel of record, and not a Guardian ad Litem. He has no legal
authority to restrict parental access.

### C. False Pretenses (MCL 750.218)

Banks obtained the police officers' compliance through a false
representation of his professional status.
