## How to Preserve Text Evidence

**Kevin's phone** (room setup photos with timestamps):
1. Screenshot each relevant text/photo — make sure the **date and time**
   are visible in the screenshot
2. Save to `photos/` with naming: `YYYY-MM-DD_description.jpg`
3. Also email them to yourself (creates a second timestamp in email metadata)

**DaSean's phone** (texts with bio mom, texts to Kevin):
1. Screenshot the full thread showing June 20 "morning" confirmation
2. Screenshot the June 21 texts to Kevin (waiting, then "got the kids")
3. Do NOT delete any texts with bio mom — ever. Even mundane ones.
4. Consider a full-thread export (Android: SMS Backup & Restore app;
   iPhone: iMazing or iTunes backup)

**General rule**: Keep original filenames and phone metadata. Copy for
binder/USB only — never move or rename originals on the source device.
