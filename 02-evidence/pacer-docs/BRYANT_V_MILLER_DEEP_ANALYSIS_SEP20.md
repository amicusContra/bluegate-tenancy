## 2. Complaint summary — claims and structural conflicts

### Core theory

**Structural institutional financial conflict** in Wayne County (and, by extension, Michigan circuit courts): criminal assessments and court operations feed county finances that **service bond debt** and **subsidize judicial/prosecutorial/indigent-defense compensation**, creating a **Tumey/Ward/Caperton**-type bias without individual proof of malice.

### The “five-link chain” (Complaint ¶24–29)

| Link | Mechanism | Documented amounts (complaint) |
|------|-----------|--------------------------------|
| **1 — Revenue** | MCL **769.1k** fines/fees/costs; Criminal Division collections “top priority” | **~$1.7M** court costs/late fees FY2024 (Exhibit K) |
| **2 — GASB 34 booking** | Assessments booked as county receivables | **$69,651,127** deferred inflows — unavailable receivables (ACFR Ex. D, p. 41) |
| **3 — General Fund** | Receivables → operating fund | **$147,104,334** “Charges for Services” (Ex. D, p. 42) |
| **4 — Bond debt service** | General Fund pays Building Authority **Cash Rentals** (irrevocable pledge) | **$121,779,583** annual debt obligations (Series 2010); **$200,000,000** jail/CJC bonds; **$143,330,000** term bond @ **10%** to **12/01/2040**; **$936,357** interest line item |
| **5 — Courthouse** | Bond-funded **Criminal Justice Center** opened **Sept 2024** — Miller presides there | Ex. K |

### Additional structural pillars

- **LGBS bounty contract** (City of Detroit **Contract 6003597-A1**): **30%** contingency on 3rd Circuit collections; **$13,662,000** collected since **March 2022** (breakdown: **$2.540M** 2022; **$4.168M** 2023; **$5.105M** 2024; **$1.849M** Jan–Apr 2025 per Ex. A / state motion index).
- **ACIP Government Crime Policy** (AIG / National Union **01-771-24-81**): Judicial Branch **Additional Named Insured** (End. **#17**); **$5,000,000** Faithful Performance (End. **#11**); officials as **Employees** — used as **corroboration** of “commercial” court enterprise, not standalone tort.
- **Michigan Judicial Council 2025 Task Force Report** (Ex. **5**): **26.2%** of trial court admin funding from defendant assessments; **58%** of court costs borne by local general funds; quotes **“perverse incentives”** and **“real or perceived conflict… between… impartiality and… generate revenue.”**
- **Closed loop — judge / prosecutor / defense counsel** (¶34–40): FY **2023–24** transition — indigent defense fees paid via **Wayne County Indigent Defense Services** from same **General Fund** that receives **769.1k** revenue and pays **Schulman** → **Tower v. Glover** conspiracy framing.
- **Plea rate**: **6,137 / 9,137** guilty pleas (**67.2%**) FY2024 (Ex. K).

### Procedural bars addressed

- **Heck v. Humphrey** — four reasons not barred (procedures not release; no final sentence; independent due-process injury; systemic Ex parte Young claims).
- **Younger** — biased tribunal (*Gibson v. Berryhill*); inadequate state forum when **every** circuit shares architecture; systemic claims don’t enjoin single prosecution.
