## WHAT TO LOOK FOR IN EACH RESULT

### If MOECS returns results:
1. **Screenshot the list page** — shows credential type and number
2. **Click the credential number** — shows:
   - Issue date and expiration date
   - Certificate type (Standard, Provisional, Temporary)
   - Endorsements (what subjects/grades they're certified to teach)
   - Whether it's VALID or EXPIRED
3. **Screenshot the detail page**
4. **Check endorsement matches assignment** — a K-5 cert teaching 7th grade = violation

### If MOECS returns "No Records Found":
1. **Screenshot it** — this IS evidence
2. That person is teaching WITHOUT a valid Michigan credential
3. Under MCL 388.1763: 50% state aid deduction + $1,500 misdemeanor fine

### Red flags:
- **Paraprofessional doing Teacher of Record work** — Bates, Hubbell, Spencer, Siebert all listed as "Paraprofessional" but assigned to specific grade levels = they may be teaching as TOR without cert
- **Deans without admin certs** — Coleman, Stevens, Norwood all hold "Dean" titles. If they do instructional leadership, evaluation, or discipline they need School Admin credentials
- **Endorsement mismatches** — Elementary cert teaching middle school, or vice versa
- **Expired certs** — Valid at time of hire but lapsed
