One thing that I will always remember about Judge Langford

Morris is her commitment to education. During many of my court

hearings in her courtroom, I can remember her telling other

defendants, whose matters were being heard before mine, to get in

school, to further their education and to stay in school. I can recall a

quote from Horace Mann that says, “Education, then, beyond all other

devices of human origin, is the great equalizer of the conditions of

men, the balance-wheel of the social machinery.” This rings true for

me as education has been one of my main driving forces and catalyst

for my personal growth and change. However, getting that education

was not as easy as I had hoped.
