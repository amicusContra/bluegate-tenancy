## EPISODE DESCRIPTION (Verbatim from Website)

"Brian Banks is a highly sought-after community educator, author,
law school graduate, and former Michigan State Representative. But
despite his tremendous career, he has experienced an equally
tremendous degree of hardship along the way, from a dysfunctional
childhood, to an adolescence marred by fraud charges, to corrupt
political forces ousting him from office. In this episode of Open
Mike, Banks discusses his personal struggles in depth and reflects
on ways they've endowed him with the talent and tenacity to
continuously create his own success."
