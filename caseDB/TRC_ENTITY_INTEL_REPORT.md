## 2. THE "CONVICTED FELON" SMEAR — TIMELINE

### What Barrett Said

On **August 13, 2026**, U.S. Rep. Tom Barrett (R-Charlotte) called his Democratic opponent **William Lawrence** a "convicted felon" during a press conference. C-SPAN recorded the exchange:

> *"He didn't just chain himself to a bulldozer; he tried to shut down a pipeline. He required hours of law enforcement coming in to try and release this ridiculous protest... I know you guys want to cover up for him and everything, but it's indisputable that he's a convicted felon."*
> — Rep. Tom Barrett, August 13, 2026 (C-SPAN, MLive, WWMT)

### What Actually Happened

| Fact | Detail | Source |
|------|--------|--------|
| **The protest** | 2013 Ingham County Line 5 pipeline protest | Court records |
| **The charge** | Misdemeanor trespassing + felony resisting & obstructing | Court records |
| **The plea** | Guilty — fines and probation | Court records |
| **The expungement** | Conviction set aside under Michigan Clean Slate Act (MCL 780.623) | Lawrence campaign, public record |
| **Legal effect** | Expunged conviction treated as though it never occurred; removed from public background-check databases | Michigan State Police policy |

### The NRCC Amplification

The National Republican Congressional Committee pre-staged opposition research:

> *"Socialist felon William Lawrence is a radical far-left liberal who puts his extreme climate agenda ahead of common sense and the priorities of hardworking Michiganders."*
> — NRCC spokesman Zach Bannon (WWMT)

The NRCC's **democratfacts.org** site pre-staged the "convicted felon" oppo research in **2022** — before Lawrence announced his candidacy AND before Michigan's automatic expungement provisions took effect.

### The Right-Wing Media Cascade

Following Barrett's remarks, right-wing outlets (RAIR Foundation via Substack, Washington Free Beacon, Republican Investor) escalated by targeting individual RITDH volunteers, publishing personal details, home addresses, and criminal histories of coalition members. This amplification:

- Named specific volunteers and their addresses
- Published internal coalition records
- Connected volunteers to Lawrence's campaign
- Created a chilling effect on participation in tenant advocacy

### The Projection

| Barrett's Label | Barrett's Funders |
|----------------|-------------------|
| Calls opponent "convicted felon" (expunged pipeline protest) | Receives $4,000 from Dykema Gossett Federal PAC |
| Implies Lawrence is dangerous | Dykema incorporated SDJ — whose president is Kenneth Daniels, **actual convicted federal felon** ($21M drug money) |

**The word "felon" travels outward** (toward Barrett's opponent and tenant advocates) **while actual convicted felons travel inward** through the Dykema-connected entity network.
