## III. THE SEEDING HYPOTHESIS — Low Oversight → Detroit Migration

### Predicted Pattern

1. **Seed phase:** Operator establishes in a small/rural community where:
   - Authorizer is distant (university 100+ miles away)
   - Community is small (low media scrutiny)
   - Board is easily captured (few qualified candidates)
   - Per-pupil funding flows without oversight

2. **Growth phase:** Operator builds "track record" in the small community:
   - Even poor results look acceptable if nobody reports them
   - "Serving underserved communities" narrative builds
   - Fellowship/credential platforms validate without verifying (Pahara, Super Lawyers)

3. **Migration phase:** Operator moves to Detroit where:
   - Per-pupil funding is higher ($9,608-$15,217)
   - Student population is larger (more heads = more money)
   - Authorizer fee incentive protects the charter
   - Political connections (judges, council members, legislators) provide cover
   - Detroit's media landscape is fragmented — harder to sustain investigative coverage

4. **Extraction phase:** Once established in Detroit:
   - Management company takes 50-73% of revenue
   - Board is stacked with controlled actors (judges, attorneys, politicians)
   - Academic results collapse but charter is maintained because authorizer needs the fees
   - Dissolve and reform entities to avoid accountability

## VI. RESEARCH PROTOCOL — Finding More Seeding

### MiSchoolData.org Searches

For each authorizer, pull:
1. **Full list of authorized schools** (by authorizer filter)
2. **M-STEP results** for each school (sort by lowest performing)
3. **Operator/management company** for each school
4. **Board of directors** (from school websites and LARA)

### LARA Entity Cross-Reference

For each charter school operator:
1. Search operator name in LARA → find all entities
2. Check registered agent → is it the operator (self-dealing flag)?
3. Check officer names → do any appear on multiple school boards?
4. Check addresses → do multiple entities share one address?

### Criminal History Cross-Reference

For each operator and board member identified:
1. OTIS (Michigan offender tracking) for corrections history
2. ICHAT for criminal convictions
3. State Bar search for claimed legal credentials
4. PACER for bankruptcy filings
