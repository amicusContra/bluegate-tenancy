# Enforcement Accountability Map
## Who Is SUPPOSED to Be Doing This?

**Created:** 2026-10-09
**Updated:** 2026-10-09
**Purpose:** Separate from network actors — this maps the enforcement/oversight bodies that have the mandate, authority, and resources to act, and documents what they have done or NOT done.

---

## The Question

"Who is collecting power, influence, authority — and not acting?"

Every enforcement body below has:
- A statutory or court-rule mandate to investigate
- Specific jurisdiction over the conduct documented
- Staff and budget to act
- Public evidence that the conduct occurred

The question is what each has done with it.

### Power Collected, Action Not Taken

Every one of these bodies collects authority — appointment power, investigation power, prosecution power, authorization power, certification power. The pattern is not that they lack the tools. The pattern is that each body exercises its authority UP TO its jurisdictional edge and then stops. The edges don't overlap. They gap.

The complainant documented the conduct. Filed with the right offices. Supplemented when it happened again. Copied the backup office. And holds two letters that point at each other.

That is not a person failing. That is a system failing.

*The enforcement bodies are not corrupt. They are jurisdictionally fragmented. Each body does exactly what its mandate says and nothing more. The Banks network operates in the gaps between mandates — where no single body has clear authority to act on the full pattern. That fragmentation is the vulnerability.
