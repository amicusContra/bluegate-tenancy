This raises serious questions:

3. **Lake Trust Credit Union is the lender:** Shayla Smith works at Lake Trust. Her parents took a $68,000 mortgage from her employer. The potential NCUA Regulation O implications for insider transactions remain relevant.

4. **The original mortgage was subprime:** America's Wholesale Lender was Countrywide's wholesale arm — one of the worst subprime lenders of the 2000s. The $89,900 mortgage on a modest Detroit home was a product of the subprime bubble. But they paid it off by 2014.

## SYNTHESIS: THE DRESDEN SCHEME

Here's what the Wayne County ROD reveals when combined with the bankruptcy data:

### Step 1: The Setup (June 24, 2025)

Leon A. Smith has owned 17370 Dresden free and clear since February 2014. For 11 years, no mortgage. Then on June 24, 2025:

- **Morning:** Leon QCDs to add Marquita to the deed ($1, tax exempt)
- **Same day:** Leon & Marquita sign a $68,000 mortgage to Lake Trust Credit Union
- Both recorded June 30, 2025

### Step 2: The Bankruptcy (June 30, 2025)

**The same day** the deed and mortgage are recorded, Marquita files Chapter 7 bankruptcy (Case 25-46692). Six days after signing the mortgage.

### Step 3: The Cascade

- Aug 3, 2025: Ch.7 dismissed (likely failed means test — she has income)
- Aug 7, 2025: Files Ch.13 (reorganization) — Case 25-47952
- Ch.13 defaults
- Jul 3, 2026: Files THIRD bankruptcy, Ch.13 — Case 26-47542

### Step 4: The Side Sale (May 2026)

Between the 2nd and 3rd bankruptcies, Marquita sells the West Point property for $34,000. Where did the money go?

### The Questions a Trustee Should Be Asking

1. **Where did the $68K go?** The house was free and clear. They borrowed $68K against it. Six days later, bankruptcy. Was the money used to pay off a favored creditor? Transferred to Shayla? Used for something else?

2. **Did Marquita disclose West Point on her bankruptcy schedules?** She owned a second property. It must be listed. If she sold it for $34K during or between bankruptcy proceedings without trustee approval, that's a problem.

3. **Why was Leon added to the title the same day as the mortgage?** Leon was already on it — but Marquita wasn't. She was added so she could be a co-borrower on the Lake Trust mortgage. This is a coordinated move.

4. **What is Shayla's involvement with Lake Trust?** Shayla works at the credit union that just lent her parents $68,000. Did she facilitate the loan? Did she have access to the application? Were her parents' financial circumstances (serial bankruptcies) properly disclosed?

5. **Who is Lemar G Jackson?** Co-taxpayer on West Point, which means he had a financial interest in that property. Was he a partner? Tenant? Co-investor?
