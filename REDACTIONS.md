# What is in this copy, and what is not

This is the public copy of the case files. It holds the text files of the latest version as they
were written. It starts fresh, without the earlier history.

## Taken out of the text

The kinds of personal identifier a court filing must leave out (MCR 1.109(D)(9)):

| Kind | Now reads |
|---|---|
| Social Security numbers | `XXX-XX-XXXX` |
| Dates of birth | `[date of birth withheld]` |
| Bank, card and loan numbers | `XXXX` and the last four digits |
| Driver's licence and ID numbers | `[ID number withheld]` |
| Passwords and keys | `[withheld]` / `[key withheld]` |

## Where people live

- The family's two houses are written `House 1` and `House 2`, in the Lansing area.
- Where the files give the address of a place a person lives, the house number is written `XXXX`.
- Offices, schools, courts, and the properties the research is about are as written.

## Left as written

E-mail addresses and phone numbers: the public contacts of public positions.

## Not in this copy

561 files that are not text: the PDF exhibits, a database, saved e-mail. Each of those needs its
own reading before it can come out; they will be added one by one.
