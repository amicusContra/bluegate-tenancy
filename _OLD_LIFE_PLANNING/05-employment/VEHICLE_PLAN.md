## Why Toyota Camry

- It runs forever (250K+ miles with basic maintenance)
- Every mechanic in America knows how to fix one
- Parts are cheap and available
- Passengers rate it highly (comfortable, quiet, clean)
- It does NOT get stolen (immobilizer standard since 2018)
- Insurance is reasonable (not a sports car, not a theft target)
- It qualifies for UberX, Uber Comfort, and Lyft standard
- Resale value stays strong if you ever want to sell

Avoid: Kia, Hyundai (theft targets), Nissan (transmission issues),
Dodge/Chrysler (reliability problems), anything luxury (expensive
parts and insurance).
