# SECTION 2: YOUR RIGHTS AS A PARENT

Michigan law protects your rights as a parent of a child
enrolled in a public school. These are state statutes —
public law that applies to every parent.

## Right to School Information

**MCL 380.1137** — A parent or legal guardian has the right
to access their child's school records and school-related
information. This includes academic records, attendance,
disciplinary records, and any information about school
operations that affect your child.

No school administrator can deny you this access.

## Right to Know Who Runs Your School

**MCL 380.502** — Public school academies (charter schools)
are public schools. They receive public tax dollars. They are
subject to public accountability requirements.

**MCL 380.503** — The charter contract between the school
and its authorizer (DPSCD for PCA and MacDowell) must comply
with all applicable laws.

## Right to Complain to the Authorizer

Purpose Charter Academy and MacDowell Preparatory Academy
are authorized by the **Detroit Public Schools Community
District (DPSCD)**. DPSCD is responsible for monitoring
these schools. DPSCD collects a **3% authorization fee**
from each school's per-pupil funding.

If you believe your child's school is being mismanaged,
you can file a complaint with:

```
DPSCD Office of Charter Schools
3011 West Grand Blvd
Detroit, MI 48202
```

## Right to File a Regulatory Complaint

Any person can file a complaint with a government agency.
You do not need a lawyer. You do not need to pay a fee.
You write what happened to you, sign your name, and submit
it. The agency investigates.

# SECTION 5: FEE WAIVER — MC 20

If you cannot afford the filing fee, Michigan courts
can waive it. The form is called **MC 20** — "Affidavit
and Order, Suspension of Fees/Costs."

**How to get it:**
- Ask the court clerk at the filing window
- Download from courts.michigan.gov (search "MC 20")
- Ask the court's self-help center

**How it works:**
- You fill in your household income and expenses
- You sign under oath
- The judge reviews and approves or denies
- If approved, all filing fees are waived for that case

**Income guidelines** (approximate — varies by court):
- The court looks at whether paying fees would be a
  hardship based on your household size and income
- Public assistance recipients (SNAP, FIP, Medicaid,
  SSI) generally qualify automatically
- There is no strict cutoff — the judge decides

**IMPORTANT:** The fee waiver covers court filing fees
only. It does not cover process server costs (typically
$50-75). However, if you receive a fee waiver, you may
also request that the court authorize service by
certified mail instead of process server, which costs
~$8-12.

# SECTION 6: HOW TO FILE

## Step 1: Complete Your Documents

- Fill in all blanks in the complaint template (Section 4)
- Complete any regulatory complaint templates you want
  to submit (Section 3)
- Make **3 copies** of everything:
  1. Original for the court
  2. Copy for each defendant
  3. Copy for your records

## Step 2: File with the Court

**Small Claims (up to $6,500):**
- Go to: 36th District Court, XXX Madison Ave, Detroit
- Bring: Original complaint + copies + filing fee ($50)
  OR completed MC 20 fee waiver
- The clerk will stamp your copies and assign a case number
- The court will schedule a hearing (usually 30-60 days)

**Circuit Court (over $6,500):**
- Go to: Wayne County Circuit Court, 2 Woodward Ave, Detroit
- Bring: Original complaint + copies + filing fee ($175)
  OR completed MC 20 fee waiver
- The clerk will stamp your copies and assign a case number
- You will need to arrange service on each defendant

## Step 3: Serve the Defendants

After filing, each defendant must receive a copy of the
complaint. This is called "service of process."

**For Brian Banks personally:**
- A process server (not you) delivers the complaint to
  Banks at:
  - Purpose Charter Academy, 10101 E. Canfield, Detroit
    (during school hours), OR
  - 1968 Severn Road, Grosse Pointe Woods, MI 48236 (home)
- Cost: $50-75
- The process server gives you a Proof of Service form
- File the Proof of Service with the court

**For The Purpose Group, LLC:**
- Serve the registered agent: Brian Banks
- Same addresses as above
- The LLC is a separate defendant from Banks personally

**For the school (PCA or MacDowell):**
- Serve any person in charge at the school address
- PCA: 10101 E. Canfield, Detroit 48214
- MacDowell: 4201 W. Outer Drive, Detroit 48221

## Step 4: Wait for a Response

- After service, each defendant has **21 days** to file
  an answer with the court
- If they do NOT answer within 21 days, you can request
  a **default judgment** — the court may enter judgment
  in your favor without a trial
- If they DO answer, the case proceeds to discovery
  and trial

## Step 5: Go to Court

- Dress professionally
- Bring all your documents organized in a folder
- Arrive early
- When your case is called, stand, state your name,
  and explain your case calmly and clearly
- The judge will ask questions
- Bring any witnesses who can support your claims
- You are allowed to have a friend or family member
  sit with you in the courtroom for support
