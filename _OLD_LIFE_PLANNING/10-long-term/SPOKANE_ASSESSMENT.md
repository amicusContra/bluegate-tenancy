**The owner is NOT claiming this as her primary residence.** In Michigan, a PRE
of 0% means one of:

1. **Vacant** — nobody lives there
2. **Rented** — tenant occupies, but owner doesn't live there
3. **Owner moved away** — retained the property but lives elsewhere

The PRE was filed effective 02/03/2016, shortly after purchase. But at 0%,
the owner is paying FULL property taxes (no homestead exemption). This
increases carrying cost with no offset — a motivated-seller indicator if the
property is not generating rental income.
