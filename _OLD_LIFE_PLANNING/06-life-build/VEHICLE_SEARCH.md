## Pre-Purchase Checklist

Before buying ANY car, do these:

### Before You Go

- [ ] Confirm it's a **4-door sedan** (not coupe, not hatch unless 4-door)
- [ ] Confirm **manual transmission** (not automated manual / DCT / CVT)
- [ ] Run the VIN through NICB VINCheck (nicb.org) — free stolen vehicle check
- [ ] Check for recalls at nhtsa.gov/recalls
- [ ] Look up the VIN on carfax.com or vehiclehistory.com (free basic report)

### At the Car

- [ ] Cold start — listen for knocks, rattles, rough idle
- [ ] Check oil — look at dipstick (milky = head gasket, black = overdue change)
- [ ] Check coolant — should be green/orange, not brown or oily
- [ ] Clutch feel — should engage smoothly, not slip at high RPM, not shudder
- [ ] Test all gears — 1st through 5th/6th and reverse, no grinding
- [ ] Drive at highway speed — any vibration, pulling, noise?
- [ ] Brakes — no pulsing, grinding, or pulling
- [ ] AC — does it blow cold? (Uber requirement)
- [ ] All 4 windows go up and down
- [ ] Check tires — even wear, tread depth, matching set
- [ ] Check underneath — rust on frame, subframe, control arms (Michigan cars!)
- [ ] Check for body damage, mismatched paint (accident history)

### After You Like It

- [ ] **Pre-purchase inspection** at an independent mechanic (~$100). Non-negotiable.
  Tell the seller: "I'd like to have my mechanic look at it before we finalize."
  Any honest seller will agree. A seller who refuses is a seller to walk from.
- [ ] Negotiate based on inspection findings
- [ ] Get a signed bill of sale with VIN, price, date, both party signatures
- [ ] Get the title — must be clean (no liens, no salvage)
- [ ] Secretary of State for title transfer + registration + plates
