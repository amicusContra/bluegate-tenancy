## FREE MEALS — Both Districts

Michigan funds universal free school meals statewide ($200 million
in the 2026-27 budget). Both Lansing and Detroit schools serve free
breakfast and lunch to all students regardless of income.

Lansing School District also participates in the federal Community
Eligibility Provision (CEP), which means:

- **No forms to fill out** — every student eats free
- **No stigma** — nobody pays, nobody is singled out
- **Breakfast in the classroom** (K-8 buildings)
