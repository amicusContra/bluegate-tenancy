### SF-180 Notes
- **Online**: eVetRecs at [archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records](https://www.archives.gov/veterans/military-service-records)
- **Paper**: Download SF-180, fill out, mail to NPRC
- **Turnaround**: 2–12 weeks (longer for complete files)
- **Cost**: Free for veterans requesting their own records
- Request EVERYTHING: personnel records, medical records, performance evaluations,
  disciplinary records. You want the full picture for the discharge upgrade.
