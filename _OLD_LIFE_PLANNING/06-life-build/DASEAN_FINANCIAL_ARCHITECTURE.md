## PHASE 2 — DEANNA'S SETTLEMENT MANAGEMENT (Oct-Dec 2026)

### The Section 8 Problem

Deanna is on Section 8 housing assistance. Section 8 has:
- **Income limits** — gross income must stay below threshold
- **Asset reporting** — lump sum payments (like settlements) must be reported
- **Rent calculation** — 30% of adjusted gross income

A $25,000-$50,000 settlement check in Deanna's bank account
creates a Section 8 problem. The housing authority may:
- Recalculate her rent contribution upward
- Require her to report the lump sum as income
- In worst case, question her continued eligibility

### The Solution: Spend-Down + DaSean as Financial Buffer

**Option A — Rapid spend-down (simplest)**

Deanna receives settlement. She immediately uses it for exempt
purposes that don't count as ongoing assets:

| Spend-down item | Amount | Section 8 impact |
|-----------------|--------|-----------------|
| Pay off any personal debts | Varies | Reduces liabilities, not counted as asset |
| Prepay funeral/burial plan | $5,000-8,000 | Exempt asset under most housing authority rules |
| Medical expenses | Varies | Exempt |
| Car purchase (reliable used vehicle) | $8,000-12,000 | Vehicle is typically exempt asset |
| Household necessities (furniture, appliances) | $2,000-3,000 | Not counted once purchased |
| Children's UTMA contributions (via DaSean) | $5,000-10,000 | Funds are the CHILDREN's, not Deanna's |
| Give to DaSean for housing down payment fund | Remainder | Gift, not Deanna's asset once given |

**Option B — DaSean as custodian/manager**

DaSean manages settlement funds on Deanna's behalf through
his own accounts. Deanna authorizes DaSean to receive and
manage the settlement proceeds. The money flows:

```
Settlement → DaSean's account → Deanna's bills/needs
                              → Housing fund (DaSean's name)
                              → Children's UTMA
                              → Emergency fund (DaSean's name, for Deanna's use)
```

Deanna's bank account stays clean. Section 8 sees no lump sum.
DaSean handles disbursements as needed.

**CAUTION:** This must be done transparently. If the housing
authority asks, Deanna says: "My son manages my finances. The
settlement was for childcare I provided. My son is holding the
funds and paying my expenses." This is legal. It is not fraud.
It is a family financial arrangement — the same kind of informal
arrangement the Smiths exploited for nine years, except this one
protects the vulnerable person instead of exploiting her.
