**Cross-references**:

**Author**: Kevin Mok — third-party witness and neutral intercessor. This
journal is maintained by Kevin with AI-assisted summarization and
documentation. Kevin provides photos, descriptions of events, and
firsthand observations. The AI organizes, catalogs, and structures
the record. This produces a cleaner evidentiary document than either
DaSean or Kevin writing a traditional journal, because:
1. Kevin is a third-party witness with no legal stake — not the parent.
2. Events are recorded contemporaneously with timestamped commits.
3. Photos are cataloged with AI-generated descriptions that can be
   independently verified against the images.
4. The journaling is not a decision DaSean makes — Kevin takes ownership
   of it so that DaSean's only responsibilities are: be a father, and
   make the hard decisions. Everything else is carried for him.

**Guiding Principle**: Record facts — times, locations, activities, who was
present. No editorializing, no accusations, no emotional language. Let the
facts speak.
