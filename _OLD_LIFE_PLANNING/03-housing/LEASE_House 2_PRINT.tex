\section{2. PREMISES}

Landlord leases to Tenant the residential property located at:

\begin{quote}

A \textbf{one-bedroom, one-bathroom} single-family dwelling, including

\textbf{Included furnishings and appliances} (Landlord-provided, remain

move-in and documented on the Move-In Condition Checklist (Section~12).

\section{3. TERM}

This Lease begins on \textbf{June 22, 2026} (``Move-In Date'') with an
initial term of \textbf{ninety (90) days}, expiring
\textbf{September 19, 2026}. At the end of each term, the Lease renews
automatically for an additional \textbf{ninety (90) day} term under the
same conditions unless either party provides thirty (30) days' written
notice of intent not to renew before the expiration of the current term
(MCL 554.134). Either party may request conversion to a twelve (12) month
fixed-term lease at any renewal point by mutual written agreement.

\section{4. RENT --- PHASED SCHEDULE}

Rent is structured in three phases to support Tenant's transition to
independent housing:

\subsection{Phase 1 --- Establishment (Months 1--3)}

\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
Item & Amount \\
\midrule
Base Rent & \$0.00 \\
Utilities & Paid by Landlord \\
\textbf{Tenant Owes} & \textbf{\$0.00/month} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip
Purpose: Assist Tenant's transition into independent housing and establish
residential occupancy. Tenant is responsible for maintaining the property
in good condition.

\subsection{Phase 2 --- Utilities Transfer (Months 4--6)}

\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
Item & Amount \\
\midrule
Base Rent & \$0.00 \\
Utilities & Paid by Tenant \\
\textbf{Tenant Owes} & \textbf{Utilities only} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip
Utilities include: electric, gas, water/sewer, trash. Tenant transfers
utility accounts into Tenant's name no later than the first day of
Month~4. Landlord will assist with account transfers.

\subsection{Phase 3 --- Full Tenancy (Month 7 onward)}

\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
Item & Amount \\
\midrule
Base Rent & \$\rule{2cm}{0.4pt}/month \\
Utilities & Paid by Tenant \\
\textbf{Tenant Owes} & \textbf{Rent + Utilities} \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}

\medskip
Rent amount for Phase~3 will be agreed upon in writing no later than
thirty (30) days before Phase~3 begins. Rent will be set at or below
fair market value for the area, reflecting the relationship between the
parties and the Landlord's intent to provide affordable housing.

\textbf{Fair market reference}: HUD FY~2026 Fair Market Rent for
ZIP~48910 is \$990/month (1-bedroom) and \$1,240/month (2-bedroom).
The projected Phase~3 rent of approximately \$700/month reflects
below-market pricing consistent with the furnished unit, the
relationship between the parties, and the Landlord's intent to provide
affordable transitional housing.

\textbf{Rent is due on the 1st of each month.} A five (5) day grace
period applies.

\section{5. SECURITY DEPOSIT}

Security deposit: \textbf{\$0.00} (waived during Phase~1).

If a deposit is required at Phase~3, it will not exceed 1.5 months'
rent per Michigan law (MCL 554.602) and will be held in accordance with
the Michigan Security Deposit Act (MCL 554.601--554.616).

\section{6. USE OF PREMISES}

The Premises shall be used exclusively as a \textbf{private residential
dwelling} for Tenant and Tenant's minor children during Tenant's lawful
parenting time.

Tenant shall not use the Premises for any unlawful purpose. Tenant
shall comply with all applicable Lansing ordinances, including
Chapter~1460 (Property Maintenance and Housing).

\section{7. OCCUPANTS}

Authorized occupants:

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item DaSean Mitchell (Tenant)
\item Sahana Mitchell (child, age 9)
\item Allanna Mitchell (child, age 7)

No other persons shall reside at the Premises without prior written
consent of Landlord.

Tenant may receive mail, school correspondence, medical correspondence,
and governmental communications at the Premises.

\section{8. LANDLORD OBLIGATIONS}

Landlord shall:

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Maintain the property in compliance with Lansing housing codes
\item Ensure working smoke detectors and CO detectors at move-in
\item Make necessary structural and systems repairs (roof, plumbing,
  electrical, HVAC) in a timely manner
\item Provide twenty-four (24) hours' notice before entry except in
  emergencies (MCL 554.601c)
\item Register the property with the City of Lansing as required
  (Lansing Code Chapter~1460, \S1460.02)
\end{itemize}

\section{9. TENANT OBLIGATIONS}

Tenant shall:

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item Keep the Premises clean, sanitary, and in good condition
\item Dispose of trash and recycling properly
\item Not damage or permit damage to the property beyond normal wear
\item Promptly notify Landlord of any needed repairs
\item Not make alterations without written consent
\item Maintain renter's insurance (recommended, not required in Phase~1)
\item Allow Landlord access for repairs with reasonable notice
\item Not assign or sublet without written consent
\end{itemize}

\section{10. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS}

\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item \textbf{Landlord} is responsible for: structural, roof, plumbing,
  electrical, HVAC, exterior, and code compliance.
\item \textbf{Tenant} is responsible for: routine cleaning, lawn care
  (mowing, snow removal from walks), lightbulb replacement, and minor
  upkeep.
\item Tenant shall report maintenance issues in writing (text message
  is acceptable).
\end{itemize}
