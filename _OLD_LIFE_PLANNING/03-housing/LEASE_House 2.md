## 4. RENT — PHASED SCHEDULE

Rent is structured in three phases to support Tenant's transition to independent
housing:

### Phase 1 — Establishment (Months 1–3)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Base Rent | $0.00 |
| Utilities | Paid by Landlord |
| **Tenant Owes** | **$0.00/month** |

Purpose: Assist Tenant's transition into independent housing and establish
residential occupancy. Tenant is responsible for maintaining the property
in good condition.

### Phase 2 — Utilities Transfer (Months 4–6)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Base Rent | $0.00 |
| Utilities | Paid by Tenant |
| **Tenant Owes** | **Utilities only** |

Utilities include: electric, gas, water/sewer, trash. Tenant transfers utility
accounts into Tenant's name no later than the first day of Month 4. Landlord
will assist with account transfers.

### Phase 3 — Full Tenancy (Month 7 onward)

| Item | Amount |
|------|--------|
| Base Rent | $______/month |
| Utilities | Paid by Tenant |
| **Tenant Owes** | **Rent + Utilities** |

Rent amount for Phase 3 will be agreed upon in writing no later than thirty (30)
days before Phase 3 begins. Rent will be set at or below fair market value for
the area, reflecting the relationship between the parties and the Landlord's
intent to provide affordable housing.

**Fair market reference**: HUD FY 2026 Fair Market Rent for ZIP 48910 is
$990/month (1-bedroom) and $1,240/month (2-bedroom). The projected Phase 3
rent of approximately $700/month reflects below-market pricing consistent with
the furnished unit, the relationship between the parties, and the Landlord's
intent to provide affordable transitional housing.

**Rent is due on the 1st of each month.** A five (5) day grace period applies.
